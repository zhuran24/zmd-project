ZMD RESEARCH ATTENTION AND REFLECTION
=====================================

Status
------
This file is the canonical runtime-attention contract for the research tree. It
is not another methodology, charter, approval gate, or research report.

Three major direction corrections occurred only after the owner stopped the
line, even though the relevant principles already existed in the methodology.
The missing object was not another reason. It was a concrete trigger telling the
agent when ordinary continuation must pause and the reasons must be brought back
into working attention.

1. The three entry surfaces have different jobs
------------------------------------------------

AGENTS.md / CLAUDE.md
  Always-on router. They establish role, read order, default attention, hard
  truth boundaries, and short pointers to runtime reflexes. They must remain
  compact. They do not carry changing campaign facts or the full methodology.

Claude Code MEMORY.md and cards
  Always-light reflex layer. It carries stable events that must interrupt work
  while a line is already running. It answers "when must I stop?" Dynamic state,
  experiment counts, and current thresholds do not belong here.

Skill index: /zmd-method and /research-charter
  Deep doctrine layer. It explains why the project works this way, how to reason
  about layers, cuts, evidence, frontier, and research economics. Load it at cold
  start and after context compression; reopen the relevant section when a pause
  exposes a real method dispute. Do not reread the whole doctrine after every
  experiment.

Division rule:
  why and distinctions live in skills;
  when-to-interrupt lives in memory;
  mode and routing live in AGENTS/CLAUDE;
  the complete operational statement lives here.

A fact has one truth source. Higher-frequency entry surfaces point to it instead
of copying it in full.

2. Attention map for the rest of the tree
-----------------------------------------

research_lab/STATE.txt
  First live read. Current endpoint, deepest positive witness, one live question,
  current reflection checkpoint, inflight recovery, and a small set of immediate
  truth guards. It is not a historical ledger.

campaigns/<id>/CAMPAIGN.txt
  Stable success object, scope, non-implications, and current campaign tension.

campaigns/<id>/RESULTS.txt
  Durable consequential conclusions with evidence identity and effect on the
  next problem. It may be long because it is queried, not read front-to-back at
  every entry.

campaigns/<id>/experiments/
  Tracked hypotheses, runners, checkers, and compact durable result records.

research_lab/local/
  Raw runs, caches, dumps, temporary models, and large receipts. Presence here
  does not make a result durable.

research_lab/PROGRAM.txt and ARCHITECTURE.txt
  Stable mission and file-flow design. Read on first entry, changed mission, or
  architecture work, not during every ordinary experiment.

research_lab/CHECKS.txt
  Validation weight selected by claimed effect. It is not the research rhythm.

3. Mandatory pause triggers
---------------------------

The pause happens before opening the next experiment or abstraction, not after a
long report has already rationalized the continuation.

P1. Same-language third step
  Before a third consecutive consequential experiment that preserves the same
  frozen skeleton/context, consumer depth, and primary success signal, when the
  previous two did not advance the deepest positive witness.

P2. Two account-only results
  After two consecutive consequential results that moved only the knowledge-
  leverage or research-machine account, before spending a third result without
  named frontier or deeper-witness movement.

P3. UNKNOWN continuation
  Immediately when the proposed response to UNKNOWN is only more time, another
  seed, solver profile, threshold, cut batch, atlas, or representation layer,
  without a new semantic variable, direct consumer, or orthogonal control.

P4. Unconsumed positive object
  Before building another abstraction above a positive geometry, witness,
  finite basis, atlas, or interface object that has not yet been sent to the
  next real consumer it was meant to serve.

P5. Interface inflation
  Before compiling another interface layer when the current cut/atlas/signature
  store is growing while cross-context reuse, transfer, or discrimination stays
  weak. A smaller state count alone is not deeper progress.

P6. Skeleton monopoly
  After three consequential experiments inherit the same skeleton or local
  basin without an orthogonal constructor/control, even if the metric or local
  representation changed between them.

P7. Stop or contradiction signal
  Immediately when a preregistered stop condition fires, an owner asks to look
  back, a new result contradicts the current narrative, or an evidence identity
  changes under the line.

P8. High-attention file change
  Before creating a new default-read file or editing AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md,
  MEMORY.md/core memory cards, a skill description/body, START.txt, the STATE
  schema, .zmd-worktree-mode, or this attention architecture.

These are event triggers, not a per-step self-review ritual. Normal research
continues at full speed until one fires.

4. The reflection pulse
-----------------------

The pulse fits on one screen and should usually take less time than starting the
next experiment. Answer only these questions:

  1. What is the deepest positive witness/consumer layer now, and what was it at
     the last checkpoint?
  2. Did the last results change the problem or consumer, or only the instrument,
     representation, evidence store, or explanation?
  3. Which of the three accounts moved: frontier, knowledge leverage, research
     machine?
  4. What assumption, skeleton, metric, or interface has silently become
     furniture rather than a tested choice?
  5. What is the cheapest direct-consumer attempt or orthogonal control now?
  6. Decision: CONTINUE, BRANCH/CONTROL, or STOP/SWITCH, with one sentence why.

Do not create an experiment number, dossier, or long reflection report merely to
perform the pulse. Do not reread both full skills unless the answers reveal a
method conflict.

Recording rule:
  unchanged CONTINUE
    Continue immediately. No durable file is required.

  changed direction, live question, stop condition, or tested assumption
    Update STATE.txt's Reflection checkpoint and Live question. Put supporting
    evidence in the campaign result/experiment that earned the change.

  architecture or entry-surface change
    Update this file and ARCHITECTURE.txt; keep high-frequency pointers short.

5. Attention effect of every durable file change
------------------------------------------------

Before creating or modifying a durable file, consider five things. This is a
mental placement check, not a form to fill out:

  audience
    Which agent/role is expected to read it?

  load frequency
    Every prompt/session, every campaign entry, only on a matching trigger, or
    only as evidence lookup?

  half-life
    Stable doctrine, stable reflex, live state, consequential result, or raw run?

  induced behavior
    What will a future agent do earlier, later, more often, or stop doing because
    this file is visible?

  identity and duplication
    Where is the one truth source, and which higher-frequency files should only
    point to it?

Placement defaults:
  role/mode/read order                 -> AGENTS/CLAUDE
  stable runtime interrupt             -> memory card + pointer here
  method/charter reasoning             -> skill
  current endpoint/question/checkpoint -> STATE
  campaign scope/success object        -> CAMPAIGN
  consequential conclusion             -> RESULTS + experiment record
  raw/reproducible runtime output       -> local
  file-flow architecture               -> ARCHITECTURE

High-frequency files must be shorter and more pointer-rich than low-frequency
evidence files. A new idea should enter at the lowest-frequency layer that still
reaches the agent at the moment it matters.

6. Anti-goal
------------

The purpose is to make methodology fire at the right moment, not to keep the
agent staring at methodology. Respecting the charter means doing research with
its distinctions present in the background, then surfacing them when an event
trigger says the current continuation may have become automatic.
