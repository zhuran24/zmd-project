ZMD RESEARCH WORKTREE PROGRAM
=============================

Status
------
This file is the durable orientation for the research worktree. It is not a
production specification, a certification claim, or permission to update U/L.
The research worktree owns the whole research program, not only the current
zero-condition campaign.

The three goal levels must never be collapsed into one another.

1. CURRENT CAMPAIGN GOAL
------------------------
The present campaign is to obtain the first zero-condition whole layout for the
current frozen instance.

"Zero-condition" removes the empty-rectangle requirement only. It does not mean
"placement-only" or "geometry-only". A successful object must still traverse
the full relevant chain: mandatory placement, power, exact port binding, exact
routing, the intended flow/throughput checks for this research stage, strict
independent validation, and eventual game-side review where required.

This campaign is the current entry point because the lower side is empty and a
whole layout is the cheapest way to expose the real construction interface. It
is a milestone and a diagnostic lens, not the mission of the research tree.
After it succeeds, the next lower-bound rung may be 6x6, followed by progressively
stronger lower bounds, but the exact next move is decided from the evidence then
available rather than frozen here as a detailed plan.

2. PROJECT FINAL TARGET
-----------------------
For the current active IndustrialPlanner problem, close the exact optimization
problem rather than merely find a plausible layout.

The intended final object is a durable, independently replayable,
certified-exact result for the active valley4_protocol_core production system:

* a complete legal layout and its full witness;
* the exact max_lex(empty-rectangle area, min-side) optimum, obtained by making
  the lower and upper ledgers meet under the same instance, rules, objective,
  and premises;
* exact placement, power, binding, routing, and strict empty-rectangle semantics;
* the project's intended throughput semantics, including discrete sustained
  production and startup reachability rather than connectivity alone;
* independent proof/replay and an honest durable publication path.

The current production theorem still has a narrower six-predicate scope. The
throughput line is therefore a separate research ledger today, but historical
owner decisions record throughput certification as a final must-do scope. The
intended final target must keep both facts visible: today's theorem must not be
silently widened, and the project must not mistake a connectivity-only close for
its eventual full game-semantic close.

For throughput, the expected final witness shape includes, as needed, a legal
bootstrap manifest, a replayable startup trace from a player-reachable initial
state, and a periodic discrete schedule that sustains the frozen production
targets. Startup duration may later become a low-priority quality objective, but
its place in the objective is not fixed here.

3. FUTURE EXPECTED TARGET
-------------------------
After the current problem is genuinely closed, the expected direction is not to
leave behind a one-instance monument. It is to turn the learned architecture
into a reusable, context-bound certified-exact solving and research system.

The expected future system should be able to:

* accept different bases, production targets, rule versions, and objective
  contexts without pretending that conclusions transfer automatically;
* derive and reuse judgments only with explicit problem/objective/context
  premises, so rules can transfer across instances or families when proved and
  are re-derived when premises change;
* use a reasoning outer loop to generate necessary conditions, constructive
  templates, reductions, bounds, and failure explanations, while exact inner
  solvers consume the residual freedom;
* reactivate the other known IndustrialPlanner bases as evidence permits,
  including larger-base deployment such as the 80x80 wuling_protocol_core path;
* study cross-base transfer of the solving architecture, rules, and proof
  machinery;
* eventually define and solve joint multi-base optimization, including
  cross-base commodity flow and a globally appropriate objective rather than
  copying the current single-base max_lex objective without proof.

This is an expected future direction, not a present certified scope or a promise
that every listed feature must be built in one step. It exists in the research
program so near-term choices do not accidentally hard-code the architecture
against the future. Conversely, future generality must not be used to burden a
cheap current experiment with abstractions it does not need.

Research principles
-------------------
1. Choices, layer boundaries, representations, and cut forms are outputs of the
   problem, not inherited furniture.
2. Every useful result must change the next problem, the next construction, or
   the next decision.
3. Computation consumes the residue left after cheap reasoning, arithmetic,
   counterexamples, and local exact checks.
4. Discovery is allowed to be provisional and adventurous. Conjectures,
   heuristics, sufficient restrictions, and temporary over-constraints must be
   labelled, not forbidden.
5. Negative results retain their exact scope. UNKNOWN is not infeasibility.
6. The research tree may create candidate mathematics and candidate machinery,
   but it never self-grants production, certification, U/L updates, or owner
   authority.
7. Promotion is a separate cold-start act: selected results cross to the
   certification tree through a compact promotion packet and are independently
   re-derived, attacked, and replayed there. The whole research branch is not the
   unit of promotion.
