ZMD RESEARCH TREE ARCHITECTURE
==============================

Purpose
-------
The research tree is the long-lived home of ZMD discovery work. It exists to
keep attention on the problem while preserving enough truth discipline that
useful results can later survive independent certification.

It is deliberately not a lighter copy of the certification process. Research
and certification have different default questions:

  research:      What structure is true or useful, and what should we try next?
  certification: Is this fixed candidate claim safe to consume with a stated
                 effect under a fixed context?

The second question has its own worktree at
/home/zhuran24/zmd-certification on certification/main. The old
main worktree is neither role; it is retained as history and source material.

1. Repository roles
-------------------

history/material tree
  Path: /home/zhuran24/zmd-pj
  Role: preserve previous Git history, old state, tracked sources, and local
        evidence that was not copied into the research worktree.
  Default access: read-only.

research tree
  Branch: research/main
  Role: all active research, including mathematics, solver design, construction,
        experiments, counterexamples, and candidate proof machinery.
  Authority: research only.

certification tree
  Path: /home/zhuran24/zmd-certification
  Branch: certification/main
  Role: cold, independent review and promotion of selected frozen research
        candidates. It does not inherit research conclusions merely by sharing
        Git ancestry.

2. Attention architecture
-------------------------
Attention is routed through three entry surfaces with deliberately different
weights. Their division is part of the research architecture, not presentation.

AGENTS.md / CLAUDE.md
  Always-on mode and routing overlay. It says what role the agent has, what to
  read first, which truth boundaries are non-negotiable, and where runtime
  reflexes live. It must remain short and must not accumulate changing facts.

Claude Code MEMORY.md and cards
  Always-light reflex layer. It carries stable events that must interrupt a line
  while it is already running. It answers when to stop, not the current campaign
  state. The external native directory and tracked seed are described in
  CC_MEMORY.txt.

Skill index: /zmd-method and /research-charter
  Deep doctrine layer. It explains why and how; it is loaded at cold start and
  after context compression, then recedes into the background. It is reopened
  when a runtime pause exposes a real method or charter dispute, not after every
  ordinary experiment.

The full routing and pause contract lives in:
  research_lab/ATTENTION_AND_REFLECTION.txt

Stable orientation:
  research_lab/PROGRAM.txt
    Goal hierarchy and research principles. Changes rarely.

Live orientation:
  research_lab/STATE.txt
    Active campaign pointer, current endpoint, deepest positive witness, one live
    question, reflection checkpoint, and immediate truth guards. It is kept short
    enough to reread every session; history belongs elsewhere.

Validation level:
  research_lab/CHECKS.txt
    R0 ordinary exploration, R1 real-solver integration, and R2 promotion
    candidate checks. The level follows the effect being claimed, not file type.

Campaign context:
  research_lab/campaigns/<id>/CAMPAIGN.txt
    Success object, scope, known facts, non-implications, current research
    tension, and links to source evidence.

Information placement follows attention frequency: high-frequency entry files are
short and pointer-rich; live facts stay in STATE; consequential conclusions stay
in RESULTS/experiment records; raw bytes stay in local. Before adding a durable
file, consider its audience, load frequency, half-life, induced behavior, and one
truth source. The complete placement rule is in ATTENTION_AND_REFLECTION.txt.

The inherited repository is a library, not the front door. Agents load old
CURRENT/PROJECT_LOCK/ledgers/history only when the current question needs exact
semantics, current authority, a historical claim, or a promotion boundary.

3. Goal hierarchy
-----------------
Every campaign is evaluated against three distinct levels:

  A. Current campaign goal
     The immediate object whose success or failure will move the project.

  B. Current-project final target
     Complete exact closure for the active base under the project's intended
     final semantics, including throughput/startup rather than connectivity
     alone.

  C. Future expected target
     A context-bound reusable exact-research system that can transfer with proof
     across instances and bases and eventually support multi-base optimization.

A current experiment does not need to implement C. It must only avoid claiming
that an accidental current-instance encoding is a universal architecture. When a
cheap special case is useful, use it and label the premise.

4. Working layers
-----------------

program layer
  PROGRAM.txt. Stable mission and principles.

state layer
  STATE.txt. Current position, reflection checkpoint, and next question, not a
  diary or cumulative result ledger.

attention/reflex layer
  ATTENTION_AND_REFLECTION.txt plus the research memory reflex. Event-triggered
  pauses and file-placement rules live here; it is not consulted after every
  ordinary step.

campaign layer
  campaigns/<id>/. Self-contained active research surface.

experiment-source layer
  campaigns/<id>/experiments/. Tracked scripts, small fixtures, hypotheses, and
  compact summaries needed to reproduce or understand a discriminating test.

local runtime layer
  local/. Ignored logs, caches, raw runs, temporary models, large receipts,
  notebooks, and mounts. Nothing here is durable merely because it exists.

role-memory layer
  CC_MEMORY.txt plus cc_memory_seed/. The native memory lives outside Git and is
  selected through ignored `.claude/settings.local.json`; the tracked seed and
  migration map preserve its intended role and provenance.

method layer
  methods/. Created only when the same reasoning or tool has survived use in at
  least two materially different contexts. Do not promote a one-off trick into a
  framework on first contact.

promotion layer
  promotion/. Frozen candidate packets for the independent certification tree. A
  packet is an interface object, not a certification receipt.

5. Research loop
----------------
The default loop is intentionally small:

  observe a concrete wall
    -> state one discriminating question
    -> choose the cheapest faithful test
    -> run or reason
    -> classify the outcome honestly
    -> change the next problem, construction, or decision

No detailed global plan is required. Pre-registration is optional and used only
when looking at the outcome could move thresholds, when a causal A/B claim is
intended, when a high-budget run needs a stop rule, or when owner instructions
explicitly require it.

The loop is interrupted only by the event triggers in
ATTENTION_AND_REFLECTION.txt. A pause is a one-screen decision pulse, not a new
experiment, dossier, or per-step audit. It exists because methodology can be
known yet remain absent from working attention at the moment continuation becomes
automatic. If the pulse leaves the direction unchanged, work resumes immediately;
if it changes the direction or live question, STATE records the checkpoint.

A result is durable only when RESULTS.txt records:

  statement
  premises/scope
  evidence identity
  status: conjecture / measured / proved / refuted / witness / unknown
  non-implications
  effect on the next problem or decision

A large log without that effect remains raw material. A beautiful method without
frontier or decision leverage remains a tool, not project progress.

6. Code placement
-----------------
Early hypotheses should usually begin as campaign-local harnesses or prototypes.
This keeps experimental vocabulary and temporary assumptions close to their
question.

Modify inherited src/ code in the research branch when the experiment genuinely
asks about integration with the real solver and a side harness would answer a
different question. Such changes are still research-only even if they touch a
file that was certification-bearing in the historical tree.

Do not preserve artificial src/ sanctity inside the research tree. The safety
boundary is packet-only promotion into certification/main, not the pathname of a
research edit. Conversely, do not interpret a successful research src/ run as a
production result.

7. Git model
------------
research/main is the research integration line, not a disposable feature branch.
Small sequential work may commit directly to it. Use research/exp/<name> branches
only for parallel agents, a risky large rewrite, or experiments that need clean
A/B ancestry.

Commit rules:
  * one coherent idea, instrument, experiment, or conclusion per commit;
  * exact pathspec staging;
  * raw local output excluded;
  * no wholesale merge from research/main into a certification branch;
  * promotion packets name exact commits or diffs to be reviewed.

The research branch may import selected historical fixes when needed, but it does
not periodically merge the old main branch merely to appear synchronized. Any
baseline refresh is an explicit research decision recorded in BASELINE.txt.

8. Evidence and external material
---------------------------------
Tracked historical documents remain in this tree because they are useful search
material. Large untracked evidence remains in the history/material tree unless a
campaign deliberately imports a compact immutable subset.

Campaign INPUTS.txt files record external path, SHA-256, size, role, and whether
that object is required or merely useful. A path alone is not identity. Missing
external evidence narrows what can be concluded but does not invite a fabricated
placeholder.

9. Promotion boundary
---------------------
Research can finish in several ways: a theorem, counterexample, witness,
algorithm, representation, negative result, or a decision-changing measurement.
Only selected results need certification.

A promotion packet contains the cold object:
  * exact claim and object space;
  * premises and context identity;
  * requested effect;
  * selected commits/diff;
  * independent reproduction commands;
  * positive controls, negative controls, and adversarial mutations;
  * known unknowns and non-implications;
  * source evidence identities.

It deliberately omits persuasive narrative, sunk-cost history, and the research
session's preference for the candidate. The future certification agent should
meet the result after it has cooled.

10. Legacy governance boundary
------------------------------
The inherited document governance and production preflight tools describe the
former combined tree. They remain useful when a research task intentionally edits
one of their governed legacy surfaces, but they are not the health authority for
research/main. In particular, a legacy checker may reject this branch's tracked
research README or branch-specific agent overlays because its old model requires
one shared front door and untracked overlays.

Research-tree health is checked by research_lab/tools/research_tree.py and the
claim-proportional levels in CHECKS.txt. Do not mutate the old governance system
solely to make an unrelated research experiment pass.

11. Architecture change rule
----------------------------
Every durable file change has an attention effect. Before adding a new default
read or editing a high-frequency index, identify who reads it, how often, how
long the information stays true, and what behavior it will induce. Put the full
content at the lowest-frequency layer that still reaches the agent when needed;
higher-frequency layers should point rather than duplicate. The canonical
placement check is in ATTENTION_AND_REFLECTION.txt.

This architecture should not grow a new rule for every incident. Change it only
when one of these is true:

  * a recurring research action has no natural home;
  * an agent repeatedly enters with the wrong attention mode;
  * durable results are being lost or raw outputs mistaken for conclusions;
  * promotion cannot identify a clean candidate object;
  * the architecture itself measurably delays ordinary research.

When that happens, repair the smallest layer responsible. Do not answer research
friction by cloning the old governance machine into research_lab.
