Baseline refresh rule
---------------------
The research tree does not automatically merge main. A baseline refresh is made
only when a concrete research question needs a later historical fix or input.
Record:

  source commit
  selected paths or commits
  reason the refresh changes the research problem
  conflicts or assumptions introduced

Then commit the import as its own coherent change. Do not hide a baseline refresh
inside an unrelated experiment commit.
