The outer loop was born as forward rule growth, then became a recursive closure,
and only then became a solver-level outer structure. A failure compiler is one
later producer inside this loop, not the loop's defining ontology.

This is the recursive core: rules combine into constraints; constraints shrink
or reshape the current space; facts that were previously expensive or obscure
become cheap enough to calculate; the new facts re-enter the rule system as the
next round's inputs. A static theorem library, a one-shot presolve, or a store
that records results without changing the next problem does not close this loop.

shrink as `F_0⊇F_1⊇F_2⊇…⊇F`. This correspondence depends on every added
statement being valid for the intended problem; it is a mathematical rendering
of the owner's loop, not an additional owner quotation.

Reliability asks whether every produced rule is true under its recorded
premises. Production completeness asks whether the useful consequences in a
declared language and obligation scope are actually being produced. A sound
checker answers only the first question. It does not justify waiting for a
proposer to guess useful rules, nor does a bounded small-model search establish
open-world saturation.

Truth, scope, and permission to affect a search space are distinct obligations.
A theorem must carry the contract for how it may be lowered or used. In
particular, a WLOG transform, a local lemma, a necessary condition, a
point-candidate UNSAT result, and a sufficient construction rule do not license
the same consumer action.

4. Anti-drift checklist from the corrected 2026-08 handoff
-----------------------------------------------------------
This checklist is the 2026-08 project-handoff correction conclusion. It tests
direction and causal effect, not whether later platform vocabulary is present.

1. "是不是先选了一条漂亮路线，再给它补理由？"
   If yes, the route violates the inner-loop birth intuition.

2. "外环这一轮有没有真正生成新规则？"
   A richer platform, checker, or report is not outer-loop progress by itself.

3. "新规则有没有重新成为下一轮的输入？"
   A stored result that does not change the successor problem leaves the loop
   open.

4. "知识来源是不是被窄化成了失败样本？"
   Keep forward rule composition, arithmetic, equality structure,
   construction, and failure inference in view together.

5. "外环是不是只会排除，不会构造？"
   In a campaign with no lower-side witness, exclusion-only drift is especially
   damaging.

6. "方法研究有没有反过来推迟既定前沿？"
   Method research must ride on and help the whole-layout campaign, not postpone
   it behind another validation program.

7. "后来的技术修补有没有冒充你的原始直觉？"
   Preserve source layers: later judgments, failure-compilation hypotheses,
   organs, and validation ladders may repair or test the loop, but are not the
   loop's birth statement.
