Rule text (canonical, verbatim from the drafting thread)
--------------------------------------------------------
稳定身份运输规则

凡结论需要跨几何、跨上下文、跨模型重建、跨进程或跨表示层消费，承重对象必须使用
与命题对象空间一致、且能在目标上下文中重新定位和验证的稳定见证。

物理几何结论使用坐标、格集、occupied-cell digest、terminal-cell witness 等物理
见证；语义赋值结论使用 canonical slot ID、稳定 body/owner identity、group-pose
key、operation/mode provenance 等语义见证。一个看似稳定的名字，只有在命题所处
对象层中保持不变时才可承担身份。

component 编号、destination 排序号、数组下标、solver variable ID、枚举顺序、
显示字符串及其他重建编号，只允许作为同一冻结上下文内的局部句柄。它们不得直接
进入跨上下文定理、cut、缓存键、实验续接或 consumer 输入。

跨边界时，producer 必须保存稳定见证与源 context identity；consumer 必须在目标
context 中重新映射，并验证映射存在、唯一、对象类型一致、承重属性一致。零匹配、
多匹配或属性漂移一律 fail closed，不得按旧编号继续运行。

例外：若 producer 与 consumer 明确运行于同一份冻结字节上下文，且结果不离开该
上下文，局部编号可以使用；但必须携带该 context 的身份，且不得被推广为跨几何或
跨上下文结论。

Scope note
----------
The rule does not claim coordinates always beat names. The principle is that
identity must live at the same object layer as the proposition, and must be
re-proved to point at the same object after crossing any boundary. Local array
indices and within-solve component numbering remain legal inside their own
frozen context.

Worked positive/negative anchors
--------------------------------
- Positive: the E074-line correction that replaced component numbers with
  stable physical witnesses (concrete slot coordinates, concrete free-cell
  sets) before re-running, and the exact masters' canonical sorted-ID-to-
  sorted-pose recovery mapping validated during the K=47 recheck

- Negative: every one of the five recurrences named above; and the stale E069
