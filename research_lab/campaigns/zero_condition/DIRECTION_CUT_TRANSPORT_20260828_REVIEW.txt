Overall verdict
---------------
The direction is NOT refuted and remains worth pursuing. The cut-necessity
theorem as written in the direction file (witness-conditional form) was
independently re-derived before reading its proof skeleton and CONFIRMED step
for step, including the aggregated-across-commodities capacity form, which is
the correct form under canonical_rules A7/mixed_commodity_flow. Three
supporting claims are refuted; several others need qualification. Two
corrections are load-bearing.

Load-bearing corrections
------------------------
A. r(t) is a polytope, not a number. canonical_rules
   semantics.rate_lemma_scope marks the per-machine duty split an explicit
   ASSUMPTION half and enumerates legal alternative allocations meeting the
   same demand. The Stage-4 prerequisite "exact terminal rates" is therefore
   not well-posed as phrased. Repair: r ranges over the duty polytope
   { sum_{m carries o} u_m = R_o, 0 <= u_m <= 1 }, and the layout-level
   necessary condition is existential — EXISTS r, EXISTS binding, FORALL S:
   sum_c [b_c(S; r)]+ <= cap+(S) — an LP oracle over the allocation polytope,
   not an exact-rate verifier.
B. The geometric transport core is not a matching. Layout reconfiguration is
   set-packing in a conflict graph (the implemented E077 probe is literally
   per-cell Sum <= 1 disjointness); symmetric differences of independent sets
   do not decompose into alternating paths, and intermediates need not be
   feasible. The matching form gives at best a LOWER BOUND on moved bodies.
   Only the E074 semantic (assignment) side is a legitimate b-matching shape,
   and there it is a retroactive framing, correctly labelled candidate.

Refuted supporting claims
-------------------------
1. Forced/exact terminal rates (see Correction A).
2. Geometric transport-core equality (see Correction B).
3. "Shortest vacancy chain = 6 bodies": unverifiable. The number 6 survives
   only as a hard-coded START_MOVED constant in a follow-up script; the only
   persisted E077 output implies 8 moved bodies; and the probe's model scope
   excludes port domains, modes, binding, and connectivity, so even a computed
   optimum would not be a minimum of the real problem. Drop the number or
   re-run with persisted output and an explicit scope statement.
4. Membrane as an E075 result: E075 exhausted 12 ordered placements and its 10
   STRUCTURAL_EMPTY verdicts come from empty option domains, not a separator
   argument. The membrane is a definition written afterwards — valid as the
   zero-capacity special case of the cut form, but E075 must not be cited as
   its proof in any promotion packet.
5. Novelty of the theorem: the same cut object is already implemented three
   times in src/ (separator_capacity_hull SAC-Hull, aggregated capacity;
   F2 cutset with a Dinic oracle and a Lean proof of the pigeonhole core;
   F4 component_reach as the zero-capacity case) and the multicommodity LP
   with shared edge capacity exists in flow_subproblem. The genuine
   contribution is narrower: b_c(S) as a net RATE injection — a proposed
   strengthening of the per-commodity coefficient from 1 to a rate-derived
   quantity in an existing, already-formalized constraint. It inherits the
   documented enclosure obligation (cutset.py) and the unbound-generic-slot
   obligation (separator_capacity_hull.py) unchanged.

Standing qualifications for every downstream consumer
-----------------------------------------------------
- Keep the witness-conditional phrasing in every summary. The layout-level
  "FORALL S" rendering inverts quantifiers over rate allocation, binding, and
  routing; the specs/10 10.9 over-cut trap applies, and frozen rate
  allocation is a new fourth constant class to add to the constant-support
  obligation list. On the zero-condition line an over-cut in the candidate
  generator would silently corrupt exhaustive-negative claims (E063, E068).
- specs/10 10.10 (CUT-R9-H1) binds Stage 1, not only Stage 4: a separator
  model is not automatically a relaxation of production routing; separator
  INFEASIBLE is telemetry / core shrinking only, never a master-cut proof
  source on its own.
- Anything rate- or capacity-valued derives from canonical_rules
  (rate_lemma_scope, A7_rates, mixed_commodity_flow), never from specs/09,
  which is stale on exactly these points (3D bridge wording voided by
  routing_cross_junction; per-commodity single-belt rule contradicting
  mixed_commodity_flow).
- Rename "membrane": the term is already taken by
  CLAIM-ORDINARY-MEMBRANE-TERMINAL-BOUND-S48 (geometric contact-length bound),
  a different object.
- The membrane repair disjunction is exhaustive only if it quantifies over ALL
  minimal blocking sets (they are not unique and need not intersect);
  compiling a single membrane into "must move a body of M" is an over-cut.
- Empty-rectangle migration is monotone in one direction only: zero-condition
  infeasibility certificates remain valid for every H (this supports the
  direction), but H is an optimized min-side-6 rectangle, so cut certificates
  must carry their H as a premise, and rectangle placement gets no guidance
  from zero-condition cuts.
- The quotient collision test is not yet runnable: the one genuinely new
  signature ingredient (region/trunk/bay decomposition with trunk capacities)
  is exactly the part left undefined. The test can only refute a basis, never
  confirm one; keep the held-out wording. The skeleton decoder test is
  confounded with constructor weakness as specified.

Gains produced by the review (candidates, not adopted changes)
--------------------------------------------------------------
- Stronger Boolean corollary without any rate premise: n_o = ceil(R_o) forces
  u_min >= frac(R_o) > 0 for fractional R_o (u = 1 throughout for integral),
  so "an isolated free component containing a source of c must contain a sink
  of c" holds for EVERY legal allocation. Strictly stronger than the
  direction's own Boolean corollary; should replace it.
- A conditional lane-separation bound already exists in rate_lemma_scope: under
  the stated allocation profile no two intermediate commodities can share one
  lane. The direction's pessimism about corridor lower bounds is overstated.
- Candidate capacity tightening: the shipped constant 2 per free wall cell is a
  factor-2 overestimate for axis-aligned walls (the perpendicularity constraint
  in routing forbids two same-axis channels on one cell; any non-straight
  component blocks the second layer). Per directed grid edge: at most one
  channel, always. This incidentally resolves the open reconciliation recorded
  in docs/research/p1_3_m4_recon_20260708/f4f2.md in the safe direction.
  Tightening a shipped constraint is a soundness-direction edit and owes its
  own PCR-R5-style proof before application.
