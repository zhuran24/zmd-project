CAMPAIGN: ZERO-CONDITION WHOLE LAYOUT
=====================================

Identity
--------
id: zero_condition
status: active
opened_for: first complete whole-layout witness on the current frozen instance
relation_to_program: current campaign, not project final target

Success object
--------------
A successful zero-condition result is one complete layout after removing only the
empty-rectangle requirement.

It is not satisfied by:
  * a placement-only incumbent;
  * a placement-plus-power master solution;
  * one FEASIBLE binding selection;
  * a routing precheck that merely avoids front-blocked;
  * an unverified external blueprint.

The intended campaign chain is:

  all mandatory facilities placed
    -> power coverage
    -> exact binding
    -> exact routing connectivity
    -> the intended research-stage flow/throughput checks
    -> strict independent validation
    -> game-side review when required

A stage may use research-only instrumentation or a narrower diagnostic model, but
only the complete chain closes the campaign.

Why this campaign is first
--------------------------
The lower side is empty. Before asking for a 6x6 or larger strict-empty rectangle,
the project needs to prove it can construct and validate any complete layout under
the remaining rules. The zero-condition object is therefore both the cheapest
constructive milestone and the clearest microscope for the real interface among
placement, power, binding, and routing.

Current evidence
----------------
1. The no-ghost placement-plus-power master has an OPTIMAL solution containing
   all 266 mandatory instances. This is an intermediate, not a whole layout.

2. Its first binding is FEASIBLE, but exact routing precheck reports front_blocked
   with 70 blocked ports. The strict validator is not reached. This only rejects
   that binding selection under the fixed layout.

3. The fixed geometry contains 1100 free cells split into 98 components; the
   largest component has 405 cells. A binding model that compiles front clearance
   and component support is INFEASIBLE before exact routing.

4. Commodity assumption minimization reduces that component-support failure to
   the single commodity blue_iron_block.

5. An independently checked four-pose pattern forms a closed three-cell pocket
   containing the mandatory blue_iron_block source front and no possible sink.
   The resulting pose-conjunction nogood is sound in the frozen context and has
   no production or ledger effect.

6. Older fixed-layout campaigns independently show that full-selection point
   nogoods repeatedly change the binding assignment without changing the local
   family of front-blocked death. They justify re-deriving the interface, not
   assuming the existing seam is adequate.

Research conclusion already earned
----------------------------------
The old seam

  placement + power -> complete binding -> routing precheck

is not treated as a neutral default. At least three classes of information should
be reconsidered before exact routing:

  static port-pattern viability
  free-space component support for selected commodity terminals
  placement-level obstacle causes when all compatible selections are impossible

This does not yet determine the final number of layers or the final cut families.
It determines that those are open research outputs.

Live question
-------------
What is the smallest faithful repartition that prevents the master/binding side
from producing geometries and selections already dead by cheap interface
reasoning, while leaving true simultaneous path conflicts to exact routing?

Candidate explanations to distinguish
-------------------------------------
local pocket regime
  A small obstacle set forces a required terminal into a sink-free component.
  Appropriate output may be a compact placement-level nogood or a parametric
  pocket rule.

global fragmentation regime
  No small core dominates; the placement creates many disconnected free-space
  islands. Appropriate output may be a positive permeable-space or variable
  backbone representation rather than many local cuts.

component-support regime
  Terminals exist but no assignment can place each required commodity source and
  sink in a compatible component. Appropriate output belongs with binding, not
  exact path routing.

simultaneous-routing regime
  Component support passes, but paths cannot coexist because of shared cells,
  directions, crossings, or corridor capacity. Only then does exact-routing cut
  design become the current wall.

Working constraints
-------------------
* Do not assume a fixed shelf, frame, corridor, or factory topology merely because
  it is easy to describe.
* Do not encode the entire routing model into the placement master without a new
  problem-derived reason.
* Do not let another interface-compressibility study postpone the whole-layout
  target; measurements should ride on the witness campaign.
* Do not infer zero-condition infeasibility from the death of A1 or any finite
  portfolio unless the quantified space is exactly exhausted.
* Do not treat the current four-pose cut as a general family until its premises
  and transport rule are generalized or independently regenerated per layout.

Relation to later goals
-----------------------
Near term, success unlocks the first strict-empty lower-bound rung. Project-wide,
the campaign should reveal a construction interface that can participate in full
exact closure and later throughput semantics. Future-facing designs should carry
premises cleanly enough to transfer or be re-derived on other bases, but current
experiments need not build a generic multi-base system in advance.
