CAMPAIGN: ZERO-CONDITION WHOLE LAYOUT
=====================================

Reading contract
----------------
This is the stable campaign contract. Read it when entering this campaign for the
first time, when its success object or scope is disputed, when a result may have
changed that scope, or before claiming campaign close.

It is not current state. The current endpoint, deepest positive witness,
reflection checkpoint, and one live question exist only in:

  research_lab/STATE.txt

Durable consequential evidence is queried in RESULTS.txt by result/experiment ID;
external evidence identity is queried in INPUTS.txt. Journals are reasoning
archaeology, not current authority.

Identity
--------
id: zero_condition
status: active
opened_for: first complete whole-layout witness on the current frozen instance
relation_to_program: current campaign, not project final target

Success object
--------------
A successful zero-condition result is one complete layout after removing only the
empty-rectangle requirement.

It is not satisfied by:
  * a placement-only incumbent;
  * a placement-plus-power master solution;
  * one FEASIBLE binding selection;
  * a native-front or component-support relaxation witness;
  * a routing precheck that merely avoids front-blocked;
  * an unverified external blueprint.

The intended campaign chain is:

  all mandatory facilities placed
    -> power coverage
    -> exact binding
    -> exact routing connectivity
    -> the intended research-stage flow/throughput checks
    -> strict independent validation
    -> game-side review when required

A stage may use research-only instrumentation, sufficient restrictions, partial
witnesses, or narrower diagnostic models. Those objects can move the campaign,
but only the complete chain closes it.

Why this campaign is first
--------------------------
The lower side is empty. Before asking for a 6x6 or larger strict-empty rectangle,
the project needs to prove it can construct and validate any complete layout under
the remaining rules. The zero-condition object is therefore both the cheapest
constructive milestone and the clearest microscope for the real interface among
placement, power, binding, routing, and later throughput semantics.

Standing research tension
-------------------------
The inherited seam

  placement + power -> complete binding -> routing precheck

is not treated as a neutral default. Cheap information may belong before exact
routing, including:

  static port-pattern viability
  free-space component support for selected commodity terminals
  placement-level obstacle causes when all compatible selections are impossible

But the campaign does not predeclare the final number of layers, a permanent
factory topology, or a fixed cut catalogue. Layer boundaries, representations,
constructive modules, and transport objects are research outputs.

The standing question is therefore not a single current experiment. It is:

> What is the smallest faithful architecture that prevents upstream construction
> from repeatedly producing objects already dead by cheap interface reasoning,
> while leaving genuinely simultaneous path conflicts to exact routing and still
> producing a complete witness?

Current experiments answer bounded pieces of that question. Their active order is
owned by STATE, not by this file.

Truth boundary
--------------
* A result in a frozen layout, binding, skeleton, partition, or sufficient
  language keeps exactly that scope.
* UNKNOWN is not infeasibility. A failed constructor does not prove no layout
  exists.
* A necessary condition is not a sufficient construction; a positive relaxation
  witness is not a whole-layout witness.
* Local component/destination/index handles do not transport across rebuilt
  contexts without stable-witness remapping.
* Research results do not update production, certification, U/L, publication, or
  owner authority.

Working constraints
-------------------
* Do not assume a fixed shelf, frame, corridor, bay, seam, or factory topology
  merely because it is easy to describe or has survived several experiments.
* Do not encode the entire routing model into the placement master without a new
  problem-derived reason.
* Do not let another interface-compressibility or research-machine study postpone
  the whole-layout target. Positive objects should be sent to their next real
  consumer before another abstraction is built above them.
* Do not infer zero-condition infeasibility from the death of one layout, one
  skeleton, one sufficient language, or any finite portfolio unless that exact
  quantified space is exhausted.
* When the runtime pause triggers fire, use
  research_lab/ATTENTION_AND_REFLECTION.txt rather than rereading this campaign
  contract as a substitute for reflection.

Relation to later goals
-----------------------
Near term, success unlocks the first strict-empty lower-bound rung. Project-wide,
the campaign should reveal a construction interface that can participate in full
exact closure and later throughput semantics. Future-facing designs should carry
premises cleanly enough to transfer or be re-derived on other bases, but current
experiments need not build a generic multi-base system in advance.

Campaign close
--------------
Close only when a complete zero-condition witness and its validation chain are
present. On close, record:

  witness identity
  exact stages passed
  remaining semantic exclusions
  methods/results that transfer to the next campaign
  methods/results that were special to zero-condition
  next campaign selected from the evidence then available
