2. Duty-allocation and typed-circulation model
----------------------------------------------
2.1 Duty polytope

For every recipe operation o, let:

  M_o       mandatory machines carrying operation o;
  R_o       frozen aggregate equivalent-full-speed machine-run demand;
  n_o       |M_o| = ceil(R_o), as recorded by the fact half of
            canonical semantics.rate_lemma_scope;
  u_m       utilization of machine m in M_o.

The legal duty variables belong to the polytope:

  U_o = {
          u in [0,1]^(M_o) : sum_{m in M_o} u_m = R_o
        }.

The global duty polytope is the product:

  U = product_o U_o,

with any additional selected-route throughput premises stated explicitly.

Equal inter-machine duty allocation,

  u_m = R_o / n_o,

is one conditional profile in canonical rate_lemma_scope, not a frozen layout
fact. The canonical weaker-precondition note records legal unequal examples:

  [1, 9/10 x5]      for demand 11/2 over 6 machines;
  [1, 19/20 x10]    for demand 21/2 over 11 machines;
  [1, 7/8, 7/8]     for demand 11/4 over 3 machines.

Therefore no direction file, summary, or solver cut may freeze one per-machine
rate allocation without carrying it as an explicit premise.

2.2 Binding and terminal-rate polytope

Let BIND(L) be the set of binding selections admitted by the exact binding
semantics for layout L. A binding beta determines which physical terminal slot
carries each concrete commodity obligation and which commodity is assigned to
each generic slot.

For a fixed beta and duty vector u in U, let R(L,beta,u) be the set of terminal
rate vectors r consistent with:

  * canonical recipe input and output coefficients;
  * the selected machine duties u;
  * the selected concrete port slots;
  * the actual commodity-to-generic-slot assignment;
  * any admitted terminal split or aggregation semantics;
  * canonical A7 aggregate lane capacity.

Thus r is a variable in a polytope or affine image of a polytope, not a number
waiting to be read from the layout.

An unbound generic slot is not a source or sink of a named commodity. Commodity
pressure may be attributed to a generic provider only after a concrete
commodity-to-slot binding is selected. This is the standing generic-slot
obligation inherited from separator_capacity_hull.py.

2.3 Selected-route network

For fixed L, beta, u, r, and a selected physical route realization, let:

  N = (V,E)

be a verifier-equivalent directed expansion of the physical material network.
Every selected terminal t attaches to a stable vertex v(t), has commodity c(t),
sign sigma(t) in {+1,-1}, and rate r(t) >= 0:

  sigma(t) = +1 for a source/output terminal;
  sigma(t) = -1 for a sink/input terminal.

Generic boundary/protocol sources and final generic sinks are explicit terminal
vertices after their binding is fixed.

For every commodity c and directed edge e, let:

  f[c,e] >= 0    commodity flow;
  q[e]   >= 0    shared physical channel capacity.

The witness satisfies:

  sum_{e leaves v} f[c,e] - sum_{e enters v} f[c,e] = b[c,v],

where:

  b[c,v] = sum_{t: v(t)=v and c(t)=c} sigma(t) * r(t),

and:

  sum_c f[c,e] <= q[e].

Capacity is aggregated across commodities, following canonical A7 and
mixed_commodity_flow. If the physical representation is undirected, use the
verifier's directed expansion and its actual shared-capacity contract.

For S subseteq V, define:

  b[c,S] = sum_{v in S} b[c,v];

  delta+(S) = directed edges leaving S;
  delta-(S) = directed edges entering S;

  cap+(S) = sum_{e in delta+(S)} q[e];
  cap-(S) = sum_{e in delta-(S)} q[e];

  [x]+ = max(x,0).

Any patch or reduced graph used for a cut must satisfy the inherited enclosure
obligation: every escape edge from S to legal free space outside the modeled
patch must be represented in delta(S), or the derived capacity is fictitious.

3. Component-cut circulation necessity
---------------------------------------
3.1 Witness-conditional theorem

Theorem candidate T-CUT-NECESSITY-WITNESS

Assume one selected-route witness satisfies Section 2. Then for every
S subseteq V:

  sum_c [ b[c,S] ]+ <= cap+(S)                         (1)

  sum_c [ -b[c,S] ]+ <= cap-(S).                      (2)

Proof skeleton

1. Fix commodity c and sum its vertex-conservation equations over all v in S.

2. Every edge internal to S appears once as outgoing and once as incoming, so
   internal terms cancel:

     b[c,S]
       = sum_{e in delta+(S)} f[c,e]
         - sum_{e in delta-(S)} f[c,e].

3. Since all flows are nonnegative:

     [b[c,S]]+ <= sum_{e in delta+(S)} f[c,e].

4. Sum over commodities and apply shared capacity:

     sum_c [b[c,S]]+
       <= sum_{e in delta+(S)} sum_c f[c,e]
       <= sum_{e in delta+(S)} q[e]
       = cap+(S).

5. Apply the same argument to -b[c,S] for incoming capacity.

This proof is independently confirmed by the heterogeneous review. It derives a
necessary inequality for a witness. It does not, by itself, prove that a chosen
separator model is a relaxation of production routing.

3.2 Layout-level quantifier form

A layout-level necessary condition must retain the existential decisions that a
real feasible witness is allowed to choose.

If a capacity substrate K(L,beta) has been independently proved to relax every
legal selected physical route under binding beta, then quantitative feasibility
of L implies:

  exists beta in BIND(L),
  exists u in U,
  exists r in R(L,beta,u),
  for every S subseteq V(K(L,beta)):

    sum_c [ b[c,S;beta,r] ]+ <= cap_K+(S),
    sum_c [ -b[c,S;beta,r] ]+ <= cap_K-(S).

The quantifier order is therefore:

  exists binding, exists duty/rate allocation, for all cuts.

It is not:

  for every binding;
  for one frozen equal-duty allocation;
  or for one arbitrary optimum endpoint returned by a solver.

Once beta is fixed, the allocation and flow feasibility question is an LP over
the duty polytope. At layout level, a finite binding search may call that LP at
its leaves, or a separately verified mixed discrete/continuous model may combine
them. A fixed-beta LP result cannot silently become a layout-level cut.

If K has not been proved a relaxation of production routing, its INFEASIBLE
result is telemetry or core-shrinking evidence only under CUT-R9-H1.

3.3 Allocation-independent Boolean strengthening

The current Boolean corollary does not need equal-duty or exact terminal rates.

For operation o, n_o = ceil(R_o). For any machine m in M_o:

  u_m
    = R_o - sum_{m' != m} u_m'
    >= R_o - (n_o - 1).

If R_o is non-integral, this lower bound is frac(R_o) > 0. If R_o is integral,
then n_o = R_o, the same equation and u_m <= 1 force every u_m = 1.

Therefore every mandatory machine carrying o has strictly positive utilization
under every legal allocation in U_o. Every selected terminal corresponding to a
positive recipe coefficient is consequently a positive-rate obligation,
independent of how duty is split among the machines.

Boolean corollary T-POSITIVE-COMPONENT

Let C be an isolated connected component of the free-cell graph. If C contains
a selected positive-rate source terminal of commodity c, then C must contain a
selected sink terminal of c. The converse holds for a positive-rate sink.
Otherwise the zero-capacity cut around C violates conservation for every legal
duty allocation.

Generic source and sink obligations require their own selected slot identity;
the corollary does not manufacture a named terminal from an unbound generic
slot.

This Boolean result is already present in production semantics:

  * routing precheck relaxed_disconnected;
  * F4 component_reach, the zero-capacity case;
  * the current binding-selection proof ladder.

It is not a novel cut family. Its value here is as the rate-independent first
consumer and as the base case of the later quantitative strengthening.

3.5 Capacity accounting note

Canonical A7 gives one item per tick per physical belt channel, aggregated across
commodities. In current routing semantics, every directed grid edge can carry at
most one selected channel across that direction. A single-cell cross-junction
may carry two perpendicular straight channels in one free cell, but it cannot
carry two same-axis channels, and a turn/splitter/merger excludes the second
channel.

Consequently:

  * per directed grid edge, the upper bound is one channel;
  * for a one-cell-thick axis-aligned separator wall, each free wall cell
    contributes at most one crossing channel;
  * for a jagged boundary, one cell may contribute two perpendicular crossings,
    so general capacity must be counted by directed cut edges, not by a universal
    one-per-cell rule.

The shipped two-per-free-wall-cell constant is therefore a candidate factor-two
loose bound on axis-aligned SAC-Hull walls. Tightening a shipped constraint is a
soundness-direction change and requires its own PCR-R5-style relaxation proof,
tests, and consumer review. This R0 file makes no production change.

3.6 Conditional lane-separation result

Canonical rate_lemma_scope already proves a lane-separation result inside one
explicit allocation profile: equal inter-machine duty, minimum lane count, and
no lane dilution. Under those premises, no two intermediate residual streams
may share a lane.

The weaker-precondition note records unequal duty profiles that retain the same
conclusion under an additional utilization lower bound. Neither profile is
recorded by the six certified predicates. These are conditional construction or
lower-bound tools, not unconditional layout theorems.

3.7 Power poles and the empty rectangle

Power and circulation are conjunctive obligations:

  PowerCover(P(L))
  AND
  CutFeasible(B(L),P(L),H(L),selected binding and duties).

A pole may improve power coverage while deleting four free cells and reducing
cut capacity. Its obstacle role belongs in the same context as the cut, not in a
late routing afterthought.

For a fixed empty rectangle H, adding H only removes legal cells and cut edges.
Thus a zero-condition infeasibility certificate whose premises do not depend on
H remains valid after adding any H. The converse fails. Because H is optimized,
every conditioned cut certificate must carry the exact rectangle as a premise;
zero-condition cuts alone do not tell the solver where the rectangle should go.

4. Zero-capacity owner separators
---------------------------------
The term membrane is deliberately not used here. It already names
CLAIM-ORDINARY-MEMBRANE-TERMINAL-BOUND-S48, a different geometric contact-length
object.

4.1 Definition

Fix layout L and two stable terminal regions A and Z, represented by physical
cell sets or stable terminal witnesses rather than transient component numbers.
Let O be the selected body and pole owners.

For M subseteq O, let G_free(L | release M) restore the occupied cells of owners
in M while keeping every other owner fixed.

M is an A-Z owner separator release set when:

  A and Z are disconnected in G_free(L),
  A and Z are connected in G_free(L | release M).

M is inclusion-minimal when no proper subset has that property. Let:

  SEP_L(A,Z)

be the family of all inclusion-minimal owner separator release sets.

4.2 Zero-capacity corollary

If a fixed selected terminal relation requires positive transfer from the A side
to the Z side, and the current free graph has no legal channel edge across the
separation, then the candidate terminal relation is infeasible. This is the
zero-capacity specialization of T-CUT-NECESSITY-WITNESS and the existing F4 /
relaxed_disconnected object.

A cross-junction cannot bypass the separator. There is no physical elevated
route: both perpendicular channels occupy one free cell and neither can occupy a
body or pole cell.

4.3 Sound repair form

For a repair that keeps the same positive transfer obligation, let F be the set
of owners whose occupied cells are released or changed enough to alter the free
graph. If the repaired free graph connects A to Z, finiteness implies that F
contains at least one member M of SEP_L(A,Z).

A complete repair may instead:

  1. change operation, mode, binding, or duty choices so the positive cross-side
     obligation disappears or becomes balanced;
  2. open a represented alternative portal/path whose support is included in the
     repair context;
  3. release a set F containing some M in SEP_L(A,Z).

One arbitrarily selected M is not an exhaustive cut. Minimal release sets need
not be unique or intersect. Compiling:

  move at least one owner of this M

as a master cut is unsound unless all alternative paths and the full separator
family have been covered, or an independent exact proof establishes the chosen
disjunction. This is another form of the CUT-R8-H1 support-context obligation.

4.4 Evidence boundary

E075 exhausted twelve ordered two-body placements and observed two INFEASIBLE
and ten empty-option-domain outcomes. It did not prove an owner separator theorem
and must not be cited as proof of Section 4. Owner-separator extraction remains a
candidate analysis to be independently implemented and checked.

5. Transport cores and geometric packing repair
-----------------------------------------------
5.1 Semantic b-matching candidate

The semantic transport space assigns operation, mode, or terminal
responsibilities to stable body rows. When the following premises hold, it has a
b-matching form:

  * stable left items and stable right body rows;
  * compatibility edges encode every local admissibility condition claimed;
  * declared degree and multiplicity requirements are fixed;
  * all compared assignments live in the same context;
  * global side constraints are either absent or carried separately.

Theorem candidate T-SEMANTIC-TRANSPORT-DECOMPOSITION

For two ordinary matchings M0 and M1 on one stable bipartite universe, every
connected component of M0 triangle M1 is an alternating even cycle or an
alternating path. If both matchings cover the same vertices with the same degree
requirements, only cycles remain. For a b-matching with one fixed degree vector,
split capacity-d vertices into d unit copies, or use an equivalent edge-colored
multigraph; the same edge decomposition follows.

Proof skeleton

Color M0-only edges old and M1-only edges new. At every equal-degree interior
vertex, the number of incident old and new edges is equal. Following unused
edges while alternating colors either closes a cycle or terminates at a degree
discrepancy. Repeating partitions the symmetric-difference edges.

This is a theorem candidate explaining the shape of E074's semantic assignment
model. E074 itself established a CP-SAT optimum over one context; it did not use
or prove matching decomposition. The b-matching framing is retroactive and must
retain candidate status until separately checked.

5.2 Minimum semantic transport core

For a fixed semantic target class T, introduce one change indicator z[u] per
stable semantic item u:

  minimize sum_u weight[u] * z[u]
  subject to assignment M1 in T.

If the b-matching premises hold, the changed support is a minimum weighted
semantic transport core, and its matching difference decomposes as above.

A small semantic core is a constructive assignment specification. It is not a
physical layout, route, throughput witness, or whole-layout proof.

5.3 Geometric repair is set packing

Physical body-to-footprint assignment has a different mathematical shape.
Candidate variables x[b,p] must satisfy at least:

  one footprint per required body;
  per-cell Sum_{b,p containing cell} x[b,p] <= 1;
  template, orientation, and footprint compatibility;
  power coverage;
  port-front and mode viability;
  any preserved outside geometry and empty-rectangle premises.

This is a colored set-packing or conflict-graph independent-set problem. Two
bodies choosing distinct but overlapping footprints are incompatible even
though an ordinary matching would allow them.

Consequences:

  * symmetric differences of feasible geometric assignments do not generally
    decompose into alternating paths or cycles;
  * applying one putative path in isolation need not preserve non-overlap or
    power feasibility;
  * a body-footprint matching relaxation can give only a lower bound on the
    minimum moved-body count;
  * the exact geometric repair must remain a set-packing optimization with its
    real side constraints.

E077's experimental geometry probe is direct evidence of this distinction: it
uses one selected-footprint count row and per-cell Sum <= 1 disjointness rows.

5.4 No adopted shortest-vacancy number

This direction adopts no claim that the shortest vacancy or relocation repair
moves six bodies. That number was not persisted as a machine result. The only
persisted E077 output currently corresponds to retaining 30 of 38 bodies, hence
eight moved bodies, and even that model omits port domains, modes, binding, and
connectivity. It is not a minimum for the real zero-condition problem.

A future numeric claim must persist the result, state the exact model scope, and
separate:

  matching-relaxation lower bound;
  exact geometric set-packing optimum;
  full placement/mode/binding/routing optimum.

5.5 Shared applicability premises

Every semantic or geometric change comparison must use:

  * stable physical or semantic identities;
  * the same object universe and explicit context transport;
  * direct-parent geometry when measuring physical movement;
  * full support for every compiled constant;
  * explicit treatment of generic slots and interchangeable copies;
  * no claim that a sequence of local changes has feasible intermediate states.

Transient destination indices, reconstructed component numbers, and display
strings are local indices only. They cannot transport a structural conclusion
across geometry or context.

6. Sufficient direction: typed orthogonal skeletons
---------------------------------------------------
The necessary property P is current Boolean component feasibility and, in a
future throughput model, duty-polytope cut feasibility. It is not one visually
fixed corridor.

For constructive search, define a stronger sufficient class P'. As a constraint
set, P' includes the required P obligations; as a solution set, P' may be a
strict subset of all valid layouts.

Candidate sufficient class: admissible typed orthogonal skeleton

A skeleton certificate K contains:

  * an embedded directed graph whose arcs map to reserved legal channel paths;
  * node types from the exact physical dictionary: dock, straight, turn,
    splitter, merger, and single-cell orthogonal cross-junction;
  * a stable dock assignment for every selected terminal front, including
    direction, commodity, slot identity, operation, and mode provenance;
  * for the quantitative extension, one legal duty allocation u in U and one
    terminal-rate vector r in R(L,beta,u), not a frozen equal-duty convention;
  * exact commodity flow on skeleton arcs when throughput is claimed;
  * shared capacity rows derived from canonical A7;
  * edge/cell occupancy compatibility, including poles and fixed H;
  * local degree and physical-pattern compatibility at every node.

A tree or cactus skeleton with typed docks is a plausible first restricted class
because its realization and flow decomposition are easier to prove. It is not
claimed necessary.

Constructor obligations

A skeleton class is sufficient only when a deterministic constructor and an
independent checker establish:

  1. every skeleton dock maps to one real selected port front or bound generic
     slot;
  2. every required terminal obligation is represented exactly once;
  3. every abstract arc maps to legal physical belt or cross-junction states;
  4. every turn, split, merge, and crossing maps to a legal local component;
  5. physical cell/channel capacity and exact side-union rules hold;
  6. directed edge balance and terminal adherence hold;
  7. operation, mode, binding, power, and empty-rectangle premises match the
     candidate layout;
  8. the constructed witness survives production precheck, exact routing,
     connectivity guard, serializer, and independent verifier;
  9. when quantitative throughput is claimed, an independent rational oracle
     replays the duty polytope, terminal allocation, and shared capacities.

Until these obligations are discharged, the skeleton is a research proposal
language, not a proof of binding or routing existence.

----------------------------------------------------------
The useful base is not the raw 70x70 cell graph and not a purely unlabeled free-

8. Cheapest falsification tests
-------------------------------
8.1 Quotient-space collision test

Purpose
  Refute a candidate quotient Q by finding two states that Q declares identical
  but whose exact continuation behavior differs.

Procedure

  1. Freeze a versioned Q implementation. For Q1, first freeze the deterministic
     decomposition algorithm; otherwise the test is not runnable.

  2. Collect diverse, fully materialized states from multiple constructors or
     geometry basins. Preserve direct-parent identity and context hashes.

  3. Compute canonical Q signatures using stable physical witnesses, not
     component or destination numbers.

  4. Group states by identical signature.

  5. Within each group compare the same exact observables:

       static port-domain status;
       component-compatible binding existence;
       positive-commodity count;
       shared component optimum and optimum face;
       exact routing status when reached;
       continuation value under one identical admitted action/context.

  6. A materially different outcome is a quotient collision. Add the smallest
     missing typed coordinate and rerun.

  7. Use held-out states from a different lineage for every revision.

Absence of sampled collisions cannot confirm a quotient. A valid abstraction
would eventually need a homomorphism, simulation, or equivalent all-state
argument. This test is only a cheap refuter.

8.2 Skeleton-decoding test

Purpose
  Refute a skeleton language or constructor without confusing constructor bugs
  with missing skeleton coordinates.

Procedure

  Calibration arm

  1. Take production routing fixtures or an independently accepted exact route
     witness whose physical realization is already known.

  2. Encode it into the proposed skeleton language and decode it again.

  3. If the constructor cannot round-trip these known-realizable cases, record a
     constructor failure. Draw no conclusion about the skeleton base.

  Language arm

  4. After calibration passes, generate held-out skeleton certificates from the
     coarse optimizer.

  5. Decode with no manual patching and materialize every certificate-to-grid
     mapping.

  6. Run production static precheck, component-aware binding, exact routing,
     connectivity guard, serializer, and independent verifier.

  7. Classify the first failed certificate premise: docking, direction,
     assignment, mode, separator support, crossing legality, terminal
     uniqueness, or another explicit coordinate.

  8. Repeated failure of one explicit skeleton premise refutes the current
     language. A generic decoder exception or failed calibration refutes only
     the constructor implementation.

  9. Count discretionary repairs after skeleton selection. If the calibrated
     decoder still requires many unrecorded local choices, the claimed short
     derivation basis is refuted.

Passing sampled decoding is evidence, not a proof that P' is sufficient. The
constructor and checker obligations in Section 6 remain.

Once E077 produces a component-compatible candidate:

  * run the calibrated deterministic constructor;
  * invoke exact routing and its connectivity guard;

Stage 4: duty-polytope LP oracle, explicitly pending

Replace the former exact-terminal-rate-verifier idea with an oracle over legal
duty allocation.

Until then, numerical circulation and rate-valued separator results are

not merely a Stage-4 restriction.

10. Initial go/no-go criteria
-----------------------------
Continue this candidate direction if at least one occurs:

  * one production-proved Boolean explanation removes many candidate contexts
    without nearing a full-assignment nogood;
  * E074-like semantic cores remain small and stable across neighboring
    contexts;
  * exact set-packing repair reduces broad geometry search to a bounded joint
    context;
  * a held-out quotient collision test eliminates coordinates rather than
    restoring the full cell state;
  * a calibrated skeleton decoder leaves little discretionary repair;
  * the line reaches exact routing sooner than the local-action lineage.

Narrow or retire it if:

  * useful production-proved failures cannot be compressed below near-complete
    assignments;
  * semantic cores are unstable or geometric repair remains global;
  * quotient collisions require restoring nearly the full cell state;
  * skeleton decoding repeatedly fails after constructor calibration;
  * theorem/checker work delays the whole-layout campaign without changing the
    next candidate or consumer;
  * the quantitative LP cannot preserve binding, generic-slot, enclosure, and
    relaxation quantifiers without becoming the original whole problem.
