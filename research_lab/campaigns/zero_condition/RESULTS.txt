R-ZC-000  Repeated point nogoods do not transmit the observed death family
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

scope: fixed-layout binding/routing experiments under their frozen contexts

statement:
  Phase-minus1 v1 observed 7578 exact routing-precheck failures across nine fixed
  layouts. Every event was front_blocked and the actual feedback was a 285-literal
  full-selection point nogood. The feedback changed the next binding selection,
  but the next selection remained FEASIBLE and returned to the same death family.

  particular local signature is a sound placement cut.

  enumeration is not the default answer to repeated front-blocked evidence.
