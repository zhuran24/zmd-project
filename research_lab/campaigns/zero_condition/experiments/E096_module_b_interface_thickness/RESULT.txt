Interpretation
--------------
The template cut is a name-level split with a geometry-sized bridge. The spatial
cut is a geometry-level split with a small explicit middle and eight integer
count obligations. The latter is the more faithful continuation language.

The selected cut also suggests a sufficient constructor that is cheaper than a
full separator master: reserve one manufacturing-free column at x=35. Low bodies
then end at x<=34, high bodies begin at x>=36, and both sides may use x=35 for
native fronts. This removes separator candidates and all cross-side body/front
coupling while preserving the eight class-allocation equations. It strengthens
the fixed-skeleton problem and therefore can produce a witness but cannot prove
the unrestricted skeleton infeasible.

Effect on the next problem
--------------------------
Do not build three template solvers. Compile the selected spatial language. The
cheapest constructive discriminator is an E097 restricted B model that reserves

native-front semantics, and expresses the eight low/high class allocations
