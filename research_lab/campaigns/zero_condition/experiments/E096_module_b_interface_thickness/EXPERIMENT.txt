EXPERIMENT E096: MODULE-B INTERFACE-THICKNESS CENSUS
===================================================

Question
--------
Which exact decomposition language has the thinner bridge for E095's 91-body
module-B native-front problem: splitting by manufacturing template, or splitting
spatially with an explicit separator module and class-count allocations?

Why this experiment
-------------------
E095 proves the y=41 A/B split is exact and localizes the remaining wall to B.
It would be premature to choose “template decomposition” merely because the
counts are 53/17/21, or “bay decomposition” merely because the geometry is
visual. The next cut must be derived from how much state it has to transport.

Object and interface semantics
------------------------------
E096 imports the hash-pinned E095 reconstruction and uses the exact active B
candidate universe under E092's fixed 53 poles and `boundary_macro_09`.

For any grouping, a cross-group occupancy cell belongs to the interface if:

  * bodies from more than one group can occupy that cell; or
  * a native front of a candidate in one group can be occupied by a body
    candidate in another group.

The count is an exact finite census of the current model's coupling surface. It
is not a solver-runtime prediction and the binary states of interface cells are
not assumed independent.

Template cut
------------
Groups are `manufacturing_3x3`, `manufacturing_5x5` and
`manufacturing_6x4`. Operation/front-demand counts are local to a template, so
no class-allocation state crosses this bridge. E096 records shared body cells,
cross-template front/body cells, participating candidates and the three-template
interaction graph.

Spatial cut
-----------
For every vertical and horizontal cut between grid coordinates, candidates are
classified as low-side, high-side or separator according to their body bounding
box. Separator candidates form an explicit third subproblem; they are not
silently deleted. The interface records:

  * cross-group occupancy cells;
  * candidates participating in those cells;
  * separator candidate count;
  * module-B class totals that require allocation among low/high/separator;
  * a log2 upper bound for the independent count-allocation box;
  * how E092's 91 selected B bodies split across the three groups.

Cuts used for the decision must be nontrivial: each side contains at least 20 of
the 91 E092 B bodies and at least 15 percent of nonseparator candidate rows; no
more than 15 E092 bodies may lie in the separator. All cuts remain in the output,
including those failing this guard.

Predeclared decision rule
-------------------------
SELECT_SPATIAL_SEPARATOR_DECOMPOSITION
  A guarded spatial cut has at most half as many occupancy-interface cells as
  the template cut, its separator contains at most 20 percent of B candidates,
  and its largest side is smaller than the largest template group. The class
  allocation vector is retained explicitly.

SELECT_TEMPLATE_DECOMPOSITION
  The template interface has no more occupancy cells and no more participating
  candidates than every guarded spatial cut. Its lack of count-allocation state
  then makes it the thinner bridge.

KEEP_BOTH_AND_BUILD_HYBRID_INTERFACE
  Neither cut dominates under the frozen rules. Preserve both and derive a
  hybrid template-within-bay representation rather than choosing by narrative.

Truth boundary
--------------
E096 is a no-solver interaction census. It establishes neither feasibility nor
infeasibility and does not estimate runtime from interface counts. A selected
cut authorizes only the next representation experiment. No certification, U/L,
production, owner, publication, routing or throughput effect follows.
