EXPERIMENT E046: OBJECTIVE-145 INTEGRATED SINGLETON-BODY PORTFOLIO
==================================================================

Question
--------
Does the fully materialized objective-145 endpoint still contain a singleton body
relocation whose value remains at least two units after port mode, operation
assignment, and binding are jointly reoptimized on the new geometry, or has the
singleton body axis saturated and forced a simultaneous body-pair neighborhood?

Why this experiment
-------------------
E044 and E045 separated ordinary body-proposal value from geometry-conditioned
joint value, but they still required two experiments. E046 integrates the cycle:

  residual body proposal
    -> exact fixed-outside ordinary screening
    -> novel geometry materialization
    -> exact geometry-conditioned joint middle-layer revaluation

The outer loop therefore proposes geometry but no longer publishes an ordinary
binding value as the geometry's continuation value.

Method
------
E046 starts from the E045 objective-145 assignment and fully materialized binding
endpoint. It reconstructs all 145 mismatch-component observations and all current
boundary literals. Among one-source mandatory or optional movable literals, it
selects an exact budget-six body portfolio by maximum residual coverage.

For every selected current literal it exhausts all non-overlapping,
power-preserving fixed-outside pose alternatives through the ordinary shared
binding objective. Same-footprint mode changes are excluded from the body axis.
Previously measured free-cell geometries are recognized by free-cell-set digest.
At most two distinct new geometries are materialized.

Each materialized geometry is then calibrated at its ordinary endpoint and solved
again with the faithful joint middle layer inherited from E045:

  conditional same-body port modes
  operation-to-footprint assignment
  native binding domains
  aggregate binding resources
  terminal uniqueness
  component-aware shared mismatch objective

The E046 runner supports checkpointed continuation. An initial five-minute tool
window ended after Seed A. The original implementation was only partly resumable:
arm checkpoints existed, but the arm manifest and materialized seeds were not
idempotent, and a layout check compared timestamps. E046 now validates and reuses
those exact bytes while recording both the pre-resume computational runner hash
and the current resumability wrapper hash. Those repairs change no candidate or
objective semantics.

Decision rule
-------------
Joint objective 0
  Enter exact routing immediately.

Best joint objective <= 143
  Retain the singleton geometry and recompute its residual body surface.

Best joint objective 144 or worse
  Stop serial singleton-body expansion and construct a simultaneous body-pair
  neighborhood.

Calibration failure
  Reject the geometry context and repair fidelity before using any free result.

Truth boundary
--------------
E046 exhausts one exact budget-six residual-selected singleton-body portfolio under
one objective-145 parent and gives exact joint middle-layer values to at most two
new free-cell geometries. It does not exhaust all body relocations or pairs, prove
a necessary placement, establish component compatibility, enter routing, change
U/L, or grant certification authority.
