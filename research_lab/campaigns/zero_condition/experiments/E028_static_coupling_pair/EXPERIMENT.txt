EXPERIMENT E028: simultaneous repair of the packaging/grinder static coupling
=============================================================================

Question
--------
Can a two-pose move reopen the packaging action that is impossible under every
fixed-outside unary context, and does the repaired domain materially reduce the
exact shared component mismatch?

Why this pair
-------------
E026 proves that packaging_battery pose 6189 has no usable unary alternative in
any live beam state. Under the objective-168 leader, replacement pose 6186 fails
for one specific reason: grinder_dense_blue_iron_002 remains at pose 6049 and its
front-filtered owner domain becomes empty.

E027 then triggers the serial-unary stop rule. The first simultaneous object is
therefore not an arbitrary high-coverage pair. It releases exactly the two poses
participating in the observed static coupling:

  packaging_battery_003          pose 6189
  grinder_dense_blue_iron_002    pose 6049

Method
------
The E024 objective-168 placement is fixed outside those two instances. Both
current poses must leave. E028 enumerates every pair of replacement poses that:

  stays inside the frozen candidate pools;
  avoids all fixed outside bodies;
  avoids mutual overlap;
  preserves the frozen selected power-pole set and exact power coverage.

There are 47 such pairs. Every pair is evaluated through the ordered interface:

  static owner domains
  aggregate binding resources and terminal uniqueness
  one shared component-mismatch optimum over all 19 commodities
  exact routing only if the shared objective reaches zero

The winning placement receives a fully materialized binding endpoint containing
the actual selection, port specifications, selected components, and mismatch
boundaries.

Competing readings
------------------
A. At least one pair reopens the packaging domain and materially improves 168.
   The new placement/binding seam should support bounded simultaneous pose
   neighborhoods rather than only unary feedback.

B. The pair reopens static domains but shared mismatch barely moves. Static
   coupling and component continuation are different ledgers; the next model
   should place pose and binding variables in the same neighborhood.

C. Every pair still dies at static or aggregate binding. The observed unary
   blocker explanation is not sufficient for repair; derive the next joint object
   from the exact rejecting domains rather than widening blindly.

D. A pair reaches zero. It enters exact routing immediately.

Truth boundary
--------------
E028 is exhaustive only for these two current instances with every outside
placement and selected power pole fixed. A negative result does not refute other
pairs, larger neighborhoods, or the no-ghost problem. A positive shared-mismatch
gain is not a routing witness.

Result
------
Completed with verdict SIMULTANEOUS_PAIR_MATERIAL_IMPROVEMENT. Forty-seven exact
pair states yield 45 empty owner domains and two shared optima at objective 166.
The winning child swaps the packaging and grinder operations across their two
existing 6x4 footprints, leaving the free-cell set unchanged while improving
168 -> 166. The complete binding endpoint is materialized. See RESULT.txt.
