E003: SINGLE-LITERAL REPAIR MATRIX
==================================

Trigger
-------
Run only if E002's four-literal responsibility-set neighborhood remains UNKNOWN.

Question
--------
Which individual member of the pocket conjunction can move while every other A1
selected group/optional pose remains fixed?

Four exact subproblems are solved independently. In each subproblem one cut
literal is forced to zero, the other three are forced to one, and every selected
A1 literal outside the cut remains one. Cardinality constraints require exactly
one replacement copy for the displaced operation group or optional pole.

Interpretation
--------------
FEASIBLE in one arm identifies a constructive repair direction and yields a
replacement geometry for the interface funnel.

INFEASIBLE in one arm proves only that this one-literal fixed-neighborhood repair
is empty.

UNKNOWN is a search/representation result. If all four arms remain UNKNOWN, the
pose-domain encoding rather than the semantic cut is the immediate wall.

The matrix does not prove global infeasibility and has no certified effect.
