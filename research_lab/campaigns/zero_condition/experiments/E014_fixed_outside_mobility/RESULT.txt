Latent project issue
--------------------
The historical interface treated a placement change and its downstream binding
response as separate events. E014 shows that this separation loses essential
bidirectional information:

  moving a boundary facility often invalidates a neighbor's port domain
  changing only a port mode preserves geometry but not component support
  moving one pole requires compensating power changes elsewhere

The minimum useful feedback object is therefore a coupling, not another unary cut
or unary relocation heuristic.

Truth boundary
--------------

not prove that any target is immovable jointly, that a particular pair suffices,
or that the no-ghost problem is infeasible.
