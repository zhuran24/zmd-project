EXPERIMENT E014: fixed-outside mobility portfolio
=================================================

Question
--------
Which of E013's 16 high-coverage E009 consumer literals possess real placement
freedom when every other selected group/optional pose is frozen, and does any
single-literal repair survive exact component-aware binding?

Why this precedes a joint placement-binding model
-------------------------------------------------
E013 gives an optimistic causal neighborhood: moving one selected boundary
literal could change many observed mismatch components. It does not show that the
literal has an alternative pose compatible with overlap, group cardinality,
power, or binding.

A large joint model would be premature if most of the proposed variables are
actually locked. With all other placements fixed, one mandatory move reduces to
exact pose enumeration, overlap, and power checks. One optional-pole move also
requires preserving power coverage for every powered facility. This thinner
problem can be exhausted before introducing dynamic connectivity variables.

Frozen neighborhood
-------------------
The reference is E009. The portfolio is the exact budget-16 maximum-coverage set
from E013. Each arm releases one current consumer literal and freezes all other
E009 placements:

  mandatory group-pose -> move the named current carrier to another pose of the
                          same facility type and operation group
  optional power pole  -> replace that selected pole pose with another pole pose

All candidates preserve:

  266 mandatory instances
  fixed outside occupied geometry
  cell non-overlap
  current optional-pole count
  exact power coverage under the master coverer table
  the already-satisfied E001 pocket judgment

The named mandatory carrier is only a materialization device. The durable object
remains the operation-group pose change.

Interface test
--------------
Every exact placement/power candidate is rebuilt as a fresh routing-aware binding
model with:

  static front-domain filtering
  duplicate terminal-key constraints
  all 19 commodity component-support guards fixed true

Candidates with an empty front-filtered owner domain are rejected before solve.
A component-feasible candidate immediately enters ordinary routing precheck and
exact simultaneous routing. No point binding nogood is generated.

Competing readings
-------------------
A. At least one one-literal repair reaches component-aware binding. The selected
   variable and its local alternatives define the first true joint neighborhood.

B. Many repairs are placement/power legal but all remain component-infeasible.
   Boundary coverage identified real mobility, but terminal choices must cohabit
   with pose choices in the next model.

C. Most high-coverage literals are locked under fixed outside geometry. The
   coverage portfolio is not an actionable neighborhood; release must expand by
   exact coupling rather than by another scalar objective.

Truth boundary
--------------
The experiment exhausts only one-literal fixed-outside repairs. An infeasible arm
does not prove that the literal cannot move when another selected facility moves
with it. A component-infeasible candidate is a fixed-layout result, not a no-
ghost impossibility proof.

Decision effect
---------------
A component-feasible arm goes directly to routing. Otherwise the exact mobility
and rejection matrix selects whether E015 should couple a small pair/portfolio of
poses to binding, or whether the neighborhood must be expanded by a proved
packing/power dependency.

Result
------
Completed with verdict PARTIAL_MOBILITY_COMPONENT_WALL_PERSISTS. Fifteen
manufacturing arms yield 425 exact placement/power alternatives; the selected
power-pole arm is locked under fixed outside geometry. Static owner domains reject
287 candidates; a later aggregate-capacity replay reclassifies three protocol-core
moves before component support, and the remaining 135 fail component support. No
candidate reaches exact routing. See RESULT.txt.
