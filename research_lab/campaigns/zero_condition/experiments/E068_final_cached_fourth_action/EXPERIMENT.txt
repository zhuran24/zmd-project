EXPERIMENT E068: FINAL CACHED FOURTH-ACTION DISCRIMINATOR
========================================================

Question
--------
Can one remaining cached E066 objective-one action, composed with a deterministic
representative of E067's strict-narrowest four-pattern face, create the corrected
fine/qiaoyu two-zero terminal-signature state?

Why this experiment
-------------------
E063 closes the complete-face-derived second-object language at directional
objective one. E066 finds only twelve actions that narrow the complete face from
six alternatives to five. E067 composes those actions and obtains six
four-alternative face classes, but no zero and no stable single unmatched
component.

The most frequent strict-narrowest class is:

  unmatched components  {1,4,8,12}
  qiaoyu sink component  15
  face frequency         16

E068 is the explicit stop test promised by E067. It does not invent a new action
universe. It fixes one deterministic representative of that class and composes it
once with every remaining objective-one action already frozen in E063/E066.

Method
------
1. Reconstruct the source-stable E063 pole parent and all 780 objective-one E063
   action specifications from the frozen chunk/manifest set.
2. Reconstruct all E067 admitted pair records. Verify that {1,4,8,12}/sink-15 is
   the unique most frequent four-pattern class, then choose its representative by
   minimum occupied symmetric difference and lowest pair index. This selects E067
   pair 27: candidate 596 plus candidate 729.
3. Rebuild the representative triple geometry from order-free parent deltas and
   reproduce its complete four-pattern face exactly.
4. For each remaining objective-one action with a distinct source object, merge
   its parent delta with the two representative deltas. Reject conflicting writes,
   cardinality drift, overlap, and power loss.
5. For every admitted fourth-action state, rebuild components, candidate-specific
   generic qiaoyu sink slots, and all manufacturing-6x4 target signatures. Require
   qiaoyu compatibility and minimize fine mismatch exactly. Enumerate the complete
   objective-one face when the result remains one.
6. Materialize a small deterministic set of objective-zero states for the full
   conditional port-mode/operation-assignment/binding consumer.

The scan consumes the existing E063/E066 action cache instead of re-enumerating
raw placement alternatives. It is checkpointed in deterministic chunks.

Decision rule
-------------
Any objective-zero state
  Enter the full conditional binding consumer immediately. A signature zero is
  proposal evidence, not yet the second zero of the full binding problem.

No zero and any UNKNOWN
  Preserve only the nonterminal states. Do not retire the lineage on incomplete
  evidence.

No zero and all states terminal
  Retire the pole-conditioned external-action lineage, regardless of whether a
  complete face narrows further. Switch to E062's distinct manufacturing-6x4
  near-miss parent. Further pole-lineage action composition would be search
  inertia rather than a new interface language.

Truth boundary
--------------
E068 fixes E055's first-zero state, the E063 pole move, the deterministic E067
pair-27 representative, and at most one additional cached E063 objective-one
action. It is a corrected terminal-signature relaxation. It does not establish
full binding, routing, throughput, U/L, certification, or a negative result for
other near-miss parents.

Result
------
Completed in run-002. The 678 admitted fourth-action states produce 671 exact
objective-one results, seven structural-empty states, no objective zero, and no
UNKNOWN. The pole-conditioned external-action lineage is retired; see RESULT.txt.
