EXPERIMENT E100: SOURCE-STABLE RESERVED-X42 HYBRID CONSTRUCTOR
==============================================================

Question
--------
Can the source-stable E099 module-B interface produce a native-front witness in
its cheapest sufficient hybrid form: exact template/front classes inside a
manufacturing-free column x=42 that separates low and high body domains?

Why this experiment
-------------------
E099 retracts E096's x_after_34 and E097's x35 continuation. The committed source
instead selects x_after_41 as the minimum guarded spatial interface, but spatial
and template interfaces are incomparable under the frozen dominance rules. The
first faithful hybrid should therefore use the x41 spatial geometry while keeping
all template-local operation/front classes explicit.

Reserving x=42 is a sufficient restriction derived from x_after_41. It is not a
claim that the unrestricted 174-candidate separator is unnecessary.

Method
------
1. Pin E095's source and machine packet, E099's source-isolated result/check, and
   E092's exact pole/boundary anchor. Compile operation_profiles and E095 from
   source bytes in a fresh process.

2. Reconstruct the complete 4,378-candidate module-B universe. Remove every body
   occupying x=42. Classify all survivors uniquely:

     low    max body x <= 41
     high   min body x >= 43

   Mechanically audit the fixed skeleton on x=42 and all low/high body/body and
   front/body interactions. The column remains free for native fronts.

3. Solve one pure-feasibility CP-SAT model retaining:

     91 module-B bodies
     exact 3x3 / 5x5 / 6x4 template counts
     all eight exact native-front demand classes
     all same-footprint native modes
     body non-overlap
     E092 fixed-pole power
     both stable E078 bodies and required operations
     eight explicit low/high allocation pairs

   No retained-count objective is present. The 87 surviving E092 anchor bodies
   are hints only; the four anchor bodies on x=42 are not silently relocated or
   treated as selected.

4. A positive is deterministically assigned named operations, combined with
   E095's frozen 128-body A witness, and replayed as a complete 219-body
   power/native-front-class witness.

Predeclared decisions
---------------------
Positive and replay PASS
  Advance the combined witness to terminal uniqueness, generic I/O and
  component-aware binding.

INFEASIBLE
  Reject only manufacturing-free x=42. Restore the source-stable x_after_41
  separator and solve through explicit separator plus allocation variables.

UNKNOWN
  Preserve censorship. Keep x=42 reserved and solve low/high sides conditioned
  on compatible allocation vectors; do not rerun the same monolith.

Truth boundary
--------------
A positive transfers to the unrestricted fixed-skeleton module-B problem. A
negative does not. Terminal uniqueness, generic I/O, commodity binding,
component compatibility, routing, throughput, U/L, certification, owner,
production and publication remain outside.
