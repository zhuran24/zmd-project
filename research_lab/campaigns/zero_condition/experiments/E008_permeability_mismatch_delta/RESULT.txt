Opposing port-domain effect
---------------------------
The same candidate prunes 602 more raw binding patterns than E001 and leaves 612
fewer survivors:

                         E001     E006     delta
  front-pruned patterns    4101     4703      +602
  surviving patterns      12955    12343      -612

At the same time, generic receiving ports improve from three to seven usable
slots. The regression is therefore concentrated in ordinary pose-level binding
alternatives rather than every type of terminal exposure.

Interpretation
--------------
Free adjacency is not the wrong direction. It buys a broad, exact reduction in
component mismatch across 13 of 19 commodities. But it is incomplete: by compacting
facilities without pricing their alternative terminal fronts, it moves pressure
from component topology into port-domain viability.

The next construction object must treat two resources jointly:

  free-space connectivity / component consolidation
  usable terminal exposure / binding-domain multiplicity

Neither should remain a late filter behind the other. A stronger adjacency-only
weight would likely continue trading one resource for the other.

Truth boundary
--------------
The deltas compare two fixed layouts, not all layouts reachable by the E006
objective. They do not prove any particular combined objective sufficient or
necessary. The result justifies retaining free adjacency as one term while adding
a port-exposure term in the same construction context.

Decision effect
---------------
The next cheapest probe optimizes the master’s already-existing front_clear
variables without adding a second large exact encoding. It produces a port-exposed
Pareto point from the E006 hint. The resulting candidate is evaluated by actual
surviving binding patterns and the same mismatch atlas; only then is a combined
front-exposure/permeability construction justified.
