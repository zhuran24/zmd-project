EXPERIMENT E008: permeability mismatch delta
============================================

Question
--------
Did E006's higher free adjacency and lower component count reduce the exact
per-commodity component mismatch measured in E004, or did it improve a proxy
while making terminal support unchanged or worse?

Why this test follows E007
--------------------------
E007 proves the E006 candidate is still component-binding INFEASIBLE. That binary
answer cannot distinguish "right direction, insufficient magnitude" from "wrong
statistic".

E008 reruns E004's differently shaped minimum-mismatch optimization on the E006
fixed placement, one fresh model per commodity. It compares every exact optimum
with the corresponding E001 optimum and independently replays the extracted
binding through production run_exact_routing_precheck.

Competing readings
------------------
A. Total and most per-commodity minima fall materially. Free adjacency is a valid
   but incomplete construction signal. Iterate or strengthen it with the missing
   statistic exposed by the residual mismatches.

B. Some commodities improve while others worsen, with little aggregate movement.
   Pairwise adjacency is not aligned with terminal topology. It needs a
   commodity-aware/main-component supplement rather than more weight.

C. Aggregate mismatch worsens. The proxy rewards compaction that blocks usable
   fronts or consolidates the wrong free cells. Retire it as the primary
   construction objective, while retaining optional-pole economy as a separate
   useful signal.

D. Any optimum/precheck disagreement refutes the measurement surface and blocks
   interpretation.

Truth boundary
--------------
Each optimum is exact only for one commodity in the fixed E006 placement binding
model. Delta signs compare two fixed layouts, not two global construction
families. The experiment does not prove the adjacency objective incapable of
finding a different better candidate.

Decision effect
---------------
A broad positive delta keeps free adjacency as one construction term. A mixed or
negative delta shifts the next positive representation toward usable-terminal
support in a shared large component, articulation reduction, or a solver-chosen
backbone. No result creates a necessary placement cut.

Result
------
Completed with verdict PERMEABILITY_PROXY_PARTIAL_INTERFACE_IMPROVEMENT. All 19
fresh mismatch solves reached OPTIMAL. Total minimum mismatch falls from 281 to
214; 13 commodities improve, four are unchanged, and two worsen. Meanwhile 602
more binding patterns are front-pruned. See RESULT.txt for the dual-resource
interpretation and truth boundary.
