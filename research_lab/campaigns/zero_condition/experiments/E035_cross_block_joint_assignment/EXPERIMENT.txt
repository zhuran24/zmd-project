EXPERIMENT E035: CROSS-BLOCK JOINT ASSIGNMENT AND BINDING
=========================================================

Question
--------
Does a residual-selected five-footprint 6x4 operation-assignment block compose
with the exact-saturated five-footprint 3x3 block when both assignment families
and all binding variables coexist in one fixed-geometry model?

Trigger
-------
E034 proves OPTIMAL 160 after directly cohabiting operation assignment and exact
binding on the five E031 3x3 footprints. Returning to swaps or permutations inside
that context is therefore closed.

Residual-context derivation
---------------------------
E035 reconstructs the materialized E034 objective-160 endpoint and its 160 exact
mismatch-component observations. The saturated 3x3 footprint set touches 51 of
them. For every current 6x4 group-pose literal, E035 transports its residual
boundary incidence into the actual group-pose consumer object.

A three-stage exact selector chooses five 6x4 footprints:

  maximize observations covered in addition to the saturated 3x3 block
  maximize operation diversity at that marginal optimum
  apply a deterministic lexical tie-break

The selection is data-derived inside the run. No footprint coordinates or
operation list are handwritten into the conclusion.

Joint model
-----------
The occupied geometry, power poles, and every operation outside the two selected
blocks remain fixed. One CP-SAT model contains, for both blocks:

  footprint-operation assignment variables
  conditional exact pose-level binding-pattern variables
  exact operation multiplicity constraints

The two blocks share the unchanged outside PortBindingModel, generic-I/O
requirements, duplicate-terminal rules, and one all-commodity component-mismatch
objective. Complete assignments are not enumerated outside the model.

Exchangeability and calibration
-------------------------------
Mandatory instances are quotiented only within operation groups whose metadata
and generic-I/O obligations are byte-equivalent. Every moving operation must have
exact pose-level binding and no dynamic generic slot family.

The model is first fixed to two known objective-160 warm states. Both must return
OPTIMAL 160 before the free result can be interpreted.

Outcome interpretation
----------------------
OPTIMAL objective 0
  Materialize the concrete assignment and ordinary endpoint, then enter exact
  routing immediately.

OPTIMAL objective 1..159
  The two assignment blocks compose. Persist the full joint witness and endpoint,
  then derive the next context from the new residual rather than returning to
  external swap enumeration.

OPTIMAL objective 160
  The expanded 3x3+6x4 fixed-geometry context is exact and saturated. The next
  representation must enlarge by another problem-derived block or release
  geometry; repeating operation actions inside these footprints is closed.

FEASIBLE / UNKNOWN
  Record only incumbent and bound. Do not claim saturation.

Truth boundary
--------------
This experiment fixes occupied geometry and every operation outside ten selected
manufacturing footprints. It is not a global assignment optimum, a routing
result, a whole-layout infeasibility proof, a lower bound, or a certification
artifact.

Result
------
Completed. Both objective-160 warm assignments recalibrate to OPTIMAL 160, and
the free two-block model proves OPTIMAL 159. The gain is carried by a 6x4
operation reassignment while the saturated 3x3 assignment remains unchanged.
