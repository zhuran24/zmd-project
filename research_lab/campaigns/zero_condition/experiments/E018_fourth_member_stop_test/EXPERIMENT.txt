Stop rule
---------
A fourth move is considered material only if it improves the exact shared
objective and that improvement survives optimum-face audit. A zero candidate
enters routing.

If no arm improves 176, or if improvement is only a small branch-specific step
amid many tied states, the research line stops canonizing one state. The next
outer-loop representation becomes a small beam over distinct equal-score
geometries, each carrying its exact shared-binding score and residual boundary
signature.

Truth boundary
--------------
This experiment is exhaustive only for four selected fourth literals after one
representative triple. Failure does not refute other triples or joint multi-pose
moves. It is a model-selection experiment for the outer loop, not a global
infeasibility proof.
