EXPERIMENT E076: THIRD-BODY FOLLOW-UP PROBES ON THE E075 FRONTIER
================================================================

Record status
-------------
This directory is a retrospective record, written after the fact. E076 was run as
six disposable probe scripts under research_lab/local/zero_condition/, not as a
tracked runner in this tree, and no question, decision rule, or stop boundary was
declared in advance. Unlike every other numbered experiment in this campaign,
E076 persisted nothing at all: none of the six scripts contains a file write of
any kind. This record therefore documents what was asked, not what was found.

Question
--------
E075 closed the target-26 two-body native context, with ten of twelve pairs dying
because moving owners A and B emptied some other destination row's native option
domain. E076 asks the obvious follow-up: which body causes that collapse, and can
a third body be moved to repair it?

What each script sets out to do, transcribed from its own docstring and, for the
undocumented first one, from its code:

  E076_probe.py
    Reconstructs the E069 parent, takes E075 pair 4, and builds its candidate
    two-body solution and base occupancy. No docstring; the entry point for the
    others.
  E076_eval_probe.py
    Causal third-owner alternatives through E075 run-002.
  E076_blocker_probe.py
    Explain all filling_capsule_001 empty domains in E075 pair 4.
  E076_pair_domain_probe.py
    Count joint filling/blocker geometry domains in E075 pair 4.
  E076_min_pair_eval.py
    Evaluate the six filling/planter009 joint geometries.
  E076_three_body_probe.py
    Exhaust E075 core pairs plus filling_capsule_001 moves.

The named objects, E075 pair 4, filling_capsule_001 and planter009, are the ones
the scripts reference; whether any of them is in fact the blocking body is not
recorded anywhere.
