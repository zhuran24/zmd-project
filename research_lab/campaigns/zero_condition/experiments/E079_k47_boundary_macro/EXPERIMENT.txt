E079 K=47 BOUNDARY MACRO
========================

Status
------
Completed in run-001 from the independently rechecked K=47 boundary-packing
theorem. Research-only. No certification, U/L, production, owner, pruning, or
publication effect.

Question
--------
Can the frozen 46-body `boundary_storage_port` placement subproblem be replaced
by one exact 47-state macro without losing any locally legal packing, pose/front
identity, or canonical instance-label recovery?

This is the first concrete test of the direction file's “necessary account ->
finite structural base” proposal. It is deliberately boundary-only.

Object space
------------
The experiment consumes the frozen candidate-placement pool and the 46 mandatory
boundary-port instances. It enforces exactly the K=47 theorem's local object:

- each selected row is one legal `boundary_storage_port` pose;
- exactly 46 poses are selected;
- selected body cells are mutually disjoint.

It does not enforce other facility bodies, front clearance against other
facilities, binding, routing, throughput, export, or game review.

Arms
----
1. Baseline pose model

   Build 136 pose Booleans, select exactly 46, and impose per-cell body
   exclusivity. Exhaustively enumerate every feasible pose set.

2. Boundary macro

   Canonically order the baseline solutions and emit one macro state per complete
   46-pose packing. Each state carries stable pose indices, body/front cells and
   digests, the omitted allowed-L cell, side anchors, and the canonical sorted-ID
   to sorted-pose label recovery.

3. Exact one-hot encoding

   Encode 47 state Booleans with ExactlyOne. Separately build an optional
   state-to-pose channeling model without `AddElement`, using linear incidence
   equalities. Enumerate and roundtrip both encodings against the baseline.

Success criterion
-----------------

- frozen full candidate-pool identity matches the independently rechecked pool;
- the boundary pool contains exactly 136 poses;
- the baseline model has exactly 47 complete solutions;
- each solution has 23 left and 23 bottom bodies, 138 distinct body cells, 46
  distinct inward fronts, and one omitted cell in the 139-cell allowed L-lane;
- all 47 omitted cells are distinct and exhaust the legal omitted-cell set;
- macro -> pose set -> macro is bijective;
- canonical instance-label recovery is complete and reversible;
- one-hot and channelled macro encodings each enumerate exactly the same 47
  states;
- all five up-to-K consumption conditions and the rank-1 non-WLOG guard are
  embedded in the artifact.

Truth boundary
--------------
A successful E079 proves an exact quotient of the boundary-only body-packing
subproblem. It does not prove that any macro state extends to a full layout. A
consumer must preserve all 47 alternatives unless it separately proves a subset
complete, remain instance-permutation equivariant or use the canonical recovery
mapping, and reapply every omitted full-layout constraint.
