EXPERIMENT E011: jointly guarded permeability step
=================================================

Question
--------
Can a radius-limited free-adjacency step around E009 improve permeability while
preserving the selected-pattern and generic-port resources already earned by
E009?

Why this test comes next
------------------------
E006 improves component topology but harms binding-domain survival. E009 restores
and exceeds real pattern survival, and E010 shows a second broad mismatch gain.
The next step must not optimize either resource in isolation.

Change under test
-----------------
E011 rebuilds the ordinary no-ghost pose-bool master with the checked E001 pocket
cut. In the frozen E009 occupancy it recomputes, for every candidate group pose:

  exact routing-visible binding patterns whose terminal cells are free
  clear physical generic input/output ports

These contextual coefficients are not optimized. They become hard guards:

  selected-pattern score >= E009 reference score
  generic-clear score    >= E009 reference score
  selected power poles   <= 53
  at most 20 E009 selected literals may leave

Within those guards, E011 adds exact adjacent-free-pair Booleans and maximizes
free adjacency. A complete E009 hint is supplied, presolve is disabled, and
bounded LNS searches around the known feasible point.

Competing readings
------------------
A. A new candidate improves free adjacency and the independently rebuilt actual
   pattern domain while reducing component mismatch. The two-resource local
   constructor is viable and may iterate.

B. The contextual pattern guard is satisfied, but actual pattern survival drops.
   Simultaneous pose movement breaks the first-order guard; shrink the trust
   region or move exact pattern viability into a local joint model.

C. No adjacency improvement is found while preserving the guards. Scalar local
   objectives have reached a useful limit; move component-aware binding into a
   smaller joint placement-binding neighborhood.

D. Component-aware binding becomes FEASIBLE. Advance immediately to production
   precheck and exact routing.

Frozen context
--------------
E009 result, assignment, and layout plus E010 mismatch atlas are pinned by SHA-256
in the runner. The runner also pins the E001 lowering and all consumed solver and
semantic sources.

Budget
------
60-second presolve-disabled LNS, eight workers, exact complete hint. Fresh
component-aware binding is always evaluated on an improved candidate.

Truth boundary
--------------
The pattern and generic coefficients are contextual first-order guards around
E009, not globally valid constraints. The trust region and pole cap are research
restrictions. A candidate remains placement-plus-power until the downstream chain
accepts it.

Decision effect
---------------
A faithful joint gain earns a fresh mismatch atlas and another evidence-driven
iteration. No gain or an unfaithful guard moves the next experiment to an exact
local joint placement-binding model. No outcome creates a production objective or
necessary cut.

Result
------
Completed with verdict JOINT_STEP_FAITHFUL_CANDIDATE. Compared with E009, the
candidate gains 29 free-adjacency edges, 30 real meaningful binding patterns, 20
free cells, and removes five power poles while satisfying the contextual guard.
Component count remains 83 and component-aware binding remains INFEASIBLE. See
RESULT.txt.
