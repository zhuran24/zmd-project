EXPERIMENT E054: CAUSAL FINE-POWDER OPERATION SWAPS
===================================================

Question
--------
Does the fourteen-body E053 context contain a first-zero state that the free
symmetric search could not find, when the operation-to-body relation is seeded by
the exact fine-powder obstruction?

Why this experiment
-------------------
E053 faithfully recalibrates objective 139, but a 180-second free target-zero
solve returns UNKNOWN without an incumbent. This is not evidence of infeasibility.
The expanded 6x4 block has strong operation symmetry.

E052 identifies three right-edge `grinder_fine_buckwheat` bodies whose outputs lie
in the source-only component and three `filling_capsule` bodies whose inputs lie in
other components. A direct operation swap should place a capsule sink on the
right-edge body and a grinder source on a sink-side body while preserving the
operation multiset and occupied geometry.

Method
------
E054 enumerates the nine source-body/capsule-body pairs. For each pair:

  all other block destinations keep their current operation and pose mode
  the right-edge body is fixed to operation `filling_capsule`
  the capsule body is fixed to operation `grinder_fine_buckwheat`
  the two swapped destinations retain all admitted same-body modes
  binding remains free
  `fine_buckwheat_powder` mismatch is fixed to zero

Each pair first receives a constant-objective exact feasibility solve. If one or
more pairs are feasible, the best observed seed is rebuilt and total mismatch is
minimized under the same swap and target-zero constraints. The resulting state is
materialized and replayed before it becomes a research state.

Decision rule
-------------
At least one feasible swap
  The merged assignment layer crosses 19/19 -> at most 18/19. Retain the exact or
  best feasible first-zero state and recompute residual causes with positive count
  as primary progress.

All nine swaps INFEASIBLE
  This specific one-for-one operation-transfer mechanism is closed. Return to the
  free fourteen-body context or build a simultaneous multi-operation/body model.

Any solve UNKNOWN
  Preserve the scoped nonterminal result; do not call the pair infeasible.

Truth boundary
--------------
E054 tests nine exact operation-pair constraints inside E053's frozen occupied
geometry and inherited conditional context. It does not exhaust all fourteen-body
assignments, body movement, routing, throughput, U/L, or certification.

Result
------
Completed. Six of nine causal swaps are exact first-zero states. The selected
`grinder_fine_buckwheat_004 <-> filling_capsule_003` relation is OPTIMAL at total
139 with positive commodity count 18 and `fine_buckwheat_powder = 0`. See
RESULT.txt.
