EXPERIMENT E066: COMPLETE-FACE ATLAS OF THE POLE-CONDITIONED PAIR FRONTIER
===========================================================================

Question
--------
Do any of E063's 780 objective-one two-object states have a genuinely narrower
complete optimum face, so that a third causal relation can target one stable
unmatched component, or does the pole-conditioned lineage remain face-wide and
need to be retired in favor of the distinct 6x4 near-miss parent?

Why this experiment
-------------------
E063 proves that one fixed lower-right pole move plus any one of 858 causally
selected second-object geometries never reaches the corrected fine/qiaoyu two-zero
signature condition. Seven hundred eighty states remain at directional distance
one. Their returned presence witnesses are not unique theorem objects: E065 already
showed that equal objective values can select different optimum representatives.

A third-object search driven by one returned component would therefore repeat the
same state-identity error. Before admitting a third relation, E066 enumerates the
complete objective-one presence face of every E063 objective-one state and asks
whether those faces compress.

Method
------
1. Pin the source-stable E063 runner, run-008 result, candidate manifest, all chunk
   records, E061/E062 sources, and E065's E062 replay.
2. Start under a fresh Python bytecode cache and verify that every module-defined
   function loaded from E061, E062, and E063 originates in its requested source.
3. Consume E063's manifest and chunk records directly; do not regenerate the 858
   candidate portfolio or use one selected presence witness as the candidate ID.
4. For each of the 780 objective-one candidate geometries, reconstruct the exact
   pair state, rebuild components and generic sink space, and enumerate the full
   qiaoyu-hard fine-mismatch-one face to completion.
5. Normalize each face as a set of terminal-presence patterns, quotient equal
   faces by digest, and record pattern count, unmatched-component union, qiaoyu
   sink set, action type, and physical candidate identity.
6. Compare every face with the parent six-pattern/six-component face. A candidate
   is a stable third-relation parent only when its complete face has one unmatched
   component in one orientation, not merely when one solver witness does.

The run is checkpointed in deterministic candidate chunks. This remains a
terminal-signature relaxation; no face class is a full binding witness.

Decision rule
-------------
At least one complete face has one stable unmatched component
  Retain a small deterministic set of those pair geometries and derive a third
  relation from the stable component's boundary or missing filling signature.

No stable singleton, but at least one face strictly reduces pattern count or the
unmatched-component union below the parent's six
  Retain only the narrowest face classes and derive the next relation from their
  common residual structure.

No complete face narrows
  Retire the pole-conditioned lineage at two-object depth and switch to E062's
  distinct 6x4 near-miss parent. Do not enlarge the pole lineage into an arbitrary
  third-object product.

Any nonterminal face enumeration
  Preserve completed chunks and continue only the nonterminal candidates.

Truth boundary
--------------
E066 analyzes E063's source-stable signature-relaxation states. It does not prove
full binding feasibility or infeasibility, close a different first-zero state,
enter routing, update U/L, or grant certification.

Result
------
