EXPERIMENT E045: REVALUE THE TWO OBJECTIVE-146 BODY GEOMETRIES WITH THE JOINT MIDDLE LAYER
================================================================================================

Question
--------
Which of E044's two ordinary-objective-146 body geometries becomes the true
continuation leader after exact geometry-conditioned port-mode,
operation-assignment, and binding reoptimization?

Seeds
-----
Seed A moves planter_buckwheat_007 one cell down from pose 11045 to 11046 while
retaining E043's 6x4 body move.

Seed B moves crusher_sandleaf_003 from pose 2155 to 5342 while retaining E043's
6x4 body move.

Both have ordinary fixed-layout shared objective 146 but different free-cell
partitions.

Method
------
E045 reconstructs the exact E043 Seed A joint context. The original E041
25-footprint blocks are first transported onto E043's retained 6x4 geometry, and
the ten E043 mode-enabled destinations remain active.

Each E044 seed differs from the objective-147 parent by exactly one body outside
that context. The moved body is added as a singleton conditional mode block. For
each seed:

  1. fix every operation, port mode, and binding choice;
  2. reproduce the frozen E044 objective 146 exactly;
  3. free the joint port-mode, operation-assignment, and binding middle layer;
  4. materialize and ordinarily replay the best joint endpoint.

The implementation reuses the already checked E043 geometry-seed solver after
reconstructing the correct E043 context. It does not compare E044's ordinary 146
values directly.

Decision rule
-------------
Objective 0
  Enter exact routing immediately.

One seed has a strictly lower OPTIMAL joint value
  Retain that geometry, rebuild its residual/body-response surface, and stop using
  E044's stale ordinary ranking.

The seeds tie with distinct witnesses
  Retain a small geometry beam and compare their body-response domains.

Neither seed improves below 146
  Stop singleton body proposals and build a simultaneous body-pair neighborhood
  around the objective-147 parent.

Truth boundary
--------------
E045 compares only two frozen singleton body moves composed with E043 Seed A.
Their joint contexts remain bounded to the transported E043 assignment blocks
plus one singleton moved body. The result does not optimize body geometry inside
one model, exhaust body pairs, prove component compatibility, reach routing,
establish a lower bound, or grant certified effect.

Result
------
Completed. Both ordinary objective-146 seeds calibrate exactly. Seed A remains
OPTIMAL 146 after freeing the joint middle layer. Seed B reaches OPTIMAL 145 and
is the strict geometry winner. Its singleton body chooses same-footprint pose
5343, and the audited ore-pair sum remains 14; the one-unit gain lies in the fixed
commodity ledger.

Seed B is retained as the new leading endpoint. See RESULT.txt for context
reconstruction, exact model sizes, optimum-face audits, and the rule that geometry
deltas must be measured against the immediate materialized parent rather than an
older named-instance assignment.
