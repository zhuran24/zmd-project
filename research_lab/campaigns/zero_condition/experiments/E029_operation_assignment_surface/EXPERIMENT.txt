EXPERIMENT E029: residual operation-assignment surface of the objective-166 pair child
====================================================================================

Question
--------
After E028 improves the shared component objective without changing free space,
which current operation-to-footprint assignments touch the remaining 166 mismatch
components, and what finite swap portfolio should be tested next?

Why this representation
-----------------------
The E028 child differs from its parent only by exchanging packaging_battery and
grinder_dense_blue_iron across two existing manufacturing_6x4 footprints. This
proves that occupied geometry and operation/terminal assignment are distinct
construction resources.

E029 therefore does not propose another scalar geometry objective or another
single-pose move. It freezes the fully materialized E028 binding endpoint,
reconstructs the exact residual mismatch-boundary incidence surface, and derives
same-facility-type operation swaps that preserve the occupied cell set by
construction.

Method
------
E029:

  verifies the E028 assignment, layout, endpoint, objective, and selection digest;
  rebuilds the 166 mismatch observations from the frozen selected components;
  transports every boundary owner to the current group/optional pose literal;
  compares the 166 ranking with the three retained objective-173 parent surfaces;
  enumerates pairs of current mandatory literals with the same facility type but
    different operations and exactly one represented source instance each;
  scores each swap by the union of residual observations touched by its two
    current literals;
  excludes the immediate E028 reverse swap, whose exact value 168 is already
    known;
  freezes a small highest-coverage swap portfolio for exact shared-binding replay.

A swap candidate is only a proposer. Identical facility type makes the exchange
occupancy-preserving, but static domains and exact shared binding still decide
whether it is useful.

Competing readings
------------------
A. A compact operation-swap portfolio covers a substantial residual fraction.
   Evaluate it before introducing a heavier pose-binding model.

B. High-coverage swaps are dominated by one operation or repeat already rejected
   actions. Use a diversified portfolio or move directly to pose-binding
   cohabitation.

C. The 166 surface differs sharply from the retained 173 parents. Preserve the
   branch-specific state and avoid a global action ranking.

Truth boundary
--------------
Boundary touch is not continuation value. E029 proves neither swap feasibility nor
objective improvement. It selects a finite, occupancy-preserving action portfolio
under one frozen endpoint and carries no routing, certification, or ledger effect.

Result
------
Completed with verdict OPERATION_ASSIGNMENT_SWAP_PORTFOLIO_PLAUSIBLE. The
objective-166 endpoint contains 166 mismatch observations and 315 current
boundary literals. E029 derives 8,486 occupancy-preserving same-type operation
swaps and freezes a diversified twelve-swap portfolio; the leading pair touches
29 observations. See RESULT.txt.
