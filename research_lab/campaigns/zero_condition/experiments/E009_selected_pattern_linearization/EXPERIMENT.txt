EXPERIMENT E009: selected-pose binding-pattern linearization
============================================================

Question
--------
Can a selection-conditioned port-exposure objective complement E006's free-space
permeability without paying for every dynamic binding pattern inside the full
master?

Rejected neighboring proxy
---------------------------
The raw sum of all master front_clear variables is not selection-conditioned.
E001 scores 4475 and E006 scores 4623 on that quantity, yet E006 has 612 fewer
surviving actual binding patterns. It rewards hypothetical ports belonging to
unselected poses and is rejected before a solve.

Change under test
-----------------
E009 treats E006 as the current outer-loop context. For every mandatory group pose
with exact pose-level binding, it enumerates the raw legal binding patterns and
counts how many have all routing-visible terminal cells free in the E006
occupancy. The resulting integer becomes a coefficient on that pose's existing
master literal.

Generic hub poses receive a smaller coefficient equal to their currently clear
physical generic ports. Power poles receive a negative tie-break coefficient.
The objective is therefore:

  exact-pattern survivors, lexicographically dominant
  then generic physical port exposure
  then fewer selected power poles

No new objective variable is introduced. The checked E001 pocket cut remains
attached.

Because the coefficients are a first-order view of one fixed occupancy, E009
uses a trust region: at most 20 currently selected group/optional poses may leave
the E006 selected set, and the power-pole count may not exceed 54. This is a
research search restriction, not a necessary condition.

Competing readings
------------------
A. Predicted and actual surviving patterns both improve while component mismatch
does not regress. The outer-loop linearization is a useful port-exposure term and
can be combined with permeability in the next constructor.

B. Predicted score improves but actual pattern survival does not. Interactions
among simultaneously moved poses dominate the first-order model. Reduce the trust
region or move exact pattern viability into a local joint placement-binding
model.

C. Actual patterns improve but component mismatch worsens. The two-resource
Pareto tension is real; construct a combined or alternating objective rather than
replacing E006.

D. Component-aware binding becomes FEASIBLE. Advance immediately to production
precheck and exact routing.

Frozen context
--------------
E006 candidate result, assignment, and layout are pinned by SHA-256 in the runner.
E007 and E008 results are also pinned as the trigger and baseline interface
measurements.

Budget
------
Master build under the frozen pose-bool model, followed by a 60-second complete-
hint, presolve-disabled LNS. Component-aware binding is evaluated on any improved
candidate.

Truth boundary
--------------
The objective coefficients are valid only as a local linearization around E006.
They are not a theorem about future occupancy and do not create a production
objective or cut. A master candidate remains placement-plus-power only until the
interface and routing chain accepts it.

Decision effect
---------------
A faithful improvement earns an alternating permeability/exposure constructor.
An unfaithful improvement moves the exact interaction into a smaller joint local
model. No-gain preserves the E006 candidate and redirects effort to a connected
backbone representation.

Result
------
Completed with verdict PATTERN_EXPOSED_CANDIDATE. The contextual linearization
predicted +1085 meaningful surviving patterns; fixed-layout reconstruction
measured +1101, with only +16 error. The candidate also reduces free components
91 to 83 and grows the largest component 405 to 414, but remains component-binding
INFEASIBLE. See RESULT.txt for the postprocess recovery and truth boundary.
