Scientific interpretation
-------------------------
E115's remaining disjunction over separator class tuples was not the dominant
search wall. Even after that choice is removed, the complete model still spends
its budget jointly selecting body geometry and satisfying dynamic native-front
occupancy.

This is an instrument result, not evidence that the tuples are infeasible. It
also prevents a new finite-basis inertia: extending the same method from twelve
tuples to all 350 separator-positive tuples would multiply censored solves
without changing the representation that currently fails.

The stronger signal remains E114's full-consumer death shape. Arbitrary
body/power representatives contain 11..22 bodies with empty local mode/class
domains. The next proposer should therefore make local native-front survival a
first-class geometry condition and learn small blocker cores when a proposed
body becomes front-dead.
