EXPERIMENT E114: DIRECT CONSUMER FOR E110 FIXED HIGH-SIDE GEOMETRIES
===================================================================

Question
--------
Does any one of E110's 27 complete 26-body body/power witnesses admit the full
x42-high native-front class semantics when its body geometry is fixed but every
same-footprint mode and all eight B class coordinates remain free?

Why this experiment
-------------------
E110 preserved one full 26-body geometry for every exact separator template
duty, but E111-E113 consumed only the three template counts. The recent inertia
audit identified this as construction-side consumer debt. A representative
geometry cannot prove a whole template state impossible, but any representative
that succeeds is already a valid constructive witness and should be sent
straight to the next consumer.

Method
------
1. Pin E095/E100/E101, E110's complete 27-state body/power atlas, and E113's
   interaction result. Reconstruct the source-stable x42 language.

2. For every E110 witness, map all 26 stable global-row identities back to E100's
   high-side rows and verify exact 10/6/10 template totals, non-overlap, fixed-pole
   power and both stable E078 bodies.

3. Build E101's unchanged complete high-side native-front model, then fix every
   high-side body variable to the E110 geometry. Leave all same-footprint modes
   and all eight B class allocations free. Solve each geometry with a
   deterministic one-worker profile; retry only UNKNOWN with a fresh multiworker
   model having the identical feasible set.

4. Collect positive high allocations. For each distinct allocation, solve E101's
   unchanged 65-body x42-low complement. Stop at the first paired witness, combine
   it with E095's frozen 128-body module-A witness, and replay all 219 manufacturing
   bodies for power and native-front class capacity.

Decision rules
--------------
Paired 219-body replay PASS
  Advance immediately to terminal uniqueness, generic I/O and component-aware
  binding.

At least one fixed high geometry positive, but no paired low witness
  Preserve the exact high allocations and low-consumer terminal states. Continue
  only their allocation handshake; do not return to abstract interface growth.

All 27 fixed high geometries exact INFEASIBLE
  Reject only these 27 representative geometries. Use their full-consumer death
  data to redesign geometry or a joint collar; do not reject any E110 template
  state.

Any UNKNOWN
  Keep it local to the named geometry or allocation. UNKNOWN creates no rule.

Truth boundary
--------------
The 27 E110 witnesses are one body/power representative per separator template
state, not a complete geometry basis. Negative results are geometry-specific.
Positive results are valid constructive witnesses. No routing, throughput,
certification or U/L effect follows from native-front success alone.
