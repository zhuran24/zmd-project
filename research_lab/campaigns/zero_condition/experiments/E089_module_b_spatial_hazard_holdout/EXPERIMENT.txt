EXPERIMENT E089: TEMPORAL-HOLDOUT SPATIAL HAZARD ATLAS FOR MODULE B
==================================================================

Question
--------
Do E088's diffuse module-B front failures nevertheless concentrate in a small
set of stable spatial roles that generalizes to later failures?

The test must not select and evaluate regions on the same 178 labels. E089 uses
module-B rules already present after E086 as training data and the 83 module-B
rules added only by E087 as a temporal holdout.

Why this experiment
-------------------
E088 rules out two attractive bulk languages. Translation-normalized stencil
families cover most registered failures only by touching almost the whole B
universe; context-sensitive support families are almost one per registered row.
That negative says the point-rule representation is too fine, but does not yet
say whether the geometry itself has a stable hazardous subregion.

A spatial role that predicts later failures would justify changing the B-bay or
reserving front halo there. A role that fits only earlier failures is not a
constructor rule and must not steer the next master.

Frozen data split
-----------------
Training labels:

  module-B candidate indices in E086's final checkpoint

Holdout labels:

  module-B candidate indices added in E087's final checkpoint and absent from
  E086

The complete unlabeled universe is E088's 4,353 candidate map. Candidate ordering
and checkpoint index identity are already checked by E088's exact remap.

Spatial features
----------------
For every candidate, E089 computes only label-independent features:

  * template and visible demand-class stratum;
  * body gap to the y=41 seam;
  * nearest top/left/right grid boundary gap;
  * Manhattan gap to protocol-core body or reserved core terminals;
  * Manhattan gap to the 52 inherited fixed-pole bodies;
  * Manhattan gap to the removed seam-pole body;
  * option-count band;
  * universe-derived quartile bands for maximum manufacturing, pole and
    boundary-state front-cell support pressure.

Distance bins are fixed before labels are inspected. Pressure quartiles are
computed from the complete unlabeled universe. Template and demand remain report
strata; they are not allowed to disappear inside an aggregate score.

Selection and holdout rule
--------------------------
E089 enumerates a fixed set of atomic role bins. Using training labels only, it
chooses at most four bins whose union covers no more than half of the complete B
universe. The exact lexicographic objective is:

  1. maximize covered training failures;
  2. minimize covered universe candidates;
  3. minimize number of selected bins;
  4. deterministic role identity.

Only after selection is frozen does E089 reveal holdout coverage.

Decision rule
-------------
STABLE_SPATIAL_HAZARD_REGIONS
  Training coverage and holdout coverage are both at least 80 percent while the
  selected union covers at most 50 percent of the B universe. Use the selected
  roles as a bounded B-bay or front-halo constructor proposal, then test the exact
  candidate-specific front rules inside it.

TRAINING_HAZARD_DOES_NOT_GENERALIZE
  Training coverage reaches 80 percent under the universe cap, but holdout
  coverage does not. Do not promote the fitted regions; the apparent pattern is
  cohort-specific.

SPATIAL_ROLES_TOO_DIFFUSE
  Even training coverage does not reach 80 percent under the cap. Retire the
  fixed-52-plus-one-replacement B geometry before another expensive master solve;
  widen pole or partition geometry.

Truth boundary
--------------
E089 is a no-solver predictive atlas on one frozen candidate language and one
temporal label split. Selected regions are constructor proposers only. They are
not cuts, do not prove any candidate feasible or infeasible, and do not establish
that future search distributions match E086/E087.
