Interpretation
--------------
E011 improves both scalar resources it was asked to protect or purchase:

  free adjacency       +29
  meaningful patterns  +30
  free cells           +20
  power poles           -5

Yet exact component mismatch worsens slightly. This is not a failure of the
measurements; every commodity optimum is independently rebuilt and checked. It is
a failure of explanatory sufficiency:

  free-edge quantity
  selected-pattern quantity
  optional-obstacle quantity

still do not encode where each commodity's selected sources and sinks land among
the components.

The remaining component rule depends jointly on placement and binding decisions.
It can no longer be represented faithfully by another scalar placement proxy
without repeatedly rediscovering counterexamples.

Inner-partition conclusion
--------------------------
The new interface partition remains valid:

  static port domains
    -> duplicate-safe, component-aware binding
    -> exact routing

But the constructive producer feeding this interface has reached a new thick
region. Local pose movement and component-aware terminal selection share variables
and mutually prune each other. For the next step they should cohabit a smaller
joint neighborhood model rather than communicate through another one-number
surrogate.

This does not mean rebuilding one global monolithic placement-routing model. The
appropriate object is local and contextual:

  most of E009 fixed
  a bounded set of movable pose units / power poles
  exact binding variables for the affected facilities and commodities
  component-support constraints inside the neighborhood or against a frozen
  outside world

Exact routing still remains downstream.

Outer-loop conclusion

The outer loop has now done more than accumulate cuts. It has:

  discovered two useful positive resources

  found the point where those abstractions cease to explain the target condition

That representation change is the result of this round. Continuing to tune scalar

Truth boundary
--------------
The trajectory covers four fixed placements and does not prove future local joint
models feasible or scalar proxies universally useless. Free adjacency and selected
pattern survival remain useful construction features; they are no longer
sufficient as the sole decision language for crossing component-aware binding.

Decision effect
---------------
Stop scalar alternation for now. Build a bounded exact local placement-binding
model around the best current candidate, E009, using residual commodity mismatch
to choose the movable neighborhood. Preserve the positive resource scores as
hints or secondary objectives, not as substitutes for exact component support.
