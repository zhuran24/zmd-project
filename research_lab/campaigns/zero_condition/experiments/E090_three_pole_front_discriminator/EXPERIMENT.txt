EXPERIMENT E090: THREE-POLE NATIVE-FRONT DISCRIMINATOR FOR THE Y=41 PARTITION
================================================================================

Question
--------
Is the current native-front wall caused mainly by the rigid 52-current-poles +
one-replacement language, or does it survive the smallest already evidenced
multi-pole repair budget?

Context and budget
------------------
E084 has an exact body/pole/power endpoint for the same y=41 operation partition
that retains 50 of the 53 current poles and relocates exactly three. E090 therefore
uses the nested pole language:

  select exactly 53 legal poles
  retain at least 50 current pole poses
  hence relocate at most 3 poles

The number three is not an arbitrary escalation. It is the first nontrivial
multi-pole budget already known to admit a complete body/pole/power geometry in
this partition.

Method
------
1. Pin E084's complete integrated body/pole/front producer, the exact three-pole
   body/power hint, E089's decision, E081/E069/E079 context, candidate pools,
   mandatory instances, strict rules and operation-profile source by SHA-256.

2. Derive a no-overwrite producer that keeps the original complete front model:

     219 exact module/template bodies
     53 selected poles
     full 47-state boundary disjunction
     y=41 module separation
     body/pole/boundary non-overlap
     manufacturing power
     all same-footprint native modes
     exact module/template/input-count/output-count class totals
     two stable E078 body identities

   The only feasible-set change is the explicit `retained current poles >= 50`
   bound. The original all-pole model is thereby restricted, while the retired
   52+1 model is strictly widened.

3. Remove the retained-body optimization and solve pure feasibility, stopping at
   the first incumbent. Seed body, pole and boundary variables from E084's exact
   three-pole power endpoint. The hint is not evidence and may be repaired or
   rejected.

4. If the class-level front model finds an incumbent, deterministically materialize
   exact named operations. Operations within each class share module, template and
   front needs; the two stable bodies are assigned their required named operations
   first, and the remaining exact multiplicities are assigned to the remaining
   selected rows. Independently replay body/pole/boundary geometry, power, pole
   budget, front capacity and named operation counts.

Decision rule
-------------
FRONT_OPERATION_FEASIBLE
  Pole rigidity is a live causal explanation. Keep y=41 and send the materialized
  witness to terminal uniqueness, generic I/O and component-aware binding.

MASTER_INFEASIBLE
  Close the at-most-three-pole front language for y=41. Move the identical
  consumer to the next E081 Pareto partition/seam. This does not refute y=41 in a
  wider pole language.

UNKNOWN without incumbent
  Preserve censorship. Do not rerun the same instrument as another vote. Move the
  identical three-pole consumer to the next E081 Pareto partition as an instrument
  control; only an asymmetric positive can distinguish the partition hypothesis.

Truth boundary
--------------
E090 concerns native-front capacity and exact named operation counts only. It does
not establish duplicate-terminal uniqueness, generic source/sink binding,
component compatibility, routing, throughput, a whole-layout witness, U/L,
certification, production, publication or pruning authority.
