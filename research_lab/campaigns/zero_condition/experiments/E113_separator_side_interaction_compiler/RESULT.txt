What was compiled
-----------------
E113 does not select one E112 separator witness and pretend that its geometry is
canonical. It reconstructs all 1,205 E103 unary-live candidates and compiles the
complete static interaction language for every candidate body and every legal
same-footprint pose/class row.

For each candidate partition it records:

  every cross-side body/body conflict edge
  every input/output native-front row whose cells can be occupied by opposite-
    side bodies
  the exact coefficient of each opposite body variable, including coefficients
    greater than one when one body covers multiple front cells
  stable source identity by global row handle, body digest, template, source
    group, pose index and B class key

The persisted selected interface contains 7,325 body-conflict edges and 2,040
nonempty coefficient buckets covering all 4,282 cross-active directional
mode/class rows. Rows with no opposite-side coefficient remain internal to their
own module and are counted separately.

`low | separator+high` is strictly better on all four metrics than both

the frontier is totally ordered under the preregistered metrics.

Why separator belongs with high
-------------------------------
The earlier three-module picture made the separator appear to be a standalone
bridge. The exact interaction graph shows a more useful computational identity:
all 154 separator candidates participate in body conflicts with the high domain,
and separator/high front coupling is dense. Putting them into one 393-candidate
cap internalizes those interactions.

Across the selected external interface:

  low candidates with cross-body conflicts          166 / 812
  cap candidates with cross-body conflicts           154 / 393
  low candidates with cross-front interaction        201 / 812
  cap candidates with cross-front interaction        183 / 393

The 239 original high candidates have no body overlap with low; all 7,325
cross-body edges are carried by separator candidates. High still participates in
front coupling, which is preserved in the cap-to-low coefficient buckets.

What changed
------------
The live architecture is no longer:

  low + separator + high as three symmetric modules

and it is not the failed E104 product that deleted separator bodies. It is:

This internalizes the densest coupling while retaining every E103 live candidate.
The full 1,205-candidate monolith is replaced by two modules and a frozen,
inspectable interface rather than by an unsupported independence assumption.
