EXPERIMENT E015: shared-binding component gradient
=================================================

Question
--------
Under one common binding selection, which of E014's 138 domain-valid unary
placement repairs minimizes the exact all-commodity component mismatch, and what
second placement literal is implicated by the best residual boundary?

Why the shared objective is necessary
-------------------------------------
E010's total 196 is the sum of 19 independently optimized commodity minima. Those
minima need not be simultaneously attainable by one binding selection. A joint
placement-binding neighborhood must be scored by one common binding, otherwise
its gradient can point toward mutually incompatible terminal choices.

E015 first solves the unchanged E009 layout with one objective:

  minimize sum over every routed commodity and free component of
           XOR(source-present-in-component, sink-present-in-component)

All binding decisions, generic assignments, static front filtering, and terminal
uniqueness are shared. It then applies the identical model to every E014 candidate
that has no empty front-filtered owner domain.

Frozen candidate surface
------------------------
E014 supplies 138 domain-valid one-literal repairs. Candidate solutions are
reconstructed from the frozen E009 assignment, the E014 arm target, and the
candidate pose. Every reconstruction must match the candidate solution digest
recorded by E014.

Validation
----------
Every OPTIMAL solution is checked against ordinary routing precheck:

  each commodity has at least one selected source and sink
  objective value equals the mismatch components extracted from selected ports
  the set of positive-mismatch commodities equals the precheck disconnected set

The E009 shared optimum is also compared with E010's per-commodity lower bounds.

Competing readings
-------------------
A. A unary placement repair reduces the shared optimum. Its moved literal is the
   first member of the joint neighborhood; the best residual-boundary partner
   supplies the second.

B. No unary repair improves the shared optimum, but different repairs shift the
   per-commodity mismatch vector. The first pair should combine complementary
   gradients rather than merely the two largest boundary-coverage literals.

C. Some candidate reaches zero shared mismatch. It immediately enters exact
   routing and supersedes pair construction.

Truth boundary
--------------
The shared mismatch objective is a necessary coarse-topology condition, not exact
routing. A lower objective does not prove that a pair containing that move can
reach zero, and an OPTIMAL fixed-layout result is not a global no-ghost theorem.

Decision effect
---------------
A zero candidate goes to routing. Otherwise the exact joint gradient and residual
boundary coverage freeze the smallest evidence-derived pair/portfolio for E016.

Result
------
Completed with verdict UNARY_SHARED_MISMATCH_IMPROVEMENT. E009's exact shared
optimum is 200. The best unary move, crusher_sandleaf pose 2186 to 15830,
reaches 186. The optimum face has a four-unit exchange only between
blue_iron_ore and source_ore. Three protocol-core candidates are reclassified as
aggregate generic-output capacity failures before component support. See
RESULT.txt.
