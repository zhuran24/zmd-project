EXPERIMENT E108: NESTED TEMPLATE-PROJECTION ATLAS
=================================================

Question
--------
Which lower/upper template splits remain possible after projecting the reserved-
y60 constructor onto three exact finite languages: body/power, complete lower
native-front feasibility, and complete upper native-front feasibility?

Why this experiment
-------------------
E105-E107 learn five template-split nogoods one proposal at a time. The interface
itself has only three integers. Continuing serially would ignore the finite basis
already exposed. E108 enumerates the three relevant projection sets and intersects
them before any further class-allocation solve.

Finite coordinates
------------------
A split is represented by the upper count vector

  (manufacturing_3x3, manufacturing_5x5, manufacturing_6x4)

with bounds 0..10, 0..6 and 0..10. The lower vector is its complement to global
high totals 10/6/10. There are at most 847 raw vectors.

Method
------
1. Reconstruct E104's source-stable 1,010-candidate lower/upper product. Build a
   body/power-only model selecting exactly 26 bodies, global template totals
   10/6/10, both stable E078 bodies, fixed E092 power and body non-overlap. Expose
   the three upper template counts as IntVars.

2. Build one generalized complete native-front model for each nested side. Each
   keeps all side candidates, same-footprint native modes, free eight-coordinate
   class allocation, fixed power, body non-overlap and front capacity. Replace
   fixed template totals with three bounded template-count IntVars, equating each
   to both selected bodies and the sum of class allocations of that template.
   Stable E078 bodies remain required wherever they physically live.

3. Enumerate distinct count vectors by solving for one witness, storing a compact
   exact witness, and adding `AddForbiddenAssignments` only on the three count
   IntVars. Enumeration is complete only when the next solve is exact
   INFEASIBLE. UNKNOWN, wall budget or vector cap leaves that projection censored.

4. If all three projections complete, compute

  body_feasible
    intersection upper_front_feasible
    intersection complement(lower_front_feasible).

   This is an exact template-level survivor atlas. It is necessary but not
   sufficient for compatible eight-class allocation across the two sides.

Predeclared decisions
---------------------
Exact empty intersection
  Close manufacturing-free y60 at the native-front template-projection layer and
  restore E103's explicit y59 separator.

Exact nonempty intersection
  Continue only the surviving split vectors through class-allocation handshake.

Any censored projection
  Preserve all enumerated witnesses and the unresolved projection status. Do not
  treat a partial intersection as complete or learn absent vectors as nogoods.

Truth boundary
--------------
Projection feasibility is exact only for its named layer. Even a vector present
in all three sets may fail class-allocation compatibility. No terminal uniqueness,
binding, routing, throughput, certification or U/L effect follows.
