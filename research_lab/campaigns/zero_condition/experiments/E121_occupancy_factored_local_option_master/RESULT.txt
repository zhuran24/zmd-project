Exact algebraic factorization
-----------------------------
E120 expanded every option's body blockers directly. E121 creates one Boolean
`occupied[c]` for each relevant in-grid, non-fixed front cell and links it to the
selected body variables covering that cell:

  occupied[c] = sum(selected[b] for b covering c).

E117's body non-overlap constraints already make the right side at most one, so
this equality is exact. Every option input/output row then uses at most one
occupancy term per front-cell occurrence. A repeated front cell would repeat the
same occupancy term and preserve multiplicity; the current language has no such
duplicates.
