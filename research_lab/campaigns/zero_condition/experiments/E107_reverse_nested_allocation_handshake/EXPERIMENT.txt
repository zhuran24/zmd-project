EXPERIMENT E107: REVERSE NESTED ALLOCATION HANDSHAKE
====================================================

Question
--------
Inside E106's open split, can the lower side propose only allocations it can
actually realize, so that the smaller upper side becomes the exact consumer?

Why this experiment
-------------------
For split lower 5/4/9 plus upper 5/2/1, E106 lets upper propose three feasible
class vectors; the lower side rejects all three exact complements. The proposal
direction is therefore mispriced: it cheaply emits vectors precisely where the
expensive side is blind. Reversing the handshake makes every proposal lower-
feasible by construction and asks the 198-candidate upper side only whether its
exact complement exists.

Method
------
1. Pin E106's result, independent check, split store and open body/template
   proposal. Reconstruct E104's source-stable 1,010-candidate lower/upper product.
   Freeze only the template split:

     lower  5 / 4 / 9
     upper  5 / 2 / 1

   The E106 body witness remains a hint, not a fixed geometry.

2. Build the complete lower native-front model with free eight-coordinate class
   allocation. E106's three upper vectors need no extra constraint: their exact
   lower complements are already proved absent from this model. Let lower propose
   a vector it can realize.

3. Compute the exact upper complement and solve the complete upper side. On upper
   INFEASIBLE, forbid only the exact lower proposer vector and continue. UNKNOWN
   creates no nogood. The frozen limit is three new lower vectors.

4. A paired high allocation is sent to E101's unchanged x42-low complement. A
   full positive is materialized and replayed over all 219 manufacturing bodies.

Predeclared decisions
---------------------
Full positive
  Advance to terminal uniqueness, generic I/O and component-aware binding.

Upper INFEASIBLE
  Add only the exact lower allocation vector to the reverse-direction store and
  continue within the frozen limit.

Lower proposer INFEASIBLE after learned upper negatives
  Do not immediately promote the split. Independently replay every lower-feasible
  vector / upper-infeasible complement and the final exhausted lower model; only
  that complete chain may close upper 5/2/1 as a template-split nogood.

Any UNKNOWN
  Stop on the exact side/allocation context with no learned rule.

Iteration limit
  Persist the bidirectional allocation store and continue from it; do not reopen
  the body frontier or rerun a joint high model.

Truth boundary
--------------
A reverse-direction nogood rejects one exact lower vector, equivalently one exact
upper complement, inside split 5/2/1. It does not reject the split unless the full
feasible lower projection is exhausted and replayed. No routing, throughput,
certification or U/L effect.
