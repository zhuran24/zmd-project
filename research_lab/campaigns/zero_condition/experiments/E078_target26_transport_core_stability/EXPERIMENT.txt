EXPERIMENT E078: TARGET-26 SEMANTIC TRANSPORT-CORE STABILITY
============================================================

Question
--------
Is E074's two-row target-26 semantic transport core an artifact of one jointly
chosen baseline endpoint, or is the same stable-body rewrite valid and optimal
throughout the complete target-26 parent face on E069's fixed geometry?

Why this experiment
-------------------
The corrected cut/transport direction makes small semantic-core stability across
nearby contexts its own first go/no-go. E074 proved a global paired minimum of two
changed rows, but the baseline and synthetic-zero assignments were optimized
together. That alone does not show that an arbitrary parent endpoint can consume
the same core.

Scope
-----
The object universe is one frozen E069 occupied geometry and its 38 stable 6x4
body rows. Component and destination integers are local handles only inside this
frozen context. Durable conclusions are carried by body digests, source-instance
identity, target free-cell-set digest, and qiaoyu slot witnesses.

Method
------
1. Reconstruct the source-stable E069 parent and E074's target-26 witness under a
   source-isolated Python cache.
2. Reproduce the target-26 baseline face and the E074 paired minimum.
3. Ask whether any target-26 parent assignment can change either of the two core
   rows from its E074 baseline option.
4. Substitute the fixed E074 zero options on those two rows while leaving every
   other parent row arbitrary, then search the complete parent face for any fine
   or qiaoyu mismatch after the rewrite.
5. Exhaust all 168 one-option alternatives to the E074 baseline. Retain only
   alternatives that remain in the exact target-26 parent face; for every such
   baseline independently minimize the synthetic-zero row distance and refute an
   optimum whose support differs from the two stable core bodies.
6. Persist a typed atlas with local handles and stable body identities separated.

Decision rule
-------------
All of the following close:

  paired global minimum              2
  either core row changed in parent  INFEASIBLE
  universal rewrite counterexample   INFEASIBLE
  every valid one-option neighbor    OPTIMAL at 2 on the same stable bodies

Then the target-26 semantic core passes the fixed-geometry stability gate. Keep
it as a bounded constructive object and test the same stable physical witnesses
under a neighboring geometry with unique remapping.

Any counterexample, core growth, alternate optimum support, or UNKNOWN
  Do not promote the two-row support as stable. Record the exact drift and narrow
  or retire the semantic-core shortcut.

Truth boundary
--------------
This experiment proves nothing about physical realization of the synthetic
filling option, body relocation, full binding, routing, throughput, a whole
layout, certification, U/L, owner authority, or publication. Passing the fixed
E069 face is not yet cross-geometry stability.

Result
------
Not run yet.
