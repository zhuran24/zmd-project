Question
--------
E074 returned one minimum two-row target-26 rewrite on E069's frozen occupied
geometry. Two distinct questions must be separated:

1. Is that concrete two-row rewrite a stable constructive specification across

2. Is it the unique minimum two-row transport core?

transport-core direction. It does not test cross-geometry stability or physical
realizability.

Decision logic
--------------
Reference rewrite stable
  All parent-face neighbors retain an OPTIMAL two-row reference rewrite; no
  parent changes either reference row; and no whole-face rewrite counterexample
  exists. Keep the rewrite as a constructive specification and test physical and
  cross-geometry realization.

Minimum core unique
  In addition, no optimum of equal size may use another support or synthetic
  destination. Only this stronger result would license wording such as "the
  unique minimum core".

Alternative optimum found
  Keep the reference rewrite if stable, but explicitly record that the minimum
  core is nonunique. Do not turn one deterministic materialization into a theorem
  about all optima.

Any UNKNOWN, identity drift, malformed output, failed reference witness, or
whole-face counterexample
  No stability verdict; preserve the nonterminal or counterexample.
