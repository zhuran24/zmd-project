EXPERIMENT E073: DIRECT OWNER-BRIDGE REPLAY
===========================================

Question
--------
Does E072's best one-owner, destination-preserving component bridge actually
realize the missing half of its real filling-capsule signature when that owner is
moved through every legal fixed-outside pose alternative?

Why this experiment
-------------------
E072 builds a causal component-owner graph and finds at least one shortest path
with one occupied owner while preserving the body that supplies the real half-
signature. A graph edge is not a geometric theorem: moving the owner may fail to
merge the intended components, destroy power, block the original terminal cells,
or make the rest of the 38-body operation assignment infeasible.

E073 therefore consumes exactly one deterministic direct bridge and tests it in
the actual corrected terminal-signature consumer. It does not scan unrelated
owners.

Method
------
1. Freeze E069, E071, and E072 result objects and their producing runners. Select
   the first E072 proposal satisfying:

     owner_count = 1
     moves_destination_body = false
     exactly one path owner

   using E072's already frozen ranking.
2. Reconstruct the E069 parent and verify the selected destination's real filling
   option and exact terminal-cell variant.
3. Enumerate every placement- and power-valid alternative of the single path
   owner with the rest of the factory fixed. Quotient byte-identical candidate
   solutions.
4. For every candidate:
   * rebuild free components and generic qiaoyu sink slots;
   * regenerate all manufacturing-6x4 native signatures;
   * map the original half-signature's terminal cells into the new component
     partition;
   * require those exact active cells to remain free;
   * locate and force the same real filling operation/mode/signature at the same
     destination;
   * solve exact fine-component equality and qiaoyu source/sink equality;
   * also solve the unforced corrected two-zero model to detect a different
     mechanism.
5. Materialize deterministic forced-zero or unforced-zero candidate layouts for
   the next full conditional binding consumer.

Decision rule
-------------
Any forced-real-option zero
  The direct owner move realizes the proposed bridge while preserving the real
  half-signature. Send the smallest candidates to the full conditional binding
  consumer immediately.

No forced zero, but an unforced zero
  The geometry is useful through a different terminal relation. Materialize it
  and inspect the selected signature before choosing the next rule.

All alternatives terminal and no zero
  Retire this direct bridge relation and test the next E072 direct proposal, or
  the shortest multi-owner relation if no direct proposal remains.

Any UNKNOWN
  Preserve only nonterminal alternatives. Do not interpret budget exhaustion as
  bridge failure.

Truth boundary
--------------
E073 moves one selected owner in E069's fixed first-zero geometry and evaluates a
corrected terminal-signature relaxation. A terminal zero is proposal evidence for
full binding, not a full binding, routing, throughput, or whole-layout witness.
