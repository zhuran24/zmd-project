E082 HISTORICAL BAY-COUNT ALIGNMENT FRONTIER
============================================

Status
------
Completed in run-001 as the cheapest geometry-aware discriminator after E080.
Research-only. No certification, U/L, production, owner, pruning, or publication
effect.

Question
--------
E080 selected a connected, area-balanced, two-commodity operation-type partition
using dependency and seam metrics only. Does that partition align with the
17-bay template-count plan of the earlier routing-aware constructor, or does a
nearby connected partition preserve a comparably small seam while requiring far
fewer bays to mix the two modules?

This experiment measures **bay-count alignment**, not physical bay feasibility.
The historical plan is explicitly `SEARCH_PLAN_ONLY`; some planned rows still
owed local construction and replay. Its 17 template-capacity vectors are used as
an optimistic geometric scaffold and nothing more.

Object space
------------
Inputs are pinned by SHA-256:

- E080 runner, RESULT.json, PARTITION_FRONTIER.json and SEAM_CONTRACT.json;
- the historical count-closure plan `count_closure_plan_20260720_v3.json`;
- the historical connected-bay assembler and composition sources that define the
  intended 17-bay / 219-manufacturing-instance context.

E082 reconstructs all 53 E080 partitions whose two induced operation graphs are
connected. All mandatory copies of one operation type remain in the same module,
as in E080.

Bay-count model
---------------
For each connected partition, let its module-A template demand be:

  (# manufacturing_3x3, # manufacturing_5x5, # manufacturing_6x4).

For every historical bay b and template t, choose an integer `a[b,t]` between 0
and that bay's planned capacity. The module-A totals must match exactly; module B
receives the complement.

A bay is:

- full A when every planned slot is assigned to A;
- full B when every planned slot is assigned to B;
- mixed otherwise.

CP-SAT minimizes the number of mixed bays and proves the optimum for every one of
the 53 connected partitions. A complete 2^17 whole-bay subset table independently
identifies every zero-mixed target. The deterministic secondary solution is only
one count allocation; it is not claimed unique.

Metrics and selection
---------------------
E082 records:

- minimum mixed-bay count and one exact allocation;
- whether a whole-bay subset realizes the target;
- seam direction count, commodity count, dependency edges and slot incidences;
- body-area and instance-count imbalance;
- the complete connected and one-third/two-thirds-balanced Pareto frontiers.

The geometry-aware construction seed is selected only inside the same
one-third/two-thirds area band used by E080, under the declared lexicographic
research criterion:

  minimum mixed bays
  -> minimum seam directions
  -> minimum seam commodities
  -> minimum consumer input-slot incidence
  -> minimum dependency edges
  -> minimum area imbalance
  -> minimum instance imbalance
  -> stable operation-type order

E080's original seam-first seed remains an explicit control. The new criterion
is a constructor choice, never WLOG.

Decision rule
-------------
A balanced connected candidate uses fewer mixed bays than E080's selected seed
without increasing seam commodity count
  Reorder the constructor frontier. Build the geometry-aware seed first and keep
  the old seam-first seed as a control.

No such candidate exists
  Keep E080's seed and proceed to the bounded geometric constructor.

Any UNKNOWN, input drift, count mismatch, or failure to reproduce E080's 53
connected partitions
  No frontier verdict.

Truth boundary
--------------
Minimum mixed-bay count is exact only in the historical **template-count** plan.
It ignores actual bay coordinates, mode/front domains, operation-specific dock
compatibility, power, non-overlap across the composed layout, generic binding,
routing and throughput. Because arbitrary same-template slots may be assigned to
A or B, it is an optimistic lower bound on mixed physical bays in any faithful
operation-aware realization.
