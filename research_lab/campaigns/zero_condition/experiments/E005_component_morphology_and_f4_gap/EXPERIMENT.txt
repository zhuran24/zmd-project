EXPERIMENT E005: component morphology and the F4 consumption gap
=================================================================

Question
--------
Why did a project that already had a family named component_reach fail to turn
years of disconnected-routing evidence into a changed placement/binding
interface?

Do A1 and its one-cut E001 replacement expose a small reusable component
morphology, or only superficially similar shapes whose causal obstacle semantics
are too diverse for a sound local family?

Cheapest faithful test
----------------------
E005 has two independent halves.

The morphology half reconstructs the exact free-cell components of A1 and E001,
then measures:

  exact cell-set persistence across the one-pole repair
  translation-normalized free-cell shape families
  translation-normalized boundary-cell semantic families
  translation-normalized owner/pose semantic families
  line-component and small-component prevalence
  coverage of E004 optimum mismatch observations

The architecture half pins and inspects the actual F4 oracle, validator, typed
registry, lifecycle, and Benders state/attach path. It asks separately whether:

  the mathematical disconnection rule exists
  the live state supplies the rule's required objects
  the live orchestrator invokes the family
  the family carries a causal placement object
  a compiler can lower the result into the master
  the object matches selectable multi-terminal binding rather than one fixed pair

Competing readings
------------------
A. A few full semantic boundary motifs cover most mismatch components, and F4
   already has a usable causal lowering. The right next move is to enable or
   repair the existing family.

B. Free-cell shapes compress strongly but full causal boundary semantics do not;
   F4 proves only fixed-state endpoint disconnection and has no live consumer.
   Shape-only point cuts would be unsound or too numerous. The next representation
   should be a positive permeability objective/restriction, while local causal
   families remain secondary products when a small exact boundary repeats.

C. Even free-cell shapes form a broad long tail. Local family compilation has no
   leverage and the project should move directly to a positive global topology
   representation.

Truth boundary
--------------
Morphology counts are exact for the two fixed layouts. An E004 mismatch component
is counted once for each optimum commodity witness in which it appears. Full
boundary semantic signatures are diagnostic equivalence classes, not proved
logical equivalence classes for future layouts.

The architecture audit proves what the pinned code can and cannot consume. It
does not prove that every possible future F4 redesign is inadequate.

Decision effect
---------------
High shape compression plus low causal compression and a missing F4 consumer
selects a positive, solver-chosen permeability proxy as the next constructive
probe. High full-semantic compression would instead select a parametric local
separator compiler. A live complete F4 path would redirect effort to repairing
that path rather than creating a parallel mechanism.
