EXPERIMENT E006: positive free-adjacency master objective
========================================================

Question
--------
Can the unchanged no-ghost placement-plus-power master, while retaining the
proved E001 pocket cut, choose a substantially more permeable geometry when it is
asked to maximize adjacent pairs of free cells?

Why this objective
------------------
A1 and E001 each leave exactly 1,100 free cells, but only 1,080/1,081 adjacent
free-cell edges. E005 shows that 80.4% of E001 components are one-cell-wide lines,
95.9% have size at most 20, and 95 of A1's 98 components survive the first local
repair unchanged.

Maximizing free-free adjacency rewards the master for consolidating free space
without prescribing a shelf, frame, corridor location, component count, or
backbone shape. It is a sufficient construction heuristic, not a necessary
condition and not a cut.

Change under test
-----------------
After building the ordinary pose-bool no-ghost master and attaching exactly the
already checked E001 four-literal cut:

  for each grid cell, occupancy is the existing at-most-one sum of pose literals;
  for each horizontal or vertical neighboring cell pair, add one Boolean that is
  exactly one iff both cells are free;
  maximize the sum of the 9,660 neighboring-pair Booleans.

The E001 replacement is supplied as a known feasible hint. No fixed corridor or
additional placement restriction is introduced.

Competing readings
------------------
A. The solver finds a candidate with a large adjacency improvement and sharply
   fewer components. The master can respond to a positive permeability signal;
   immediately test component-aware binding and exact routing.

B. Adjacency improves, but component count or mismatch remains poor. Pairwise
   adjacency is too local; the next positive statistic must expose articulation,
   main-component terminal support, or an explicit variable backbone.

C. The solver cannot improve the E001 incumbent within the bounded run. This is
   UNKNOWN about the global objective, but it shows the current encoding/search
   cannot cheaply consume this proxy. Try a staged or local-repair formulation,
   not a stronger claim.

D. The objective produces a candidate that regresses the proved pocket cut,
   placement/power semantics, or recomputed objective identity. This is execution
   failure.

Budget
------
Master wall cap: 240 seconds, 8 CP-SAT workers, automatic search, fixed seed.

The run may return FEASIBLE with an incumbent and an open objective bound. Such a
candidate is valid for downstream research, but no optimality claim is made.

Truth boundary
--------------
A candidate proves only placement plus power under the ordinary no-ghost master,
the E001 cut, and the stated objective encoding. Free adjacency is a heuristic
quality measure. It does not imply component-aware binding, routing, throughput,
or a complete layout.

Decision effect
---------------
A materially better geometry is immediately sent to the interface ladder. A
small or absent improvement keeps the positive-permeability direction but changes
the representation or search decomposition. No result licenses a production
objective or necessary cut.

Result
------
Completed with a research-only FEASIBLE candidate. The ordinary exact-master
solve path spent its budget in presolve and returned UNKNOWN without registering
the known-feasible hint. A presolve-disabled, complete-hint LNS recovery retained
the same objective semantics and found objective 1166 versus E001's 1081,
reducing free components from 97 to 91 and selected power poles from 64 to 54.
See RESULT.txt for identities, execution-container interpretation, and truth
boundary.
