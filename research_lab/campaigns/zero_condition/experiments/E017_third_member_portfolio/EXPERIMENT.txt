EXPERIMENT E017: four-arm third-member portfolio
===============================================

Question
--------
With E016's successful pair fixed, which of four evidence-selected current
literals composes as a third placement move, and can any exact third move drive
the shared component mismatch from 178 to zero?

The fixed pair is:

  crusher_sandleaf pose 2186 -> 15830

Third-member portfolio

  crusher_source pose 6073            high residual coverage
  refinery_blue_iron pose 5279        strong unary gradient family
  refinery_blue_iron pose 5343        strong unary gradient family

Each arm re-enumerates all exact alternatives after both previous moves. No old
alternative list is reused across changed geometry.

Interface order
---------------
Every candidate preserves mandatory cardinality, fixed outside geometry,
non-overlap, 53 poles, and exact power. It is then evaluated by:

  owner-domain filtering
  aggregate/shared base binding and terminal uniqueness
  one exact all-commodity shared mismatch objective
  exact routing only at mismatch zero

Competing readings
-------------------
A. One third member reduces the exact objective below 178. The successful triple
   extends the evidence-derived portfolio.

B. One third member reaches zero and enters exact routing.

C. None improves 178. Sequential causal assembly has saturated; the next model
   must release multiple pose and binding decisions simultaneously.

Truth boundary
--------------
This experiment is exhaustive only for four chosen current literals, one carrier
per literal, and fixed outside geometry. A positive objective is not global
infeasibility. Residual coverage and unary gradients are neighborhood selectors,
not sound cuts.

Decision effect
---------------
A zero candidate goes to routing. A positive improvement freezes the best triple
and derives a fourth member. No improvement ends sequential portfolio growth.
