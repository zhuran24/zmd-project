EXPERIMENT E025: residual action surface of the live 168/173 beam
================================================================

Question
--------
After the class-4-specific E024 move lowers one branch from shared mismatch 173
to 168, does that branch expose a materially different residual action surface
from the three retained objective-173 parents, and which non-retired action should
be tested next?

Why this experiment is necessary
--------------------------------
The outer loop now carries four live states rather than one canonical incumbent:

  one objective-168 E024 child
  three objective-173 E021 parents

E022 showed that residual boundary coverage proposes useful action portfolios but
is not itself continuation value. E023 then rejected the common grinder action
7754 for a second time. A fresh surface calculation must therefore preserve both
state identity and action history. Recomputing a high coverage score is not
permission to resurrect a repeatedly negative action.

Method
------
The E024 placement and its exact shared-binding endpoint are frozen by SHA-256.
The endpoint is not re-solved. Its selected mismatch components are converted to
boundary observations and transported to the real pose-bool consumer literals by
the corrected E013 transport.

The new objective-168 surface is compared with the frozen E022 surfaces for live
classes 1, 2, and 3. The comparison records:

  complete ranking identity
  top-10 and top-20 overlap
  common literal coverage across all four states
  exact maximum boundary coverage at budgets 1, 2, and 4
  common and state-specific leaders

The action ledger then excludes grinder_fine_buckwheat pose 7754, which worsened
all twelve E019 parents and all four E023 parents by exactly two. The next common
and objective-168-specific candidates are selected only after this exclusion.

Competing readings
------------------
A. The 168 surface has a new leader or weak overlap with all 173 surfaces. Expand
   the 168 branch through its state-specific action while retaining the parents.

B. The four states still share a strong non-retired portfolio. Replay the best
   common action across all four to measure continuation-value invariance.

C. No credible unary action remains after the action ledger is applied. Stop
   unary expansion and build a simultaneous two-pose or pose-binding neighborhood.

Truth boundary
--------------
Boundary coverage says only that changing a literal could alter an observed
mismatch component. It is not a repair theorem, placement cut, routing proof, or
whole-layout result. Historical negative response is contextual evidence for the
current unary ledger, not a global theorem that the pose can never participate in
a successful joint move.

Result
------
Completed with verdict LIVE_BEAM_RESIDUAL_SURFACE_SHARED_PORTFOLIO. The
objective-168 endpoint was recovered exactly after parallel replay first returned
a different optimum. The raw coverage leader remains the already-retired grinder
pose 7754; after applying the action ledger, packaging_battery pose 6189 is the
strongest common action and crusher_sandleaf pose 2155 is the leading distinct
objective-168 action. See RESULT.txt.
