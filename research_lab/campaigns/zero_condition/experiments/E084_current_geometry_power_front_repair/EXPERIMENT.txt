====================================================================

Status

the first physically checked repair surface after the 16-body retention quotient

Method
------
1. Pin every landed E084 producer script, result JSON and inherited input by
   SHA-256. Missing terminal/global binding result files are treated as absence,
   not inferred outcomes.

2. Reconstruct E081's selected context from its frontier:

     partition_90abd29523f2a0dc
     horizontal seam y=41
     module A below, module B above
     two stable E078 footprints forced onto module B

3. Without invoking an optimizer, replay each materialized positive geometry:

   * exact module/template body counts;
   * 219 manufacturing bodies and, where applicable, 53 power poles;
   * body non-overlap against protocol core, boundary bodies and poles;
   * the selected E079 boundary-state identity;
   * all body restrictions against the y=41 corridor, boundary fronts and core
     front reservation used by the producer;
   * power coverage for every manufacturing body;
   * current-footprint and current-pole retention counts;
   * stable E078 footprint remapping.

   The inherited E069 context contains one old pole on one optional protocol-core
   front cell. The disposable fixed-52-pole models inherit that overlap; they do
   not require the slot to be active. New manufacturing bodies, replacement
   poles and all-pole witnesses remain forbidden from all core-front cells.

4. For the three fixed power-optimal geometries, independently re-enumerate
   production pose-level binding patterns for every body and verify every named
   empty native-front domain.

5. Preserve producer status exactly. `OPTIMAL` is not extended past the model it
   solved; `UNKNOWN` remains censored. The two one-replacement Benders
   `MASTER_INFEASIBLE` rungs are recorded as producer conclusions with no fresh
   solver replay by the artifact checker.

Evidence hierarchy
------------------
Load-bearing and independently replayed:

  materialized geometry
  body and pole counts
  non-overlap under the producer's reservation semantics
  manufacturing power coverage
  current-footprint / current-pole retention recount
  stable E078 body presence
  named empty native-front domains on fixed witnesses

Producer status retained but not independently re-solved:

  CP-SAT optimality and objective bounds
  one-replacement Benders r31/r32 MASTER_INFEASIBLE
  all UNKNOWN runs

Not produced:

  one terminal-unique manufacturing assignment
  generic source/sink binding
  component-aware binding
  exact routing
  throughput or whole-layout validation

Decision rule
-------------
If a power-feasible fixed witness has no empty native-front body, continue to
terminal uniqueness and component-aware binding.

If every checked power optimum contains empty bodies, stop post-filtering power
optima. Make native-front feasibility part of the proposer. In the one-replacement
language, r31 and r32 may close only at their producer evidence strength; r33 is
the first unresolved rung. In the all-pole language, the integrated front model
is UNKNOWN and supplies no incumbent.

Truth boundary
--------------
E084 studies one E081 partition, one straight seam and same-template footprint
quotients. It does not establish a minimum number of moved bodies for the full
zero-condition problem. The all-pole objective values are exact only for their
respective body/pole/power models; the fixed witnesses are then independently
refuted by empty front domains. No certification, U/L, owner, production,
publication or pruning effect follows.
