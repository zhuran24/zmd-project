EXPERIMENT E056: CAUSAL-PAIR PORT-MODE FRONTIER
================================================

Question
--------
After E055 proves the selected causal relation plus fourteen-body operation
assignment is lexicographically exact at positive count 18 and total mismatch 139,
can admitted same-body port modes in that 6x4 context create a second zero without
changing occupied geometry?

Why this experiment
-------------------
The current inner hierarchy has now been measured one layer at a time:

  causal source-body/capsule-body relation  creates the first zero
  operation assignment with modes fixed    saturates at (18,139)
  admitted same-body port modes             not yet tested jointly

E056 releases only that next layer. It preserves the selected causal body
locations, keeps `fine_buckwheat_powder = 0`, fixes every non-6x4 operation and
mode, and frees operation assignment plus the eighteen already-admitted same-body
mode destinations in the merged 6x4 context.

Method
------
The materialized E055 state must first recalibrate exactly at positive count 18
and total mismatch 139.

The primary free solve minimizes positive commodity count. A separate resumable
stage fixes the exact positive optimum and minimizes total mismatch. Any terminal
winner is materialized and replayed through the fixed conditional-owner model and
production component precheck.

Decision rule
-------------
Positive optimum <= 17
  The admitted port-mode layer creates a second zero. Minimize total on that face
  and retain the exact state.

Positive optimum = 18
  The causal relation, operation assignment, and admitted port modes are jointly
  saturated at one zero. Minimize total secondarily, then select a new structured
  commodity cause or body/rescue expansion.

UNKNOWN / FEASIBLE
  Preserve the scoped status and refine the mode-search container without claiming
  saturation.

Positive optimum = 0
  Materialize and enter exact routing immediately.

Truth boundary
--------------
E056 fixes occupied geometry, the selected causal relation, and all non-6x4
operation/mode choices. It releases only the admitted mode and operation variables
inside the fourteen-body 6x4 block. It does not exhaust new body poses, additional
rescue relations, global geometry, routing, throughput, U/L, or certification.

Result
------
Completed. The E055 state recalibrates at 18/139. Releasing admitted 6x4 modes and
operation assignment proves positive-count OPTIMAL 18, so no second zero exists in
this bounded context. Secondary total minimization and the total<=138 discriminator
remain UNKNOWN; 18/139 is retained as a known feasible witness, not a proved
