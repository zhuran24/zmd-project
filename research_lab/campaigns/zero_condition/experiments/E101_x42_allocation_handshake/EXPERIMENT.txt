EXPERIMENT E101: X42 LOW/HIGH ALLOCATION HANDSHAKE
==================================================

Question
--------
Can E100's exact low/high product be solved by moving its eight class-allocation
coordinates outside the geometric side models, instead of asking one monolith to
discover geometry and allocation simultaneously?

Why this experiment
-------------------
E100 proves that, after reserving x=42, low and high module-B bodies have no
body/body or front/body coupling. Its 38k-variable monolith nevertheless returns
UNKNOWN after 8.4 million branches. The remaining bridge is explicit and only
eight-dimensional. E101 must make that bridge operational.

Method
------
1. Reconstruct the source-stable E100 candidate language from pinned source.

2. Solve a cheap body/power-only x42 model to obtain one exact low/high template
   allocation and body hint. This stage does not claim front feasibility and does
   not optimize retention.

3. Build a high-side native-front model with the body witness's exact high-side
   3x3/5x5/6x4 counts. Its eight class counts remain explicit integer variables,
   bounded by the global module-B demands. Solve for one high-side geometry,
   native modes and allocation vector.

4. Build a low-side model with the exact complementary class vector. If it is
   feasible, combine both sides, deterministically materialize named operations,
   combine with E095's frozen A witness and replay all 219 manufacturing bodies.

5. If low is exact INFEASIBLE, add the high allocation tuple as a nogood to the
   high model and continue. At most three allocation vectors are tested. UNKNOWN
   on either side stops the experiment censored; it is never converted to a
   nogood.

Budgets
-------

  body/power allocation witness       20 seconds
  each high-side proposal             25 seconds
  each low-side complement check      40 seconds
  maximum high allocation vectors      3

Predeclared decisions
---------------------
Paired positive and combined replay PASS
  Advance to terminal uniqueness, generic I/O and component-aware binding.

Body/power INFEASIBLE
  Reject reserved x=42 itself and restore the explicit x_after_41 separator.

High-side INFEASIBLE for the chosen template allocation
  Reject only that low/high template allocation and request another body-only
  allocation.

Low-side INFEASIBLE for one or more high allocations
  Preserve the allocation nogoods and continue from them in a fresh successor;
  do not reject x42 or the template allocation unless the high allocation space
  is exhaustively closed.

Any UNKNOWN
  Preserve censorship and the exact allocation/vector at which it occurred.

Truth boundary
--------------
Only a combined positive transfers to unrestricted module B. Every negative is
scoped to x42 and, where applicable, one body-only template allocation or one
class-allocation tuple. Terminal uniqueness, generic I/O, commodity binding,
component compatibility, routing, throughput, U/L, certification, owner,
production and publication remain outside.
