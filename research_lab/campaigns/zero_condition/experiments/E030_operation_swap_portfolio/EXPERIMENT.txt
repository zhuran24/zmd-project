EXPERIMENT E030: exact replay of the objective-166 operation-swap portfolio
============================================================================

Question
--------
Do occupancy-preserving operation swaps form a reusable constructive layer, or was
E028's packaging/grinder exchange an isolated local improvement?

Frozen portfolio
----------------
E029 derives twelve same-facility-type, cross-operation swaps from the fully
materialized objective-166 endpoint. Each pair preserves the occupied cell set by
exchanging the two current pose assignments. The portfolio is selected by residual
boundary union coverage with a diversity cap; coverage proposes but does not rank
continuation value.

Method
------
For every frozen swap, E030:

  verifies both current literals and represented instances;
  exchanges their pose indices and pose records exactly;
  proves the occupied cell set is unchanged;
  checks the frozen power-pole set and exact power coverage;
  applies static owner-domain and aggregate binding gates;
  solves one exact shared component-mismatch objective over all 19 commodities;
  enters exact routing only if the objective reaches zero.

All surviving child states are distinguished by placement and binding-selection
digests. The best child receives a complete materialized binding endpoint.

Competing readings
------------------
A. One or more swaps materially improve 166. Operation-to-footprint assignment is
   a standing constructive layer and the improved states remain in the beam.

B. Swaps survive static domains but are equal, harmful, or only marginal. Boundary
   coverage is insufficient; operation-assignment and binding variables must
   cohabit the next neighborhood.

C. The portfolio is entirely static-dead. The assignment layer needs static-domain
   propagation before candidate generation.

D. A swap reaches zero. It enters exact routing immediately.

Truth boundary
--------------
E030 is exhaustive only for the twelve E029 swaps under the objective-166 outside
state. It does not exhaust all 8,486 candidate exchanges, prove a necessary
assignment pattern, or establish a global no-ghost result.

Result
------
Completed with verdict OPERATION_SWAP_PORTFOLIO_MATERIAL_IMPROVEMENT. All twelve
occupancy-preserving swaps pass static domains and reach OPTIMAL shared bindings,
with objectives 163-169. The best swaps crusher_blue_iron pose 2105 with
refinery_steel pose 2947 and lowers 166 -> 163 while free space remains
byte-identical. The exact optimum face and complete binding endpoint are frozen.
See RESULT.txt.
