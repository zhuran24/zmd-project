EXPERIMENT E047: SIMULTANEOUS BODY-PAIR PROPOSER AT OBJECTIVE 144
================================================================

Question
--------
Which bounded pair of physical body relocations should replace the now-saturated
singleton-body action language at the fully materialized objective-144 endpoint?
Can two moves jointly restore domains or improve the ordinary shared component
objective enough to justify exact geometry-conditioned middle-layer revaluation?

Why this experiment
-------------------
E046 proves that the best integrated singleton response is only 145 -> 144 and
that the middle layer contributes no additional gain on that geometry. Continuing
one body at a time would become another manually scheduled unary climb.

A body pair cannot be proposed by simply combining the two lowest singleton
scores. Earlier E028 evidence shows that a move which is statically dead alone can
become meaningful when the owner whose domain it destroys is moved at the same
time. E047 therefore retains two proposal channels:

  value-composing pairs from domain-valid singleton actions
  direct rescue pairs where one singleton's empty domain names the other body

Residual boundary incidence proposes the six primary target bodies. Static
owner-domain diagnoses determine rescue relations. Exact ordinary shared binding
ranks only complete pair geometries. Ordinary pair value remains a proposal value,
not the geometry's final continuation value.

Method
------
From the E046 objective-144 assignment and binding endpoint, E047 reconstructs all
144 mismatch-component observations and current boundary literals. It selects an
exact budget-six body portfolio by maximum residual coverage and exhausts every
fixed-outside body alternative for those six targets.

For every singleton body action E047 records:

  overlap- and exact-power-validity
  ordinary shared-binding status and objective
  for PORT_DOMAIN_EMPTY actions, the exact empty owner set

A bounded action pool is formed per target:

  up to four best domain-valid body actions
  up to three statically dead actions that directly empty another selected body

Cross-target pairs are admitted when either both singleton actions are domain-
valid or one action directly names the other target as an empty owner. Each pair
is reconstructed from the unchanged parent, checked for overlap and exact power,
and then evaluated by the ordinary shared component objective. Pair families are
checkpointed independently.

At most three distinct new free-cell geometries are materialized, with target-pair
diversity preferred. A later experiment must rebuild the faithful joint port-mode,
operation-assignment, and binding middle layer on those geometries.

Decision rule
-------------
Ordinary pair objective 0
  Materialize the endpoint and enter exact routing immediately.

One or more new domain-valid pair geometries
  Freeze up to three diverse seeds and pass them to exact joint revaluation.

No domain-valid pair geometry
  Broaden the rescue relation or move to a native simultaneous geometry model;
  do not return to singleton action enumeration.

Truth boundary
--------------
E047 exhausts a finite pair portfolio derived from one exact budget-six residual
body set. It does not exhaust all body pairs, prove a necessary move, assign final
continuation value to a geometry, establish component compatibility unless the
ordinary objective is zero, enter routing, update U/L, or grant certification.

Result
------
Not run yet.
