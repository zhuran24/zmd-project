EXPERIMENT E075: TARGET-26 TWO-BODY NATIVE FRONTIER
==================================================

Question
--------
Can E074's minimum two-row target-26 assignment core be physically realized by
moving only its two stable adjacent 6x4 bodies and selecting native port modes, so
that the corrected terminal-signature model reaches zero without any synthetic
option?

Why this experiment
-------------------
E074 replaced a 103-owner geometry inventory with exact row-transport cores. Five
synthetically sufficient targets require three changed rows; target 26 requires
only two:

  body A  grinder_dense_source_001
          stable body digest da277903...
          fine-grinder output moves from component 6 to component 26

  body B  grinder_fine_buckwheat_002
          stable body digest ef71d17d...
          filling input must move from component 6 to component 26 while qiaoyu
          output remains in component 29

On E069 geometry, body A is the unique occupied owner directly adjacent to both
free components 6 and 26. Its one-body domain is only three poses; the two real
body moves make its row structurally empty. Removing both A and B from the fixed
outside occupancy exposes only nine legal 6x4 footprints, twelve ordered
non-overlapping footprint pairs, and 48 raw mode pairs. This is the complete
smallest physical context suggested by the assignment core.

Method
------
1. Pin source-stable E069, actual E070 run-004, and E074's target-26 transport
   witness.
2. Reconstruct E069 and identify A/B by stable source_instance_id and body digest.
3. Remove both current footprints from outside occupancy. Enumerate every legal
   6x4 footprint available to either body, quotient same-footprint modes, then
   enumerate all ordered non-overlapping footprint pairs. Assert the expected
   nine footprints and twelve pairs.
4. For every pair, construct a real two-body placement state using canonical pose
   representatives. Recheck mandatory/pole cardinality, no overlap, and exact
   power coverage.
5. Rebuild the free components, native same-footprint mode domains, generic qiaoyu
   sink space, and all 38 body option rows. Resolve A/B back to current destination
   rows by stable source_instance_id after body sorting; never reuse E069's parent
   destination integers.
6. Solve three native terminal-signature arms with exact operation counts and
   qiaoyu sink fixed to component 29:

   FREE
     Any native operation/mode assignment with fine source/sink equality.

   CORE_OPERATIONS
     As above, with A forced to grinder_fine_buckwheat and B forced to
     filling_capsule.

   TARGET26_SIGNATURES
     As above, with A forced to the exact native signature fine output {26} and B
     forced to the exact native signature fine input {26}, qiaoyu output {29}.

7. Materialize all terminal results and any positive assignment endpoint by stable
   body identity. If a positive exists, preserve the full native option assignment
   and the corresponding two-body placement state for the next, fuller consumer.

Decision rule
-------------
TARGET26_SIGNATURES feasible
  The synthetic target has been physically realized in the complete two-body
  context. Promote this bounded geometry plus native mode/assignment endpoint to
  the full operation-assignment/component-binding consumer.

Only FREE or CORE_OPERATIONS feasible
  Preserve the native zero but derive the exact operation/signature difference
  before entering the fuller consumer.

No native zero and every pair terminal
  Close the complete two-body geometry/mode context. Derive the next stable body
  only from the exact structural-empty or signature obstruction; do not reopen a
  broad owner scan.

Any UNKNOWN
  Preserve only the nonterminal pair/arm. Do not convert it into a negative.

Truth boundary
--------------
E075 exhausts a fixed-outside two-body geometry and native terminal-signature
context. Even a positive is not yet full named-operation assignment, component
binding, routing, throughput, a whole-layout witness, or certification.

Result
------
Not run yet.
