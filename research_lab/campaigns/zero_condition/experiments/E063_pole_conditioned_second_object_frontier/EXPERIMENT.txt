EXPERIMENT E063: POLE-CONDITIONED SECOND-OBJECT FRONTIER
========================================================

Question
--------
After fixing the smallest E062 pole near miss, which causally selected second
object, if any, removes the unmatched fine-powder component on one of its complete
objective-one face states and reaches the corrected fine/qiaoyu two-zero
terminal-signature condition?

Why this experiment
-------------------
E065's source-stable replay confirms that only five one-object states reduce the
qiaoyu-hard fine mismatch from two to one. Four are translations of the same
lower-right optional pole. The smallest move

  power_pole pose 3725 -> 3794
  p_x53_y68_o0_m_omni -> p_x54_y68_o0_m_omni

has a valid source-only-component-1 representative, but E065 also demonstrates
that other pole actions can return components 4 or 26 as equally optimal
representatives. More importantly, one returned representative is not the complete
optimum face of a fixed geometry. E063 therefore enumerates the complete
directional optimum face of the selected 3725->3794 geometry before selecting any
second object; no E062 representative is treated as canonical.

A full 4,714-by-4,714 pair product would discard the causal information. E063
conditions on this pole parent and admits a second object only when it belongs to
one of three problem-derived classes:

  * a selected owner touching any unmatched optimum-face component and at least
    one other free component;
  * any current manufacturing-6x4 body, because moving it can create a new target
    terminal signature;
  * the generic-input provider, because its pose controls the qiaoyu sink space.

Symmetric occupancy aliases are quotiented before exact evaluation.

Method
------
1. Reconstruct E055's first-zero parent and apply the exact pole move 3725->3794.
2. Rebuild the corrected generic sink space and all 38 current 6x4 native target
   signatures. Reproduce qiaoyu-hard fine mismatch one, then enumerate every
   distinct source/sink presence pattern on that exact optimum face.
3. Derive boundary owners for the union of all source-only and sink-only
   components on the complete face; retain the multi-component separators, all
   6x4 bodies, and the protocol core.
4. Enumerate every placement- and power-valid second pose change in that causal
   set. Quotient identical `(facility type, old body, new body)` geometry changes;
   all 6x4 same-body modes remain simultaneous inside the signature consumer.
5. For each distinct pair geometry, rebuild components, candidate-specific generic
   sink slots, and all 6x4 signatures. Require qiaoyu zero and minimize fine
   mismatch exactly.
6. For every objective-zero candidate, independently replay the corrected joint
   two-zero feasibility model. Materialize a small deterministic candidate set for
   full conditional-binding validation.

The scan is checkpointed in deterministic chunks. Qiaoyu-hard objective zero is
proposal evidence from a relaxation, not a complete binding witness.

Decision rule
-------------
At least one objective-zero pair
  Carry the smallest deterministic candidates into the full conditional
  port-mode/operation-assignment/binding consumer. Exact full-consumer success
  creates the second zero state; rejection refines the missing coupling.

No zero, but objective-one states remain
  This causal two-object language cannot finish the bridge. Use the residual
  presence patterns to derive a third relation or switch to the distinct E062 6x4
  near-miss parent. Do not scan the unrestricted pair product.

Any UNKNOWN
  Preserve the completed chunks and continue only nonterminal candidates.

Truth boundary
--------------
E063 fixes E055's first-zero parent, fixes one specific pole move, and changes one
additional selected object's pose inside a complete-face-derived causal set. It uses the
corrected target-signature relaxation with candidate-specific generic sink space.
It does not establish full binding feasibility, routing, U/L, or certification.
