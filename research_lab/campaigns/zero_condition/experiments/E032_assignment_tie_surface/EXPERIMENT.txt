EXPERIMENT E032: residual action surfaces of the two objective-161 assignments
============================================================================

Question
--------
Are the two distinct objective-161 3x3 operation assignments equivalent as
continuation states, or do their residual assignment surfaces justify retaining a
beam?

Why this follows E031
---------------------
E031 finds two exact objective-161 states under the same occupied geometry. One is
a pair exchange; the other is a three-way reassignment. Their placement and
binding-selection identities differ, so choosing the first merely because it was
materialized would repeat the single-line bias already exposed by the earlier
beam experiments.

Method
------
E032 reconstructs both E031 states from the frozen objective-163 parent and the
exact 3x3 neighborhood. It materializes the second binding endpoint when the
frozen selection digest can be recovered, then rebuilds each state's 161 exact
mismatch-component boundaries without changing the endpoint.

For each state it computes:

  current literal boundary-coverage ranking
  exact small-budget boundary coverage
  all current same-size cross-operation swaps
  a diversified occupancy-preserving swap portfolio

The two surfaces are compared by ranking digest, top-set overlap, common leaders,
and proposed swap identities. No swap is assigned continuation value in this
experiment.

Competing readings
------------------
A. The complete residual and swap surfaces agree. The two witnesses can be
   quotiented for the next assignment action.

B. They share a leader but differ elsewhere. Retain both until exact replay of a
   common and a branch-specific action resolves continuation value.

C. Their leaders or portfolios diverge. The objective-161 tie is a real beam split.

Truth boundary
--------------
Residual incidence proposes actions only. Equal or different proposal surfaces do
not prove equal or different exact continuation values. E032 concerns two frozen
assignment/binding endpoints under one occupied geometry and has no routing,
certification, or U/L effect.

Result
------
Completed with verdict OBJECTIVE_161_SHARED_LEADER_DISTINCT_SURFACES. The second
binding endpoint is fully materialized. The two states share their residual and
swap leaders, but only 8/10 top swaps and 10/12 portfolio actions overlap. See
RESULT.txt.
