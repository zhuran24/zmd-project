EXPERIMENT E085: FRESH FRONT-AWARE REPLAY OF THE FIRST UNRESOLVED R33 RUNG
============================================================================

Question
--------
In the bounded one-replacement language inherited from E084, can a fresh exact
run retain 186 current manufacturing footprints and move 33 while satisfying:

  * E081 partition_90abd29523f2a0dc;
  * horizontal y=41 module separation;
  * all 219 module/template body counts;
  * the complete 47-state boundary disjunction;
  * 52 inherited non-seam poles plus exactly one replacement pole;
  * manufacturing power coverage;
  * the two stable E078 body/operation identities;
  * the 34 already registered necessary body-local native-front rules;
  * exact named operation counts in the fixed-geometry checker?

Why this experiment
-------------------
E084 closed retained rungs 188 and 187 as producer MASTER_INFEASIBLE and left
186 as UNKNOWN. It also showed that optimizing body packing and power before
front viability produces endpoints with empty native-front domains. R33 is the
first unresolved state in the narrowest currently materialized front-aware
language. It is cheaper and more diagnostic than reopening all pole positions or
launching a full coordinate master.

Method
------
1. Pin the E084 front-Benders producer source, its 34-rule checkpoint, E084's
   durable snapshot/result, E081/E069/E079 geometry inputs, the candidate pool,
   and operation-profile source by SHA-256.

2. Import the frozen E084 producer without editing it. Redirect both its mutable
   checkpoint and output into a new no-overwrite E085 run directory.

3. Override only the experiment controls:

     target retained manufacturing footprints   186
     target moved manufacturing footprints       33
     maximum Benders iterations                    1
     master solve cap                            250 s

4. Preserve the producer status exactly. `UNKNOWN` and `ITERATION_LIMIT` are
   censored. A master positive must still pass the producer's exact named-
   operation checker. `MASTER_INFEASIBLE` closes only this one-replacement r33
   language.

5. Record process maximum RSS and cgroup-v2 memory snapshots before and after the
   run. Memory telemetry is diagnostic, not a proof condition.

Decision rule
-------------
FRONT_OPERATION_FEASIBLE
  Materialize the producer geometry and operation/mode assignment. Next run
  production pose-level patterns with duplicate terminal uniqueness and generic
  I/O, then component-aware binding.

MASTER_INFEASIBLE
  Close r33 inside this bounded language. Do not infer partition or seam
  infeasibility; select a deliberately wider pole/repair language.

UNKNOWN or ITERATION_LIMIT
  Preserve censorship. Do not advance the rung. The next discriminator must be
  solver-diverse replay, a larger explicit budget, or a representation repair
  justified by telemetry.

Truth boundary
--------------
E085 replays one producer model at one target rung. Its front rules are necessary
body-local capacity conditions, not full commodity-to-port binding. A positive
has no terminal-uniqueness, generic-I/O, component, routing or throughput status.
A negative is contextual to 52 inherited poles plus one replacement pole and the
y=41 partition context. No certification, U/L, owner, production, publication or
pruning effect follows.
