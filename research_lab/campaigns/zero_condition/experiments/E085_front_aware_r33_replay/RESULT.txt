Scientific reading
------------------
E085 does not close r33. It also does not justify repeating the identical model,
seed family and representation indefinitely. Compared with the known 28-GiB
coordinate-master incident, E085 stayed near a 1.85-GiB process peak and reached
the time cap without an incumbent. The next experiment should therefore change
the search instrument rather than silently lengthen the same vote.

A faithful successor may:

  * decompose or quotient the complete 47-state boundary choice while preserving
    its full disjunction;
  * switch to a feasibility-first retained-footprint objective rather than pay
    exact fixed-rung tax before any witness exists;
  * use a solver-diverse exact 0-1 formulation;
  * or apply an explicit, predeclared larger budget with different search controls.

Any positive must still materialize the 219 body rows, replacement pole,
boundary state and exact named operation/mode assignment before terminal
uniqueness and component-aware binding. Any negative remains contextual to the
one-replacement y=41 language.

Truth boundary
--------------
UNKNOWN is censorship, not infeasibility. E085 produced no layout, no front-
operation witness, no component result and no routing result. The absence of OOM
is a telemetry observation, not a theorem about the cause of search failure.
