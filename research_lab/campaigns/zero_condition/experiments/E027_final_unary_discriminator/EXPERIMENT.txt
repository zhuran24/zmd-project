EXPERIMENT E027: final serial-unary discriminator on the objective-168 leader
=============================================================================

Question
--------
Does the objective-168-specific crusher_sandleaf pose 2155 produce a material
shared component-mismatch gain, or has fixed-outside serial unary motion reached
its useful limit?

Why this action
---------------
E025 ranks pose 2155 as the strongest objective-168 action distinct from the
common packaging action. E026 then rejects that common action completely at the
static owner-domain layer. Pose 2155 therefore becomes the highest live
branch-specific unary candidate.

The action has 51 exact placement- and power-valid alternatives under the E024
leader. It is large enough to test real continuation, but still bounded enough to
exhaust without choosing a new global factory topology.

Method
------
E027 freezes the E024 objective-168 placement outside the selected
crusher_sandleaf group pose, verifies exact power coverage, enumerates all 51
alternatives, and evaluates one shared component-mismatch optimum over all 19
commodities for every base-feasible child.

The exact compact binding endpoint of the winning candidate is materialized,
including selected components, rather than leaving only a digest. A zero child is
sent directly to exact routing.

Decision rule
-------------
Improvement of at least two units
  Retain the child as a real beam advance. Recompute its residual surface before
  deciding whether any further unary step is justified.

Improvement of one unit, equality, or harm
  End serial unary expansion. The next construction object is a simultaneous
  two-pose or pose-binding neighborhood.

No base-feasible child
  Retire the action at its earliest exact rejecting layer and move directly to the
  simultaneous neighborhood.

Truth boundary
--------------
The experiment is exhaustive only for one current group pose with the outside
placement fixed. A negative result does not prove the group globally immovable or
exclude useful joint motion. A positive mismatch gain is not a routing witness.

Result
------
Completed with verdict SERIAL_UNARY_SATURATION_SIGNAL. Fifty-one exact
alternatives yield 47 empty owner domains and four shared-binding optima; the
best move lowers 168 only to 167, below the predeclared two-unit material-gain
threshold. The exact winning binding endpoint is fully materialized. Serial unary
motion stops and the next object is a simultaneous placement neighborhood. See
RESULT.txt.
