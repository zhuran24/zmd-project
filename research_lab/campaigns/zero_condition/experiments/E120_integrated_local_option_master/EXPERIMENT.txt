EXPERIMENT E120: INTEGRATED LOCAL-OPTION EXISTENCE MASTER
========================================================

Question
--------
Can the source-stable x42-high language select 26 bodies while simultaneously
witnessing at least one locally viable same-footprint mode/class option for every
selected body?

Why this experiment
-------------------
E117/E118 learn 587 sound containment cuts from 47 locally-dead geometries. E119
shows why another lazy batch is a poor bargain: E118's cuts remain structurally
novel, but exact subject novelty is only 28 percent and no new-cohort cut rejects
another E118 geometry. The cuts are repeatedly approximating one simpler
existential condition. E120 puts that condition in the proposer itself.

Exact language
--------------
Reuse E117's frozen language:

  * 1,205 E103 unary-live body candidates;
  * exact high template totals 10 / 6 / 10;
  * fixed E092 power and body non-overlap;
  * both stable E078 bodies;
  * the 25 still-open separator template vectors;
  * 9,808 syntactic local options from all same-footprint modes and compatible B
    demand classes.

No global eight-class totals are imposed at this layer.

Encoding
--------
For each body candidate b and each quotient local option o, create a Boolean
witness q[b,o]. Post:

  sum_o q[b,o] = selected[b]

so every selected body chooses exactly one local witness and unselected bodies
choose none. For each input/output side of each option, count fixed blocked cells
plus every selected body variable covering each front cell, preserving
multiplicity when one body covers several front cells. Enforce:

  blocked_count <= front_count - required_count + front_count * (1 - q[b,o]).

The option quotient may carry several class keys only when pose, fronts and
required counts are identical. This is exactly E117's local-front existential
semantics, not a class assignment.

Solver arms
-----------
Arm A uses the direct integrated model with one worker and PSEUDO_COST_SEARCH.
If censored, Arm B rebuilds the same integrated model, adds the 587 independently
replayed E117/E118 cuts only as redundant search aids, and uses multiworker
AUTOMATIC_SEARCH. The cuts are not needed for correctness and cannot change the
feasible set.

Decision rules
--------------
Integrated positive
  Independently replay local survival, then send the fixed 26-body geometry to
  E114's unchanged full high class consumer. A high positive goes to E101's
  x42-low complement and E095's 219-body replay.

Integrated exact INFEASIBLE
  Close only the source-stable x42-high local-option necessary language. Since
  full high feasibility implies local-option feasibility, retire x42 as a
  sufficient constructor.

Both arms UNKNOWN
  Preserve censorship. Do not return to containment-cut growth. The next
  discriminator must change the geometry representation or skeleton.

Truth boundary
--------------
Local-option existence is necessary but not sufficient for global class totals.
A positive is only a constructive geometry seed until the unchanged full high
consumer accepts it. No terminal uniqueness, binding, routing, throughput,
certification or U/L effect follows.
