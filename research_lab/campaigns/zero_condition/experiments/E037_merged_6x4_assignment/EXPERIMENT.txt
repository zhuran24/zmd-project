EXPERIMENT E037: MERGED TEN-FOOTPRINT 6x4 ASSIGNMENT BLOCK
=========================================================

Question
--------
Does merging the live five-footprint 6x4 block with the strongest remaining five
6x4 footprints expose cross-subset operation assignments below objective 159, or
is fixed-geometry assignment freedom saturated enough to release geometry?

Trigger
-------
E036 adds a high-coverage 5x5 block but proves the three-block optimum remains
159. Its live blocks touch 125/159 mismatch observations. Exact five-footprint
marginal coverage outside the current pose sets is +9 for 3x3, +8 for 5x5, and
+14 for 6x4.

Why merge rather than add another block
---------------------------------------
Two independent 6x4 assignment blocks would preserve an artificial partition:
operations could move inside each five-footprint subset but not across them. E037
therefore combines the existing and newly selected 6x4 footprints into one
10-footprint assignment block with one exact operation multiset.

Method
------
E037 reconstructs the materialized E036 endpoint, selects five additional current
6x4 footprints by exact marginal coverage, operation diversity, and deterministic
tie-break, then builds one model with:

  the live five-footprint 3x3 block
  the live five-footprint 5x5 block
  one merged ten-footprint 6x4 block
  conditional exact binding patterns for every operation-footprint pair
  unchanged outside binding and generic-I/O semantics
  terminal uniqueness and one shared component-mismatch objective

The E036 and E035 objective-159 states are fixed and replayed as calibration
witnesses before the free solve.

Outcome interpretation
----------------------
OPTIMAL objective 0
  Materialize the endpoint and enter exact routing immediately.

OPTIMAL objective 1..158
  Retain the merged-block witness and derive the next context from its residual.

OPTIMAL objective 159
  Fixed-geometry operation assignment is saturated across the strongest tested
  20 footprints. Stop assignment-only expansion and release a problem-derived
  geometry neighborhood together with assignment and binding.

FEASIBLE / UNKNOWN
  Record incumbent and bound only.

Truth boundary
--------------
The experiment fixes occupied geometry and every operation outside twenty
selected manufacturing footprints. It is not a global assignment optimum,
routing result, whole-layout infeasibility proof, lower bound, or certification
artifact.

Result
------
Completed. Both objective-159 calibration states replay exactly, and the free
merged-block model proves OPTIMAL 157. The best state moves operations across the
former 6x4 subset boundary and also selects the alternate 3x3 branch. See
