EXPERIMENT E004: minimum component-mismatch atlas
=================================================

Question
--------
Is E002's 19/19 singleton result a faithful consequence of the production
routing-precheck condition, or did the hard component compiler accidentally make
binding stricter than the precheck?

If the result is faithful, how many source/sink component mismatches are
unavoidable for each commodity, and how large are the occupied obstacle
boundaries around the mismatched components selected by an optimum binding?

Why this test comes next
------------------------
E002 proved, inside its guarded CP-SAT formulation, that every routed commodity
is individually component-infeasible. That is too broad to justify another local
cut immediately. Before changing placement architecture, the same condition must
be asked in a differently shaped way.

E004 does not add source==sink hard equalities. For one commodity at a time it
builds a fresh front-filtered, duplicate-safe binding model, introduces one
Boolean for every component where source presence and sink presence differ, and
minimizes the number of such mismatched components.

For the optimum binding, the ordinary production run_exact_routing_precheck is
then run as an external evaluator. With both source and sink present, objective
zero is equivalent to that commodity passing the precheck's component condition.
A strictly positive proven optimum therefore independently reproduces the
singleton wall without consuming E002's repeated-solve assumption-core API.

Competing readings
------------------
A. At least one commodity has optimum mismatch zero. The E002 compiler is too
   strong or its assumption construction is wrong. Interface semantics must be
   repaired before any placement feedback.

B. Every commodity has a positive optimum, but the optimum mismatches are mostly
   tiny components with small repeated obstacle boundaries. The geometry is
   fragmented by a reusable local separator family; the next outer-loop product
   should be a checked family compiler rather than 19 point cuts.

C. Every commodity has a positive optimum and the relevant boundaries are broad,
   diverse, or heavily shared across the board. The local-pocket language has
   exhausted its leverage; placement needs an explicit positive permeability
   resource, variable backbone, or equivalent constructive representation.

D. One or more optimization solves remain non-optimal within the cap. Those
   commodities remain UNKNOWN; no minimum-mismatch or global-fragmentation claim
   is allowed for them.

The runner also pins the canonical rules, preprocess plan, mandatory instances,

Model relation
--------------
Always present:

  placement-fixed routing-aware binding domains
  exact binding cardinalities
  generic input/output cardinalities
  duplicate terminal-key exclusion

Per commodity objective:

  minimize sum_c XOR(source_present_in_c, sink_present_in_c)

No component equality is imposed. The objective is solved to OPTIMAL or left
UNKNOWN. The extracted optimum selection is replayed through the production
routing precheck and checked against the objective value.

Outcome classification
----------------------
COMPONENT_COMPILER_COUNTEREXAMPLE
  At least one commodity has a proven zero-mismatch binding while E002 called its
  singleton component rule infeasible, or the optimum/precheck comparison
  disagrees.

GLOBAL_FRAGMENTATION_REPRODUCED
  All 19 commodities are OPTIMAL with minimum mismatch at least one, and every
  optimum witness agrees with the production precheck.

PARTIAL_FRAGMENTATION_UNKNOWN
  No semantic disagreement is found, but at least one commodity is not solved to
  OPTIMAL within its cap.

EXECUTION_FAILURE
  Frozen identity, front-domain, duplicate-key, objective, extraction, or
  precheck comparison fails.

Truth boundary
--------------
A positive optimum is exact only for one commodity in the fixed E001 replacement
binding model. Boundary profiles describe one optimum mismatch witness; they do
not by themselves prove that a particular component or obstacle set occurs in
every optimum binding. The experiment does not prove no-ghost infeasibility,
produce a placement cut, run exact routing, or update any certified ledger.

Decision effect
---------------
A counterexample repairs the interface before further search. A fully positive
atlas moves the next question from "which commodity" to "what geometric resource
prevents all commodities from fragmenting". Boundary-size and owner-sharing
statistics decide whether to pursue a local separator family or a positive
permeability representation.

Result
------
Completed with verdict GLOBAL_FRAGMENTATION_REPRODUCED. All 19 commodity
objectives reached OPTIMAL, every minimum mismatch was positive (range 4 to 32),
and every optimum witness agreed with production run_exact_routing_precheck. See
RESULT.txt for the frozen identity, boundary statistics, and truth boundary.
