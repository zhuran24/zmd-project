Semantic differential
---------------------
For each of the 19 routed commodities, E004 built a fresh binding model with:

  static placement-dependent front filtering
  exact ordinary binding and generic-I/O cardinalities
  duplicate terminal-key exclusion
  no hard component equality

It then minimized the count of free components containing source terminals but no
sink terminals, or sink terminals but no source terminals, for that commodity.
All 19 solves reached OPTIMAL. Every optimum binding was independently evaluated
by production run_exact_routing_precheck, and target-commodity disconnection
agreed exactly with the positive mismatch objective.

No commodity obtained mismatch zero. E002's 19/19 singleton result is therefore
not an artifact of imposing source==sink component equalities in that one
formulation.

Boundary observations
---------------------
Across the 19 optimum witnesses, 281 mismatched-component observations were
recorded.

  occupied boundary-owner count: min 3, median 6, max 154
  component size:               min 1, max 405

Many mismatch components therefore have small local boundaries, while some
commodities also mismatch against the 405-cell main component whose boundary
contains much of the factory. The result is a mixed morphology: globally broad
fragmentation assembled from many small repeated islands and a few large shared
regions.

The same facility instances recur around mismatch boundaries across many
commodities. This suggests substantial geometric sharing, but the current counts
are over one selected optimum binding per commodity. They do not yet tell whether
the small islands compress into a few translation-equivalent separator families
or form a diverse long tail.

Interpretation
--------------
The current master is not merely producing one bad terminal pocket. It produces a
facility geometry in which every routed commodity remains disconnected after the
binding layer optimizes specifically for that commodity. The placement/binding
seam must therefore transport a positive notion of permeability or a compact
family of repeated separator violations; another commodity point cut would be the
wrong unit.

Truth boundary
--------------
Each minimum is exact only inside the fixed E001 replacement binding model. The
boundary profile describes one optimum witness for each commodity. It does not
prove a named component or obstacle owner is present in every optimum binding,
and it does not prove any particular backbone topology necessary.

Decision effect
---------------
The next cheapest discriminating question is morphological compressibility. Build
a translation-normalized atlas of the original A1 and E001 replacement free-space
components and their occupied boundaries. If a few repeated motifs cover most
mismatch islands, derive a parametric separator/pocket family. If the motifs and
boundaries are diverse, stop local cut accumulation and prototype a positive,
solver-chosen permeability or backbone resource.
