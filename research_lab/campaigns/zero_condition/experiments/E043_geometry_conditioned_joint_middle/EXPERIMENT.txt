EXPERIMENT E043: GEOMETRY-CONDITIONED JOINT PORT-MODE/ASSIGNMENT/BINDING PORTFOLIO
===================================================================================

Question
--------
Which of two structurally distinct E042 body geometries remains strongest after
the faithful E041 port-mode, operation-assignment, and binding middle layer is
rebuilt and freed on that geometry?

Geometry seeds
--------------
Seed A is E042's best body child:

  grinder_dense_blue_iron pose 6228 -> 6098
  fixed concrete endpoint objective 150
  move occurs inside the existing merged 6x4 assignment block

Seed B is the next distinct occupied geometry:

  crusher_sandleaf pose 2155 -> 5342
  fixed concrete endpoint objective 151
  move occurs outside the existing 25-body assignment context

Pose 5343 is the same body geometry as 5342 with another port mode, so it is
quotiented into Seed B's joint mode model rather than treated as a third geometry.

Method
------
For each seed, E043 updates the E041 body destinations to the seed geometry. The
nine original E041 mode-enabled destinations remain enabled. A moved destination
inside an assignment block is additionally mode-enabled; an outside moved body is
added as a one-body conditional mode block with fixed operation multiplicity.

Each seed first fixes every operation, mode, and binding choice and must reproduce
its materialized E042 endpoint exactly. Only a successful calibration admits a
free solve. The free solve jointly chooses:

  same-body port modes
  operation-to-body assignment inside the existing blocks
  binding patterns
  aggregate and component-aware shared mismatch

Occupied body geometry is fixed separately for each seed.

Decision rule
-------------
Objective 0
  Enter exact routing immediately.

One seed has a strictly lower OPTIMAL joint value
  Retain that geometry and recompute its residual surface before selecting a body
  partner.

The two seeds tie with distinct witnesses
  Retain a small geometry beam and compare their next body-response domains.

Neither seed improves its fixed calibration value
  The carried middle layer is saturated on both singleton geometries; build a
  simultaneous body-pair neighborhood.

Truth boundary
--------------
E043 compares only two frozen single-body geometry states under bounded
conditional mode/assignment contexts. It does not optimize body placement inside
one model, exhaust body pairs, prove component compatibility, reach routing,
establish a lower bound, or grant certified effect.

Result
------
Completed. Both frozen geometries calibrate exactly. Seed A reoptimizes from 150
to OPTIMAL 147; Seed B reoptimizes from 151 to OPTIMAL 150. The fixed-assignment
geometry ranking survives, but the joint middle layer contributes three additional
units on Seed A and one on Seed B.

Seed A is retained as the leading geometry. Its objective-147 endpoint is fully
materialized and becomes the sole source for the next residual/body-partner
surface. See RESULT.txt for exact model sizes, optimum-face audits, and frozen
artifact identities.
