EXPERIMENT E042: UNADMITTED PORT MODE VERSUS BODY-RELOCATION DISCRIMINATOR
================================================================================

Question
--------
At the fully materialized objective-152 endpoint, does the next bounded freedom
come from same-footprint pose/port modes not admitted by E041, from occupied-body
relocation, or from a hybrid neighborhood?

Why this experiment
-------------------
E041 proves that nine same-body mode destinations, operation assignment, and
binding can cohabit faithfully and improve 154 -> 152. The current layout still
has 19 positive commodity mismatches. The next context cannot be selected by the
old 157 residual surface or by one manually chosen pose.

Method
------
E042 reconstructs all 152 mismatch-component observations and current boundary
literals from the frozen E041 endpoint. Before any binding solve it enumerates the
placement- and power-valid fixed-outside pose domain of every admissible current
literal and classifies each alternative as:

  same-footprint pose/port mode
  occupied-body relocation

A body whose modes were already admitted by E041 is excluded from the new-mode
universe but remains eligible for body relocation. Two independent exact
maximum-coverage portfolios are then frozen:

  six unadmitted-mode targets
  six body-relocation targets

One target may belong to both portfolios; the union is evaluated once. Every
selected target's complete fixed-outside action domain is passed through static
owner domains and the exact shared component-mismatch objective. Mode and body
responses are reported separately even when they share an arm.

The complete 268-target mobility census is written to a local manifest. Coverage
proposes the finite portfolios; exact static domains and shared binding determine
value.

Decision rule
-------------
Objective 0 on either axis
  Materialize and enter exact routing immediately.

Mode improvement of at least two units, stronger than body relocation
  Expand the faithful joint mode/assignment/binding context with the selected
  unadmitted body destinations. Do not continue one mode at a time.

Body improvement of at least two units, stronger than unadmitted mode
  Build a simultaneous body-geometry neighborhood carrying the existing joint
  mode/assignment/binding consumer.

Both axes materially improve at equal best value
  Build a bounded hybrid mode-plus-body neighborhood.

Neither axis materially improves
  Treat fixed-outside unary mobility as saturated and build a simultaneous body
  geometry neighborhood rather than another point action.

Truth boundary
--------------
E042 exhausts fixed-outside alternatives only for the exact six-plus-six
residual-selected portfolios under one objective-152 parent. The portfolios are
not necessary families, and a positive objective gain is not component
compatibility, routing, a lower bound, or certified effect.

Result
------
Completed. The exact six-target mode portfolio covers 69/152 observations and
finds a best same-footprint result of 151. The exact six-target body portfolio
covers 71/152 observations and finds a material body relocation at 150 by moving
grinder_dense_blue_iron pose 6228 to pose 6098. Both optimum faces retain the
ore-pair fixed sum 14, so the body's structural gain is buckwheat -1 and
sandleaf_powder -1.

The decision is to carry the faithful joint port-mode, operation-assignment, and
binding consumer across a small portfolio of distinct body geometries before
selecting another geometry partner. See RESULT.txt for frozen identities and the
exact truth boundary.
