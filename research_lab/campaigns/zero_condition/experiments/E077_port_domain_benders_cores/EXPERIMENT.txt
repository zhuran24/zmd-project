EXPERIMENT E077: PORT-DOMAIN BENDERS CORES OVER THE A+B VACANCY
==============================================================

Record status
-------------
This directory is a retrospective record, written after the fact. E077 was run as
a series of disposable probe scripts under research_lab/local/zero_condition/,
not as a tracked runner in this tree, and no question, decision rule, or stop
boundary was declared in advance. The sections below transcribe the model that
the surviving scripts actually build and the results that were actually
persisted. Everything the probes printed only to stdout is gone; RESULT.txt marks
those items unverifiable rather than reconstructing them.

Question
--------
E075 closed the two-body native context by showing that moving owners A and B
empties other rows' native option domains. The follow-on question is how much of
the 38-body footprint layout must be rebuilt to keep the A+B cells clear while
every operation still gets a legal port domain: how many current bodies can be
retained, and what minimal footprint combinations are already impossible.

Model actually built
--------------------
The integrated master is the only script whose output survives. It builds:

1. A footprint atlas of 6x4 placements, excluding any footprint meeting the fixed
   outside occupancy or the critical cells, where critical cells are the union of
   owner A's and owner B's current cells taken from E075.
2. A set-packing selection: one Boolean per candidate footprint, per-cell
   disjointness, and Sum(y) == 38.
3. Power coverage through the existing coverer tables.
4. Operation demand classes derived from port profiles rather than named
   operations, quotienting the 38 rows into three classes by required input and
   output slot counts, with exact per-class cardinality.
5. Per-footprint, per-pose, per-demand-class option variables gated by port front
   usability.
6. A Benders loop over a master with a retained-body cardinality target, in which
   each subproblem infeasibility is replayed in a checker and stored as a
   no-good over the footprint indices it assumed.

The retained target is an environment parameter; retained r corresponds to
38 - r moved bodies.

What the model does not contain
-------------------------------
No routing, no connectivity, no component binding, no commodity terminals, no
throughput, and no named-operation identity beyond the three port-profile demand
classes. A footprint combination this model rejects is rejected on port-domain
and packing grounds only.

Truth boundary
--------------
E077 is a port-domain relaxation over 6x4 footprint geometry with the A+B cells
forbidden. Cores it produces bound that relaxation. They are not placement cuts
for the certified problem, not a separator or membrane result, and not evidence
about any layout property the model does not represent.

Result
------
Two checkpoint artifacts survive and are transcribed in RESULT.txt. No terminal
verdict was persisted, and the minimum moved-body count E077 was built to find is
not recoverable from any artifact.
