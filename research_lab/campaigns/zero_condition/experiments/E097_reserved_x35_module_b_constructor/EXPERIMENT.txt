EXPERIMENT E097: RESERVED-X35 MODULE-B CONSTRUCTOR
==================================================

Question
--------
Can E095's exact 91-body module-B native-front problem be solved under the
sufficient spatial restriction selected by E096: no manufacturing body occupies
column x=35, low bodies end at x<=34, and high bodies begin at x>=36?

Why this experiment
-------------------
E096 proves that the x=34 spatial split has a far thinner exact interface than
the template split. Its unrestricted separator still contains 436 candidates.
The cheapest constructive use of that discovery is to reserve the adjacent
column x=35 from manufacturing bodies. This turns the low/high body and
front/body relation into an exact product while preserving x=35 as a usable
native-front lane. The only remaining bridge is the eight-dimensional allocation
of B operation/front-demand classes between the two sides.

Method
------
1. Pin E095's exact factorization packet and module-A witness, E096's selected
   interface result/check, and E092's fixed 53-pole / boundary_macro_09 skeleton.
   Reuse E095's hash-pinned context reconstruction and operation materialization.

2. Reconstruct all active B candidates and delete only candidates whose body
   occupies x=35. Classify every survivor uniquely:

     low    max body x <= 34
     high   min body x >= 36

   Column x=35 above the y=41 seam must contain no fixed solid, but it remains
   available to input/output fronts. Front/front coincidence remains outside the
   native-front layer exactly as in E095.

3. Build one exact satisfaction model with:

     * all 91 B bodies and exact template counts;
     * all B front-demand classes and same-footprint native modes;
     * body non-overlap and fixed-pole power;
     * the two stable E078 bodies and their required operations;
     * eight explicit integer allocation pairs
       low[class] + high[class] = required[class].

   There is no retained-count objective. E092's compatible B bodies are hints
   only. Stop after the first solution.

4. A positive is materialized into deterministic named operations, combined with
   E095's frozen 128-body A witness, and independently replayed as a complete
   219-body power/native-front assignment.

Predeclared decisions
---------------------
Positive and combined replay PASS
  Advance the combined witness to duplicate terminal uniqueness, generic I/O and
  component-aware binding.

INFEASIBLE
  Reject only the reserved-x35 sufficient restriction. Return to E096's full
  low/separator/high language and solve conditioned on explicit allocation
  vectors; do not call unrestricted module B infeasible.

UNKNOWN
  Preserve censorship. Decompose into low/high side solves conditioned on
  allocation vectors, using the reserved column rather than rerunning the same
  monolith.

Truth boundary
--------------
E097 strengthens one fixed-pole, fixed-boundary native-front problem by reserving
a manufacturing-free column. A positive is valid for the unrestricted parent;
a negative is not. Terminal uniqueness, generic I/O, commodity binding,
component compatibility, routing and throughput remain outside. Research-only;
no certification, U/L, owner, production, publication or pruning effect.
