EXPERIMENT E053: MERGED 6X4 FIRST-ZERO JOINT CONTEXT
====================================================

Question
--------
Can the smallest operation-to-body expansion selected by E052 create the first
component-compatible commodity without changing occupied geometry?

The expansion merges four relevant bodies into the live ten-footprint 6x4 block:

  filling_capsule_002
  grinder_fine_buckwheat_004
  grinder_fine_buckwheat_005
  grinder_fine_buckwheat_006

The resulting fourteen-footprint block lets all three capsule operations and all
five fine-buckwheat grinder operations exchange 6x4 bodies while native port mode
and binding remain in the same consumer.

Why this experiment
-------------------
E051 proves the old bounded context cannot make any commodity reach mismatch zero.
E052 then proves that same-body modes and all 38 relevant one-body relocations
cannot make `fine_buckwheat_powder` reach zero. Four directly implicated bodies
remain outside the operation-assignment block. Merging those bodies is narrower
than another geometry portfolio and directly tests the missing freedom.

Method
------
E053 reconstructs E050 Seed C and the faithful E051 context, replaces only the
6x4 assignment block, preserves all occupied cells, and enables same-body modes at
the four added destinations while retaining every previously enabled mode.

It performs four exact stages:

1. fixed-state calibration, which must reproduce objective 139;
2. free sum-optimal solve in the expanded context;
3. positive-count optimization over the complete expanded joint model;
4. total-mismatch minimization with positive count fixed to its exact optimum.

A zero-bearing lexicographic state is materialized and replayed with operation and
mode fixed while binding remains free under the same positive-count constraint.
The selected port set is checked against the production component precheck.

Decision rule
-------------
Positive commodity count = 0
  Enter exact routing immediately.

Positive commodity count < 19
  Retain the lexicographic first-zero state. Recompute residual causes with
  positive-count preservation as the primary progress axis.

Positive commodity count = 19
  The operation-to-body extension is saturated for first-zero progress. Move to a
  native simultaneous body context selected by the fine-powder boundary rather
  than continue shared-sum descent.

Calibration failure
  Reject all free results and repair the expanded conditional-owner context.

Truth boundary
--------------
E053 is exact only for E050 Seed C occupied geometry and the expanded fourteen-body
6x4 assignment block together with the inherited 3x3, 5x5, and singleton blocks.
It does not prove a global first-zero bound, routing, throughput, U/L, or
certification.

Result
------

calibrations reproduce 139. The unrestricted sum solve and direct target-zero
