EXPERIMENT E057: ONE EXTERNAL CORE-SIDE BODY FOR THE SECOND ZERO
================================================================

Question
--------
Can one causally selected external 6x4 body create `qiaoyu_capsule = 0` while
preserving the verified `fine_buckwheat_powder = 0` state, thereby lowering the
positive commodity count from 18 to at most 17 without changing occupied geometry?

Why this context
----------------
The E055 witness has `qiaoyu_capsule = 2`. Its sole sink is the protocol-core
terminal in free component 15. The three capsule sources occupy components 15, 1,
and 40. Inside the fourteen-body merged 6x4 context, exactly three body/mode choices
can emit a filling-capsule output into component 15, but the first-zero causal
relation reserves one of those bodies for `grinder_fine_buckwheat`. Hence only two
remain for three filling-capsule operations.

A complete conditional-domain audit of the 24 current 6x4 bodies outside the block
finds exactly one same-body mode whose filling-capsule output reaches component 15:

  grinder_dense_blue_iron_004
  current pose 6239  p_x47_y65_o0_m_BT
  admitted mode 6238 p_x47_y65_o0_m_TB

E057 appends only this body to the existing block. It does not move the body or
open a global geometry portfolio.

Method
------
1. Reconstruct the exact E055 18/139 witness and E056 mode context.
2. Append `grinder_dense_blue_iron_004` to the merged 6x4 block, preserving all
   existing destination identities and adding its two same-body modes.
3. Recalibrate the expanded fixed state at positive count 18 and total 139 without
   imposing the new qiaoyu relation.
4. In the free model, retain the original first-zero causal body relation, require
   the appended body to carry `filling_capsule`, require both
   `fine_buckwheat_powder = 0` and `qiaoyu_capsule = 0`, and leave the remaining
   merged-block operation assignment, admitted modes, and binding free.
5. Minimize positive commodity count, then total mismatch on that face. Materialize
   and replay any exact winner through the production component precheck.

Decision rule
-------------
Positive count <= 17
  Retain the materialized second-zero state and recompute the residual frontier.

INFEASIBLE
  Close this single-external-body qiaoyu relation only. Do not infer that qiaoyu
  cannot reach zero through body relocation or a different causal context.

UNKNOWN
  Preserve the calibrated context and change the solver container or add a narrower
  relation; do not classify the relation as absent.

Truth boundary
--------------
E057 covers one external body, one fixed occupied geometry, the inherited
fine-powder causal relation, and the current conditional assignment/mode/binding
consumer. It does not prove global positive-count optimality, enter exact routing,
update U/L, or grant certification.

Result
------
Completed. The expanded context recalibrates exactly at 18/139, but the
operation-count-preserving three-operation cycle is INFEASIBLE while requiring
both fine_buckwheat_powder and qiaoyu_capsule mismatch zero. See RESULT.txt and
