EXPERIMENT E065: SOURCE-STABLE REPLAY MATERIALIZATION
====================================================

Question
--------
Can E057-E062 be re-executed from their tracked source under fresh Python bytecode
caches, with durable machine outputs present on disk, so the active E063 frontier
has a real source-stable premise rather than a narrative-only locator?

Trigger
-------
E064 recorded a source-cache investigation and named six fresh replay results, but
at the next independent inspection those six directories were empty. Its two
remaining origin-audit files also both reported zero foreign functions, so they do
not reproduce the historical contamination described by E064's prose. The current
research tree therefore lacks the machine objects that E063 attempted to pin.

This is an evidence-materialization defect, not a new scientific counterexample.
E065 does not assume E064's historical cache event can still be reproduced after
the worktree move and later interpreter activity.

Method
------
1. Execute each tracked runner E057-E062 in its own Python process.
2. Give every process a fresh private `PYTHONPYCACHEPREFIX` before interpreter
   startup and the exact `EXPECTED_ENV` declared by that runner.
3. Import the original tracked source without copying or editing it. Before
   execution, verify that every module-defined function's code filename resolves
   to that exact source file.
4. Redirect only the runner's output/checkpoint globals into a new ignored E065
   run directory. All frozen semantic inputs and source hashes remain those of the
   original experiment.
5. Compare E057-E061 with their original machine results after removing only
   timestamps, runtime counters, identity envelopes, and evidence locators.
6. For E062, compare the complete objective distribution, physical near-miss
   identities, exact objective values, atlas content, and exhaustion counts
   separately from the arbitrary representative chosen on an optimum presence
   face.
7. Freeze the new output hashes in one terminal result and a receipt. E063 may pin
   only the resulting E062 replay, not E064's absent locator.

The six runs are checkpointed by label. A completed label is reused only when its
source-origin receipt and required output files are present.

Decision rule
-------------
E057-E061 scientific projections identical, E062 physical/objective frontier
stable, and every source-origin audit clean
  Establish the E065 replay set as the active source-stable premise. Resume E063
  from its complete directional optimum face.

Any changed verdict, exact objective, physical near-miss identity, exhaustion
count, or atlas content
  Quarantine that result and every dependent frontier. Restart from the last
  source-stable predecessor.

Only E062 representative-presence changes
  Preserve the physical/objective result, record all representative differences,
  and require E063 to enumerate the complete optimum face rather than inherit one
  representative.

Truth boundary
--------------
E065 re-executes the frozen E057-E062 research models and repairs their local
machine-evidence availability. It does not prove the historical cause of E064's
missing artifacts, certify any model, establish a second zero, enter routing, or
update U/L.
