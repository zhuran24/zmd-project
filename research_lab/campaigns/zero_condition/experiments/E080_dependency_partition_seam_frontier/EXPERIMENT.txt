E080 DEPENDENCY PARTITION / SEAM FRONTIER
=========================================

Status
------
Completed in run-001 as the first cheap discriminator for the partition-plus-seam
sufficient-construction line. Research-only. No certification, U/L, production,
owner, pruning, or publication effect.

Question
--------
Before building any new coordinate master, does the frozen 17-operation recipe
graph admit a balanced two-module partition with a small, explicit typed seam?

The seam is a construction interface, not a necessary cut and not a throughput
proof. E080 works at operation-type level: all mandatory copies of one operation
type remain in the same module.

Inputs
------

- frozen mandatory exact instances;
- canonical operation port profiles;
- frozen candidate-placement pool, used only to verify facility body area;
- E079's exact 47-state boundary macro and its complete consumption license.

Enumeration
-----------
Fix one operation type in module A to quotient A/B symmetry, then exhaust every
nontrivial bipartition of the remaining operation types.

For every partition record:

- operation types, mandatory copy counts, body area, and instance count per
  module;
- whether each induced dependency subgraph is connected;
- every producer-to-consumer commodity dependency crossing the partition;
- directional concrete input/output slot incidences on the seam;
- named external-source input obligations per module, while keeping their
  generic-provider binding existential;
- final-sink output obligations per module;
- area and instance imbalance.

No per-machine duty or terminal rate is frozen. Slot incidence is a discrete
interface count, not a lane-throughput certificate.

Outputs
-------

- the complete metric histogram over all canonical bipartitions;
- the Pareto frontier among partitions whose two induced operation graphs are
  connected;
- deterministic best candidates under three declared balance bands;
- one selected 1/3--2/3 connected partition, if it exists;
- an explicit seam-interface contract referencing all 47 E079 boundary states.

Success / refutation
--------------------
A small connected balanced seam is positive evidence that partition-plus-seam is
worth a geometric constructor. Failure to find one refutes only this type-level
bipartition language; it does not refute instance-level partitioning, more than
two modules, or the general skeleton direction.

Truth boundary
--------------
Operation copies cannot move independently in E080. Crossing slot counts do not
prove required throughput, and generic source slots are not assigned to named
commodities until binding. The selected partition is a deterministic research
choice, never WLOG. The K=47 macro remains a complete 47-way boundary
disjunction.
