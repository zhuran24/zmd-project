Competing readings
------------------
A. Total mismatch falls again and one or more commodities reach zero. Alternating

B. Pattern survival improves but mismatch is flat or worse. Port-domain quantity
   and component placement are separate enough that the next local model must
   include component-aware binding directly, not alternate scalar proxies.

C. Most mismatch improves but a small commodity set regresses. Use the residual

   preserving the two proven useful resources.
