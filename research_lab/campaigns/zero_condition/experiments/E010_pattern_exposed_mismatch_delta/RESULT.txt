Interpretation
--------------
The two positive signals are not merely exchanging one proxy for another.
Permeability first buys a large mismatch reduction. Selection-conditioned pattern
exposure then increases real binding-domain survival by 1101 while also reducing
aggregate mismatch further.

This supports an outer-loop constructor of the form:

  compute current occupancy
    -> optimize one positive interface resource locally
    -> rebuild the exact fixed-layout interface
    -> derive the next contextual resource coefficients
    -> move within a trust region
    -> repeat

However, no commodity has yet reached zero minimum mismatch. Scalar alternation
has moved the whole distribution, but it has not crossed component feasibility.
The next step should preserve the E009 port-exposure gain while improving
permeability, rather than independently optimizing adjacency and risking another
trade reversal.

convergence of every future alternating step, and none of the three is a
component-compatible layout. Each minimum is exact only within its fixed-layout
binding model.
