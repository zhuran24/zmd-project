EXPERIMENT E013: residual mismatch boundary coverage
===================================================

Question
--------
Is E009's residual component wall controlled by a bounded set of current
master literals and a compact spatial neighborhood, or is the causal surface too
distributed for one local joint placement-binding model?

Why this precedes the joint model
---------------------------------
E012 proves that another scalar placement proxy is no longer justified. It does
not prove that one small local neighborhood carries enough of the residual cause.
Choosing a window by visual intuition would repeat the project's old ansatz error.

E013 takes the 196 exact mismatch-component observations from E010. Every occupied
boundary owner is transported from its named instance identity to the actual
pose-bool consumer object:

  mandatory instance -> (operation group, selected pose)
  power pole / required optional -> (template, selected pose)

It then computes:

  exact maximum observation coverage for literal budgets 1,2,4,8,16,32,64
  exact minimum literal count for 50%,75%,90%,100% coverage when reachable
  best single axis-aligned windows of sizes 8..40, where a literal is movable only
  if its current occupied body is fully contained in the window
  operation-family and power-pole participation

Coverage means only that moving at least one selected boundary literal could
change that mismatch component. It is an optimistic causal-surface upper bound,
not a repair theorem.

Competing readings
------------------
A. A small literal set and one compact window touch most observations. Build one
   exact local placement-binding neighborhood.

B. A small literal set covers broadly but is spatially dispersed. Use a bounded
   group-pose neighborhood or several small regions, not one geometric window.

C. Even 16 or 32 literals cover little of the residual. The local-cut/joint-window
   hypothesis is too narrow; move toward a solver-chosen global backbone or a
   decomposed multi-region representation.

Frozen inputs
-------------
E009 assignment/layout and E010 mismatch atlas are SHA-pinned in the runner,
along with mandatory instances, candidate placements, rules, and the master
source defining group identity.

Truth boundary
--------------
Boundary touch is necessary only for changing the currently observed component
boundary. Moving one touched literal may fail to repair it, may alter other
components, or may require an alternative pose outside the reported window. The
set-cover result therefore chooses a research neighborhood; it does not prove
that neighborhood sufficient.

Decision effect
---------------
The exact coverage curve and spatial compactness determine whether E014 is a
single local joint model, a dispersed group-pose portfolio, or a global positive
backbone representation.

Result
------
Completed with verdict DISPERSED_GROUP_POSE_NEIGHBORHOOD_PLAUSIBLE. After
transport there are 313 consumer literals and 151 exact coverage-equivalence
classes. Eight literals cover 58.7%, 16 cover 87.8%, and 26 are sufficient for
100% coverage. Small-budget optima are manufacturing-heavy, not pole-only. A
best 24x24 window covers 43.4%, while 40x40 covers 74.5%. See RESULT.txt.
