EXPERIMENT E007: permeability candidate interface test
======================================================

Question
--------
Did E006's higher free adjacency and lower optional-obstacle count move the real
placement/binding interface enough to produce one binding whose terminals are
component-compatible, and if so can exact routing use it?

Why this test comes immediately
-------------------------------
E006's score is only a proxy. The candidate must not accumulate another layer of
proxy analysis before meeting the exact interface that killed A1 and E001.

E007 rebuilds, from frozen inputs and a fresh CP-SAT model:

  static terminal-front filtering
  ordinary exact binding cardinalities
  duplicate terminal-key exclusion
  all 19 commodity component-support rules

All component guards are fixed true by ordinary constraints. E007 does not reuse
E002's repeated-solve assumptions or sufficient-core API.

If binding is FEASIBLE, the selected ports are replayed through production
run_exact_routing_precheck and then sent directly to RoutingSubproblem. A routing
witness, if found, enters the same non-ghost strict terminal validator used by
E001.

Competing readings
------------------
A. Component-aware binding is FEASIBLE. The positive permeability signal crossed
   the previous interface wall; exact routing becomes the current wall or yields
   the first routing-complete zero-condition research witness.

B. Component-aware binding is INFEASIBLE, but a new minimum-mismatch atlas is
   substantially better than E001. The proxy moved the right statistic but not
   far enough; strengthen or iterate the positive construction layer.

C. Component-aware binding is INFEASIBLE and mismatch is unchanged or worse.
   Pairwise free adjacency rewards the wrong geometry, most likely by removing
   optional clutter without reconnecting terminal-support components. Replace it
   with a main-component, articulation, or variable-backbone statistic.

D. Binding or routing remains UNKNOWN within the cap. Preserve the exact stage
   reached and do not infer feasibility direction.
