Apparatus correction
--------------------
Run-001 imported module-B domain counts from the neighboring all-poles integrated
model. The fixed-52-plus-one-replacement language reconstructed only 1,335 B 3x3
candidates rather than the borrowed 1,585 and failed closed before producing an
atlas.

Run-002 removes that neighboring-language calibration and derives every domain
count from the current language. Correct reconstruction is established instead by
exact ordered remapping of all 181 checkpoint rules, body/template identity,
demand classes and producer option counts. The run-001 failure remains on disk as
apparatus evidence and is not interpreted as a scientific negative.

The E087 checkpoint contains 178 registered module-B rules, or about 4.09 percent

Concentration result
--------------------
The coarse stencil language groups most failures into a few shapes, but those
shapes cover nearly the entire module-B candidate universe:

  target coverage   signatures   registered covered   candidates expanded
  80 percent                 6                  145                3,740
  90 percent                16                  161                3,818

The finer support language expands to fewer candidates, but loses reusable
compression because almost every registered rule needs its own signature:

  target coverage   signatures   registered covered   candidates expanded
  80 percent               129                  143                  259
  90 percent               147                  161                  294

The predeclared bulk-support criterion required at most 8 signatures for 80
percent coverage. The predeclared bulk-stencil criterion required at most 4
signatures for 90 percent coverage and at most 3,500 expanded candidates. Both
fail.

Interpretation
--------------
The 178 failures are not one or two reusable front rules wearing different
coordinate labels.

At coarse resolution, the hot shapes are too broad: compiling them would eagerly
attach rules to roughly 86 percent of the whole B universe. At fine resolution,
the failures are sparse and context-specific: 129 support signatures are needed
to explain only 143 registered rows.

Therefore another blind lazy solve is not the next justified action. The point
rule language has reached the wrong grain. The next discriminator should measure
where failures live in module B as spatial roles or subregions, and should use
that measurement to redesign or decompose the B bay or pole language.

Non-implications
----------------
E088 does not prove:

  * any unregistered member of a signature feasible or infeasible;
  * the bounded one-replacement language infeasible;
  * module B globally responsible for every future failure;
  * any shared signature cut sound;
  * the y=41 partition or seam invalid;
  * any terminal uniqueness, binding, routing or throughput property.

Effect on the next problem
--------------------------
Stop adding coordinate rules one endpoint at a time. Build a no-solver spatial
hazard atlas for the complete module-B universe and the 178 registered failures.
Measure concentration by stable geometric roles such as distance to the seam,
outer boundary, protocol core, fixed poles and candidate-front support density.

If a small set of spatial regions captures most failures while excluding a large
fraction of the B universe, derive a decomposed B-bay constructor or reserved
front halo from those regions. If spatial roles are also diffuse, retire the
fixed-52-plus-one-replacement B-bay language and widen pole or partition geometry
before another expensive master solve.

Truth boundary
--------------
This result is an exact finite census of one frozen rule store and one frozen
candidate language. It changes the research representation and next question; it
is not a theorem about existence of a complete layout.
