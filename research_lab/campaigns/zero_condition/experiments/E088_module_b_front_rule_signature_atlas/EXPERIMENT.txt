EXPERIMENT E088: COMPLETE MODULE-B FRONT-RULE SIGNATURE ATLAS
================================================================

Question
--------
Do the 178 registered module-B front rules occupy a small reusable family inside
the complete module-B candidate universe, or are they diffuse enough that the
y=41 bay/pole language should change before another master solve?

Why this experiment
-------------------
E086/E087 grow the rule store 34 -> 98 -> 181. E087's 83 new rules are all in
module B and its two candidates still have 39 and 44 empty-front bodies. Blindly
continuing the lazy loop would pay for more coordinate rows without first asking
whether those rows are translations of a small rule language.

Object space
------------
E088 exactly reconstructs E086/E087's ordered manufacturing candidate rows:

  module A then B
  manufacturing_3x3, manufacturing_5x5, manufacturing_6x4
  same y=41 side restrictions
  fixed 52 non-seam poles
  protocol-core body and front exclusions
  corridor exclusion
  complete 47-state boundary eligibility

The initial calibration hypothesis imported a 5,706-row module-B count from the
all-poles integrated model. Run-001 rejected that hypothesis immediately: the
fixed-52-plus-one-replacement language has only 1,335 B 3x3 candidates before the
other template counts are reached. That failure is retained as apparatus evidence.

Run-002 therefore derives all module-B counts from the current language itself.
Correct reconstruction is established by ordered index/body/template remapping of
all 181 checkpoint rules, exact demand-class matching, exact option-count matching,
and the complete 47-state/fixed-obstacle construction, not by borrowing counts
from a neighboring pole language.

Two canonical proposer signatures
---------------------------------
For each module-B candidate, the actual rule demand is the minimal module/template
class used by E086, except the stable E078 operation overrides recorded in the
checkpoint.

`stencil_signature` contains:

  template
  demand classes
  every same-footprint pose mode
  input/output front cells after in-grid and fixed-occupied clipping
  all front cells normalized by subtracting the body's minimum x/y

`support_signature` additionally records, for every normalized front cell, the
number of possible dynamic manufacturing, replacement-pole and boundary-state
occupiers.

These signatures are **proposer groupings only**. They do not merge CP-SAT
variables or authorize one shared constraint. Any bulk consumer must instantiate
the existing exact candidate-specific front rule for every selected candidate in
a chosen signature family.

Outputs
-------

  RESULT.json                 compact decision and concentration metrics
  SIGNATURE_ATLAS.json        per-signature universe/registered counts
  CANDIDATE_SIGNATURES.json   complete current-language candidate-to-signature map

Predeclared decision rule
-------------------------
BULK_COMPILE_HOT_SUPPORT_SIGNATURES
  The minimum support-signature prefix covering at least 80% of registered B
  rules has at most 8 signatures and expands to at most 2,500 B candidates.

BULK_COMPILE_HOT_STENCIL_SIGNATURES_WITH_EXACT_PER_CANDIDATE_RULES
  The support criterion fails, but at most 4 stencil signatures cover at least
  90% of registered B rules and expand to at most 3,500 candidates.

RULE_FAILURES_DIFFUSE_REVISE_MODULE_B_GEOMETRY_OR_DECOMPOSE
  Neither concentration condition holds. Do not eagerly instantiate essentially
  the whole B universe merely to imitate a coordinate-level closure; change the
  B-bay/pole language or decompose it first.

Truth boundary
--------------
This is an exact finite census and a representation discriminator, not a
feasibility result. Signature concentration does not prove all members fail and
signature diffusion does not prove the one-replacement language infeasible.
Research-only; no certification, U/L, production, owner, pruning, publication,
routing or throughput effect.
