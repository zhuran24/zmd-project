EXPERIMENT E050: FAITHFUL JOINT REVALUATION OF EXTERNAL RESCUE STATES
=====================================================================

Question
--------
Do the three E049 rescued pose states remain below the objective-144 parent after
port mode, operation assignment, and binding are rebuilt and jointly optimized,
or is their 143/143/144 ordinary gain only an admission effect?

Why this experiment
-------------------
E049 proves that structured external empty-owner relations can revive fixed-
outside dead actions, but ordinary shared-binding value is not geometry
continuation value. Two rescued states move both bodies; the third moves one body
and changes the second owner's same-body port mode. The E048 pair-context compiler
counts only occupied-body changes and therefore cannot faithfully represent all
three.

E050 generalizes the context boundary from exactly two body changes to exactly two
pose changes with one or two body changes. Every changed instance updates its
existing assignment destination or receives a singleton block, and both changed
destinations receive native same-body mode choices.

Method
------
E050 reconstructs the exact objective-144 parent context. For each E049 seed it:

  verifies exactly two pose changes relative to the immediate parent
  records which changes alter occupied body cells
  preserves all inherited operation multisets and selected-instance sets
  fixes pose mode, operation assignment, and binding to reproduce the frozen
  ordinary objective 143, 143, or 144
  only after exact calibration releases the faithful middle layer
  materializes the best concrete assignment and ordinary fixed-layout endpoint

Each seed is checkpointed independently and may be resumed without recomputation.

Decision rule
-------------
Joint objective 0
  Enter exact routing immediately.

Best joint objective < 144
  Retain the strict rescued state and recompute its residual causal/mobility
  surface. External rescue becomes a confirmed continuation mechanism.

Every joint objective >= 144
  Separate rescue admission from continuation value and proceed to a broader
  native simultaneous geometry context.

Calibration failure
  Reject the affected result and repair pose-change context fidelity before any
  free solve is interpreted.

Truth boundary
--------------
E050 assigns exact bounded middle-layer values to three E049 rescued pose states
only. It does not exhaust external rescue, body pairs, multi-owner relations, all
pose domains, or global geometry. It cannot update U/L or grant certification.

Result
------
Completed. All three frozen states calibrate exactly at 143, 143, and 144. Their
free faithful joint values are OPTIMAL 141, 141, and 139. The ordinary-144 Seed C
therefore becomes the strict continuation winner. Verdict:

  EXTERNAL_RESCUE_JOINT_IMPROVEMENT

The objective-139 endpoint is fully materialized and becomes the next residual
state. See RESULT.txt.
