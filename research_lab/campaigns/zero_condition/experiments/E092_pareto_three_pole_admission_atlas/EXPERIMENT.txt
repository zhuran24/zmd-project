EXPERIMENT E092: COMPLETE THREE-POLE BODY/POWER ADMISSION ATLAS FOR THE E081 PARETO BEAM
================================================================================================

Question
--------
Which of E081's seven geometry Pareto partition/seam states can reach the native-
front layer at all under the same exact three-pole body/power language?

Why this experiment
-------------------
E090's complete front consumer is censored on the geometry winner. E091 then
shows that the next Pareto state is exact body/power INFEASIBLE before front
checking. Building another 100k-variable front model state by state would spend
budget on candidates that may not even admit a skeleton. The seven-state beam is
finite, so the body/power admission question should be closed first.

Common language
---------------
For every Pareto state, E092 keeps:

  * the state's own operation partition, axis, seam coordinate and side order;
  * exactly 219 module/template manufacturing bodies;
  * the two stable E078 bodies in the module recorded by that state;
  * the complete 47-state boundary disjunction;
  * exact body/pole/boundary non-overlap;
  * manufacturing power coverage;
  * exactly 53 poles, at least 50 at current pole poses;
  * pure feasibility and stop after the first incumbent.

The same derived producer is parameterized by state identity. The y=41 state
alone receives E084's exact three-relocation body/power witness as a calibration
hint. Other states receive current-geometry hints only. Hints do not alter the
feasible set.

Calibration
-----------
The sweep is admissible only if it reproduces:

  partition_90abd29523f2a0dc / y=41   BODY_POWER_FEASIBLE
  partition_97f9ba7e7ad710dc / y=17   MASTER_INFEASIBLE

A calibration failure is an apparatus failure, not a partition conclusion.

Decision rule
-------------
Additional admitted states exist
  Send the earliest additional Pareto survivor to the same complete three-pole
  native-front consumer used by E090.

Only y=41 is admitted and no state is UNKNOWN
  The three-pole skeleton language itself selects y=41. Choose explicitly between
  a wider pole budget and decomposing the censored y=41 front consumer.

Any remaining state is UNKNOWN and no additional survivor exists
  Preserve censorship. Resolve the body/power admission instrument before
  claiming y=41 is the sole survivor.

Truth boundary
--------------
E092 is body/pole/power admission only. INFEASIBLE is contextual to the named
partition, seam, boundary object and three-pole budget. FEASIBLE does not imply
native-front, terminal, binding, routing, throughput or whole-layout feasibility.
