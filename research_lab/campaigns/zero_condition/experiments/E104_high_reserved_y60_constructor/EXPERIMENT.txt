EXPERIMENT E104: HIGH-SIDE RESERVED-Y60 HYBRID CONSTRUCTOR
==========================================================

Question
--------
Can E103's selected `y_after_59` spatial/template hybrid produce the first exact
high-side native-front allocation when manufacturing bodies are forbidden from
row y=60?

Why this experiment
-------------------
E103 proves that template and spatial interfaces are incomparable, capability
signatures are degenerate, and the y59/template hybrid is the only representation
that passes the frozen hybrid gates. Reserving y=60 is its cheapest sufficient
realization. It removes the explicit spatial separator while retaining all
template-local operation/front classes.

Method
------
1. Reconstruct E100's source-stable x42 domain and E103's exact live high subset.
   Remove every live high candidate whose body occupies y=60. Classify survivors:

     lower   max body y <= 59
     upper   min body y >= 61

   Fixed poles/core/boundary remain unchanged and may occupy y=60; they are
   constant front blockers, not manufacturing variables. Mechanically verify that
   lower/upper body/body and front/body coupling is zero.

2. Solve one pure-feasibility high model with:

     exact high template totals 10 / 6 / 10
     all eight high class-allocation coordinates
     same-footprint native modes
     fixed-pole power
     both stable E078 bodies
     exact body non-overlap and native-front capacity
     surviving E101 body witness rows as hints only

   Row y=60 remains available to native fronts where fixed solids do not block it.

3. If high is feasible, pass its exact eight-class complement to E101's unchanged
   x42 low-side model. A paired positive is materialized into named operations,
   combined with E095's frozen module-A witness and replayed over all 219 bodies.

Budgets
-------

  high reserved-y60 solve          120 seconds
  low exact complement             120 seconds

Predeclared decisions
---------------------
Paired positive and replay PASS
  Advance to terminal uniqueness, generic I/O and component-aware binding.

High INFEASIBLE
  Reject only manufacturing-free y=60. Restore E103's explicit 154-candidate
  separator under y_after_59.

High UNKNOWN
  Keep y=60 reserved and externalize lower/upper class allocations; do not rerun
  the same high monolith.

Low INFEASIBLE
  Record the high allocation as one exact x42-low nogood and continue the
  allocation handshake.

Low UNKNOWN
  Preserve the exact high allocation and replay only that low complement with a
  solver-diverse instrument.

Truth boundary
--------------
A positive transfers to the unrestricted fixed-skeleton module-B problem. Any
negative is scoped to reserved y=60 and, if low-side, one exact class vector.
Terminal uniqueness, generic I/O, commodity binding, component compatibility,
routing, throughput, U/L, certification, owner, production and publication remain
outside.
