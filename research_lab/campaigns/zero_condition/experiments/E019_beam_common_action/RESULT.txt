What the beam differences do and do not mean

This separates two claims that had previously been entangled:

1. E017's equal-score states contain representational diversity.
2. That diversity necessarily changes every future action's value.

E019 proves the first but refutes the second for grinder pose 7754. Geometry or
binding identity alone is not a sufficient beam-retention rationale. A branch is
valuable only when a downstream action actually distinguishes its continuation.

Hidden methodological issue
---------------------------
Residual-boundary coverage selected grinder 7754 as the strongest unused literal.
It worsened the representative E017 triple in E018 and now worsens all twelve
seeds identically. Coverage remains useful for proposing actions, but it does not
measure compositional value.

The outer loop therefore needs two distinct ledgers:

  causal reach: which residual observations an action touches
  continuation value: what exact objective change the action produces from each state

Collapsing these into one ranking recreates the earlier error in a more refined
form: a broad explanation is not automatically a good construction move.

Truth boundary

states. It does not show that all actions are branch-invariant, that the seven
free-cell families are equivalent, or that a simultaneous multi-pose model is
unnecessary.
