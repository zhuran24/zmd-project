EXPERIMENT E020: branch value under a known composing action
============================================================

Question
--------
Does refinery_blue_iron pose 5343, which lowers the representative E017 triple
from 176 to 174, have the same exact continuation value across every eligible
objective-176 beam state?

Trigger
-------
E019 applied the residual-coverage leader grinder pose 7754 across all twelve
seeds. Every branch worsened identically to 178. Representational diversity alone
therefore did not establish beam value.

E020 tests the complementary case: an action already known to compose on one
branch.

Eligible parents
----------------
The twelve E017 seeds are reconstructed exactly. Four have already moved the
5343 group-pose literal and are ineligible. The remaining eight keep:

  mandatory::group::manufacturing_3x3::refinery_blue_iron::7::5343

at its E009 pose and receive the same complete fixed-outside alternative set.

Evaluation
----------
Every child preserves exact placement, no-overlap, and power semantics. The
ordered interface is:

  owner-domain filtering
  aggregate generic I/O and terminal uniqueness
  one shared component-mismatch optimization over all 19 commodities
  exact routing only when the shared objective reaches zero

Parents and children are deduplicated by placement plus binding-selection
identity. The beam remains width 12 and retains exact-score states while
preserving free-cell and binding-selection diversity.

Competing readings
------------------
A. Eligible seeds produce different best objectives. Equal-score parents have
   different continuation value, providing direct evidence that the outer loop
   must preserve multiple states.

B. Every eligible seed reaches the same objective 174. The known fourth move is
   action-invariant on this eligibility class; the beam may quotient these states
   for this action while retaining port-mode alternatives for other actions.

C. Some seeds cannot realize any base-feasible child. Eligibility at the literal
   level is not enough; neighboring branch geometry changes the action domain.

D. A child reaches zero. It immediately enters exact routing and supersedes beam
   model-selection work.

Truth boundary
--------------
The experiment is exhaustive only for one target literal on eight frozen
objective-176 seeds. A common objective response does not prove equal future
value for other actions, and a differential response does not establish global
beam completeness.

Result
------
Completed with verdict KNOWN_ACTION_ELIGIBILITY_CLASS_INVARIANT. All eight
eligible seeds improve 176 -> 174, but their exact alternative domains differ:
51-63 candidates and 9-23 base-feasible children. The best objective is one
action-equivalence class while the child-domain and objective-histogram surfaces
remain distinct. The next test applies crusher-source pose 6125 across four
leading objective-174 free-space classes. See RESULT.txt.
