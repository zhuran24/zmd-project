EXPERIMENT E105: NESTED LOWER/UPPER ALLOCATION HANDSHAKE
========================================================

Question
--------
Can E104's exact lower/upper product produce the first concrete high-side class
allocation without putting both geometries and all allocation coordinates back
into one solver?

Method
------
1. Pin and source-isolate E095, E100, E101 and the committed E104 constructor.
   Reconstruct exactly E104's 1,010 live survivors under fixed E092 poles,
   boundary_macro_09, x42 and manufacturing-free y60.

2. Solve body/power only for exactly 26 high bodies and template totals 10/6/10,
   preserving both stable E078 bodies. The selected geometry is a hint; the
   resulting lower/upper template totals are the binding proposal.

3. Let the nested side with fewer required bodies propose an eight-coordinate
   class allocation under its exact template totals. Let the other high side
   solve with its exact template totals and per-class caps equal to global B
   demand minus the proposer allocation. If that consumer is exact INFEASIBLE,
   forbid only the exact proposer vector and request another, for at most three
   vectors. UNKNOWN never creates a nogood.

4. For a paired high witness, sum the two nested allocations and send the exact
   complement to E101's unchanged x42-low model. A low positive is combined with
   both high witnesses and E095's frozen module-A half, then replayed over all
   219 manufacturing bodies.

Predeclared decisions
---------------------
Body-only INFEASIBLE
  Reject reserved y60 for the high 10/6/10 template totals and restore E103's
  explicit separator. UNKNOWN preserves censorship.

Nested proposer UNKNOWN
  Change only that side's solver or derive side-specific capacity bounds.

Nested consumer INFEASIBLE
  Forbid only the exact proposer allocation and continue, up to the frozen limit.
  If the limit closes without a pair, preserve the remaining open vectors.

Nested pair positive, x42-low INFEASIBLE
  Record the exact total-high allocation nogood. Do not forbid the proposer alone.

Any UNKNOWN downstream
  Preserve the exact body/template/allocation context and replay only that
  consumer; do not rerun the whole high model.

Full positive
  Advance the independently replayed 219-body native-front witness to terminal
  uniqueness, generic I/O and component-aware binding.

Truth boundary
--------------
Body-only geometry does not prove native fronts. A nested-side negative is scoped
to the exact template split and allocation bounds. An outer-low negative is scoped
to one total-high allocation. Only a complete replayed positive transfers to the
fixed-skeleton parent. No routing, throughput, certification or U/L effect.
