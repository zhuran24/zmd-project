EXPERIMENT E067: COMPLEMENTARY COMPOSITION OF NARROW PAIR FACES
================================================================

Question
--------
Can two of E066's twelve face-narrowing actions compose on the fixed E063 pole
parent to reach the corrected two-zero signature condition, or at least shrink the
complete residual face below five unmatched components?

Why this experiment
-------------------
E066 exhausts all 780 objective-one E063 pair states. None has a stable singleton
face. Exactly twelve states, drawn from two dense-blue-iron grinder bodies and two
remote power-pole bodies, reduce the complete face from six patterns/components to
five. They form six face classes and remove different source-only alternatives.

This supplies a bounded, causal third relation. E067 does not add an arbitrary
third object to 780 states. It composes only the twelve actions that have already
proved complete-face narrowing, yielding at most 66 unordered pairs before same-
object and physical-admission rejection.

Method
------
1. Pin E063 run-008, E066 result/atlas, the twelve selected candidate identities,
   and the source-stable E061/E062/E063/E066 runners.
2. Reconstruct the E063 pole parent and each selected action's exact single-action
   child. Extract an order-free mapping delta from parent to child, including
   optional-pole key replacement.
3. For each unordered action pair with distinct source objects, merge the two
   deltas simultaneously against the frozen parent. Reject conflicting writes,
   duplicate occupation targets, cardinality drift, overlap, and power loss. The
   current twelve-action set contains no target-key swap, so no broader swap
   semantics are claimed.
4. Independently validate mandatory/pole counts, no-overlap occupancy, and exact
   power coverage on the merged state.
5. Rebuild components, generic sink space, and all current 6x4 signatures. Require
   qiaoyu compatibility and minimize fine mismatch.
6. Replay every objective-zero state in the joint signature model and materialize
   a small deterministic set for full conditional-binding validation.
7. For objective-one states, enumerate the complete face and compare it with the
   five-component parents rather than trusting one representative.

Decision rule
-------------
At least one objective-zero composition
  Materialize it and immediately validate the full conditional port-mode,
  operation-assignment, and binding consumer.

No zero, but a complete face has one stable unmatched component
  Derive one final narrow relation from that stable component. Do not broaden the
  triple lineage outside the E067 states.

No singleton, but a complete face has fewer than five unmatched components
  Retain only the strict narrowest class and test one more relation under an
  explicit stop boundary.

No face below five
  Retire the pole-conditioned lineage and switch to E062's distinct 6x4 near-miss
  parent.

Any UNKNOWN
  Preserve the state and continue only the nonterminal candidates.

Truth boundary
--------------
E067 changes the fixed E055 first-zero state by the E063 pole action plus two
E066-selected actions and evaluates the corrected terminal-signature relaxation.
It does not establish full binding, routing, throughput, U/L, or certification.
