EXPERIMENT E119: LOCAL-FRONT CUT-FAMILY COVERAGE AND SATURATION AUDIT
====================================================================

Question
--------
Are E117/E118's 587 local-front containment cuts still discovering a narrow,
structurally new family worth targeted completion, or has the current Benders
language become broad but repetitive?

Why this experiment
-------------------
E118 triggers its preregistered no-improvement stop: eight new exact geometries
and 83 sound cuts do not lower the best empty-body count below E117's six. Cut
count alone is not progress. Before another solve, E119 measures what the store
actually covers and whether the second cohort adds new causal shapes.

Inputs
------

  E117: 39 witnessed geometries, 504 checked cuts
  E118:  8 witnessed geometries,  83 checked cuts
  merged cut store: 587 rules
  candidate universe: 1,205 E103-live x42-high rows

Static signatures
-----------------
Each cut receives two stable, component-free identities.

1. Exact local structural signature:

     subject template and E103 group
     subject same-footprint option/front stencil normalized to subject origin
     each blocker template and group
     each blocker body footprint translated relative to subject origin

   This preserves every local-front predicate used by the cut while quotienting
   absolute board translation.

2. Coarse family address:

     subject template
     subject E103 group
     subject five-row centre-y band
     core size
     blocker template/group multiset

Measurements
------------

  * universe, selected, subject and blocker candidate coverage;
  * coverage by template, E103 group and spatial band;
  * exact subject/blocker recurrence and structural-signature recurrence;
  * E118 novelty relative to E117;
  * per-iteration novelty progression;
  * retrospective cut hits on all 47 witnessed geometries;
  * leave-own-iteration hit rate inside each cohort;
  * candidates selected repeatedly but never appearing as a dead subject;
  * weakly covered families among candidates actually sampled.

Adaptive-data guard
-------------------
The 47 geometries were generated adaptively under earlier cuts. Therefore:

  * a geometry must have zero hits from cuts present before it was generated;
  * own/later-cut hits are in-sample explanation, not predictive accuracy;
  * universe coverage is descriptive, not a feasibility bound;
  * absence from the cut store never means a candidate is locally live.

Predeclared classification
--------------------------
NARROW_UNSATURATED_FAMILY
  E118 structural novelty ratio is at least 0.50; one coarse family carries at
  least half of E118's structurally novel cuts; and that family's pre-E118 subject
  coverage among repeatedly sampled candidates is below 0.50. Continue only that
  targeted family.

BROAD_REPETITIVE_PLATEAU
  E118 structural novelty ratio is at most 0.25; exact subject novelty is at most
  0.25; at least half of E118 geometries are hit by a cut from a different E118
  iteration; and no coarse family carries half of all E118 cuts. Change proposer
  representation rather than continue Benders.

INCONCLUSIVE_MIXED_SATURATION
  Every other outcome. Do not authorize a blind batch. Use the measured weakest
  family or a stronger representation in one bounded discriminator.

Truth boundary
--------------
This is a no-solver descriptive audit of sound cuts and adaptively generated
geometries. It proves neither saturation nor incompleteness of the master and has
no routing, certification or U/L effect.
