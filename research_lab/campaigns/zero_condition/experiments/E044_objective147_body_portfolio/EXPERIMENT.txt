======================================================

Question
--------
Which new occupied-body geometries are proposed by the fully materialized E043
objective-147 endpoint after known reverse and previously joint-valued geometries
are removed from the action ledger?

Method
------
E044 rebuilds all 147 mismatch-component observations and current boundary
literals. It performs a placement- and power-only mobility census, retains every
current literal with an actual body-relocation alternative, and solves one exact
budget-six maximum-coverage problem.

The selected six targets cover 70/147 residual observations. Their complete
fixed-outside pose domains are evaluated through static owner domains and the
ordinary exact shared-binding objective. Same-footprint alternatives are ignored
in this body portfolio.

Geometry states already carrying exact joint values are not treated as new:

  E041 original fixed-body geometry              joint value 152

  current E043 Seed A geometry                   parent value 147

The remaining candidates are ranked only as cheap geometry proposals. The two
best distinct free-cell geometries are fully materialized for E045, where the
joint port-mode/assignment/binding middle layer will be rebuilt and freed.

Decision rule
-------------
Objective 0
  Enter exact routing immediately.

At least one new base-feasible geometry

  conditioned joint-middle portfolio.

No new base-feasible geometry

Truth boundary
--------------
E044 exhausts fixed-outside alternatives only for one exact budget-six target
portfolio. Its ordinary shared-binding values are proposers, not final geometry
continuation values. The result does not prove component compatibility, reach
routing, establish a lower bound, or grant certified effect.
