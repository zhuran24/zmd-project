EXPERIMENT E117: HIGH-SIDE GEOMETRY-FIRST LOCAL-FRONT BENDERS
==============================================================

Question
--------
Can the source-stable x42-high language produce a 26-body geometry in which every
selected body has at least one locally viable same-footprint mode/class option
under the actual selected occupancy?

Why this experiment
-------------------
E114 shows that arbitrary body/power representatives contain 11..22 bodies with
empty local mode/class domains. E115/E116 show that fixing separator template or
class coordinates does not resolve the joint geometry/front search. The proposer
must therefore learn native-front death before global class assignment.

Master
------
Select exactly 26 bodies from E103's 1,205 unary-live x42-high candidates with:

  * exact global template totals 10/6/10;
  * body non-overlap;
  * fixed E092 power;
  * both stable E078 bodies;
  * separator template vector in E110's exact body/power basis, excluding the two
    exact E115 negatives 0/3/0 and 5/0/0.

The master contains no mode or class variables. E110's least-front-dead stored
geometry is a hint only.

Checker and cuts
----------------
For every selected body, enumerate every legal same-footprint pose mode and every
compatible B class. Under fixed solids plus the selected 26-body occupancy, a
body is locally live if any option has enough free input and output front cells.

For each empty body:

1. collect only selected bodies covering at least one front cell of one option;
2. delete blockers one at a time while the body remains empty;
3. retain the resulting inclusion-minimal blocker core;
4. add the sound monotone containment cut

     subject_body + sum(core_blockers) <= |core_blockers|.

The cut forbids every future geometry containing the subject and all blockers;
additional bodies cannot restore a blocked option. Front-cell multiplicity is
recomputed from occupied cells, not collapsed to Boolean adjacency.

Consumer
--------
The first geometry with all 26 bodies locally live is fixed in E101's unchanged
complete high-side native-front class model. A high positive goes immediately to
the x42-low complement and E095's 219-body replay. A high exact negative or
UNKNOWN is preserved on that promising geometry; the Benders master does not
silently discard it.

Decision rules
--------------
Paired 219-body replay PASS
  Advance to terminal uniqueness, generic I/O and component-aware binding.

Locally-live geometry, high consumer positive but low not paired
  Continue only its allocation handshake.

Locally-live geometry, high consumer exact negative or UNKNOWN
  Preserve the geometry and its class-domain/Hall diagnosis. Change the class
  consumer on that fixed constructive seed; do not resume abstract tuple scans.

Master exact INFEASIBLE after replayable blocker cuts
  Close the remaining 25-state x42-high language at the local-front necessary
  layer and retire x42 as a sufficient constructor.

Master UNKNOWN or iteration cap
  Preserve all cuts and remain censored.

Truth boundary
--------------
Local-front survival is necessary, not sufficient, for global class allocation.
Only independently replayed containment cuts may support a master negative. No
routing, throughput, certification or U/L effect follows.
