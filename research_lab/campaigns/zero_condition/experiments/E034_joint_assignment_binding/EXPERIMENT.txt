EXPERIMENT E034: JOINT OPERATION-ASSIGNMENT AND BINDING MODEL
============================================================

Question
--------
On the fixed occupied geometry shared by the two objective-160 E033 states, can a
single CP-SAT model choose both the operation assigned to each selected 3x3
footprint and that operation's exact pose-level binding pattern, thereby moving
the exact shared component-mismatch objective below 160 or to zero without
externally enumerating complete assignments?

Why this is the next object
---------------------------
E031 proves a five-footprint 3x3 assignment neighborhood can improve 163 to 161.
E033 then tests the strongest common and branch-specific external swaps on the
two tied states: the common action improves both only to 160 and the branch
actions worsen both to 163. Continuing to propose one complete assignment after
another would turn the outer loop into a manual assignment hill-climber.

The selected operations have no generic input or output slots. Their only moving
binding semantics are exact pose-level patterns on fixed footprints. This permits
a small faithful cohabiting model without rebuilding protocol-core or storage-box
slot semantics.

Model
-----
The full occupied cell set is fixed. All facilities outside the five E031 3x3
footprints use the existing PortBindingModel unchanged. For each selected
footprint f, operation o, and front-filtered exact pattern p, E034 creates a
conditional literal z[f,o,p].

  sum_p z[f,o,p] = y[f,o]
  sum_o y[f,o] = 1                         for every footprint f
  sum_f y[f,o] = frozen multiplicity(o)    for every operation o

The existing duplicate-terminal and shared component-mismatch compiler then sees
outside binding literals and the conditional joint literals in one model. Thus an
assignment cannot be selected independently of its binding pattern.

Calibration
-----------
Before freeing assignment variables, the same model is built twice with operation
assignment fixed to each E033 objective-160 state. Both fixed models must prove an
OPTIMAL objective of exactly 160. A calibration mismatch blocks interpretation of
the free solve.

Inputs
------
E031 neighborhood and exchangeability proof, E033 first and second common
assignments/endpoints, the frozen candidate pool, mandatory instances, canonical
rules, preprocess plan, and generic-I/O requirements are admitted by SHA-256 in
the runner.

Outcome interpretation
----------------------
OPTIMAL objective 0
  Materialize the concrete assignment and ordinary fixed-layout endpoint, then
  enter exact routing immediately.

OPTIMAL objective 1..159
  The cohabiting layer has constructive leverage that external action replay did
  not expose. Persist the complete joint witness and fixed-layout endpoint; use
  the resulting residual to decide whether the bounded footprint set should grow
  or whether geometry must move next.

OPTIMAL objective 160
  The five-footprint cohabiting neighborhood is exact and saturated. Do not return
  to external swaps. Expand the joint footprint context from residual causes or
  move to a broader assignment-binding representation.

FEASIBLE / UNKNOWN
  Record only the incumbent and bound. Do not claim saturation or optimality.

Truth boundary
--------------
This experiment fixes occupied geometry, power poles, and every operation outside
five 3x3 footprints. It is not a global operation-assignment optimum, a component-
compatible binding unless the objective is zero, an exact-routing result, a lower
bound, or a certification artifact.

Result
------
Completed. Both frozen objective-160 warm assignments recalibrate to OPTIMAL 160
inside the cohabiting model, and the free exact solve also proves OPTIMAL 160.
The five-footprint 3x3 context is therefore saturated rather than undersampled.
