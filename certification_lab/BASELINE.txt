The certification worktree intentionally does not duplicate the virtual
environment or large external candidate-placement artifact. Any review that
consumes external bytes must record and verify their identities. A path alone is
not a frozen input.

Baseline refresh rule
---------------------
The certification tree does not automatically merge history/main or research/main.
A baseline refresh is a separate certification commit and records:

  source tree and exact commit
  selected paths or commits
  named packet or certification reason
  semantic, authority, and source-pin consequences
  conflicts or assumptions introduced
  required tests, replay, and reseal consequences

Do not hide a baseline refresh inside a packet verdict or implementation commit.

Packet import rule
------------------
A research packet names exact research commits, diffs, and evidence hashes. The
certification review imports those frozen objects; it does not review whatever
bytes happen to be present at the mutable research worktree path later.
