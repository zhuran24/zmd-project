ZMD CERTIFICATION WORKTREE CHARTER
==================================

Status
------
This charter defines the role of the certification worktree. It does not replace
PROJECT_LOCK.md, canonical rules, machine authority, owner decisions, supervisor
seal, or the public publisher.

Mission
-------
The certification tree receives selected, frozen outputs from the research tree
and determines whether a precisely stated claim may be consumed with a precisely
stated effect under the current project context.

Its job is not to continue discovery until a candidate becomes attractive. Its
job is to make the candidate survive without the researcher's momentum,
narrative, or goodwill.

Repository roles
----------------
History/material tree
  /home/zhuran24/zmd-pj, branch main.
  Preserves the former combined tree, old Git history, tracked sources, and local
  evidence. Read-only from certification sessions.

Research tree
  /home/zhuran24/zmd-research, branch research/main.
  Produces conjectures, theorems, constructions, algorithms, experiments, and
  frozen promotion packets. Read-only from certification sessions.

Certification tree
  This worktree, branch certification/main.
  Performs independent intake, re-derivation, adversarial review, effect binding,
  certification-side implementation, and integration checks.

Core laws
---------
1. Packet before narrative.
   The review object is the frozen packet and its immutable source identities.
   Research history may explain where an idea came from, but it cannot fill a
   missing premise or grant authority.

2. A claim is frozen while reviewed.
   Changing the proposition, object space, premise set, runtime binding, context,
   or requested effect creates a new candidate. The old review cannot silently
   slide onto the new object.

3. Truth, execution, permission, and usefulness are separate.
   A theorem may be true while its lowering is wrong. A lowering may be sound
   while its consumer lacks permission. An admitted effect may be useless. No
   axis pays for another.

4. Transport is a proof obligation.
   A binding-level fact does not become a placement ban, a fixed-layout result
   does not become a cross-layout family, and a local infeasibility result does
   not become a frontier exclusion without an explicit transport argument.

5. Failure removes the new effect, not the old search domain.
   Missing evidence, stale context, checker failure, exception, timeout, or
   UNKNOWN causes NO_EFFECT / HOLD / RETURN, never a conservative candidate ban.

6. Certification is independent of authorship.
   The certification reviewer reconstructs the claim from locked inputs and
   attacks it with counterexamples, mutation canaries, alternate derivations, and
   the strongest relevant current semantics. Re-running the research harness is
   necessary evidence when applicable, not independence by itself.

7. Repair and approval are different acts.
   A reviewer may harden an implementation only when the frozen semantics and
   requested effect remain unchanged. Any semantic repair returns to research as
   a new packet. The same act cannot both widen the candidate and approve the
   widened object.

8. The branch name grants nothing.
   certification/main may contain review records and certification-ready code,
   but U/L updates, durable CERTIFIED minting, owner-only closure, and public
   publication remain governed by the existing project authority chain.

Admissible outcomes
-------------------
ADMITTED
  Packet identity and minimum contract are complete enough to review.

RETURN_TO_RESEARCH
  The candidate may be promising, but the frozen packet lacks a necessary
  premise, exact object, transport proof, reproducible input, or stable effect.

REJECTED_AS_CLAIMED
  A counterexample or proof failure refutes the packet's exact claim. This does
  not automatically refute neighboring, narrower, or repaired claims.

UNKNOWN
  The review could not decide within the available evidence or budget. No new
  effect is granted.

NO_EFFECT
  The theorem may remain true, but the requested consumer/effect is unsupported,
  stale, disallowed, or incorrectly lowered.

CERTIFICATION_CANDIDATE
  The frozen claim, concrete effect, current context, and applicable tests have
  passed the certification-tree review. This is eligibility for the next
  existing authority step, not durable certification itself.

Scope of work
-------------
The certification tree may:

  * import and freeze a research promotion packet;
  * independently recompute theorem, witness, certificate, or counterexample;
  * create adversarial tests and mutation canaries;
  * implement or harden a semantics-preserving consumer;
  * run targeted and full project gates appropriate to the requested effect;
  * issue a scoped verdict and prepare a certification candidate.

The certification tree must not:

  * browse research work and choose a candidate without an explicit packet;
  * treat research branch state, report prose, or test success as authority;
  * modify packet semantics in place to make a review pass;
  * update U/L or strong status merely from its own verdict;
  * bypass supervisor seal, owner decisions, or publisher checks;
  * write into the history or research worktree.

Completion criterion
--------------------
The certification worktree architecture is functioning when a fresh agent can
enter in certification mode, identify an active packet or idle state, reproduce
its immutable inputs, produce a scoped review without inheriting research
attention, and hand a passing candidate to the existing authority chain without
confusing that handoff with certification itself.
