## 研究问题与领域叫法

1. 游戏 AI／长期规划：已有工厂游戏代理怎样表达制造依赖、空间和长期生产目标？关键词：`Factorio learning environment`、`automation game planning`、`factory-building benchmark`、`production chain planning`、`long-horizon resource management`。
2. 运筹学／生产工程：制造配方、产线流量、机器和搬运系统怎样共同优化？关键词：`Factorio integer programming`、`production planning conveyor layout`、`factory layout optimization game`、`process synthesis material flow`、`automated factory design`。
3. 程序综合／形式化方法：能否从目标吞吐综合网络或从失败反馈修复构造？关键词：`conveyor belt balancer SAT`、`splitter network synthesis`、`constraint-based procedural content generation`、`program synthesis spatial construction`、`counterexample guided factory generation`。
4. 程序化内容生成／计算设计：现成工厂生成器的表示是否能替换逐格整体求解？关键词：`procedural factory generation`、`graph grammar factory layout`、`search-based procedural content generation`、`procedural content generation reinforcement learning`、`automated level design constraints`。
5. 网络算法／并行计算：分流器抽象能接到哪些可证明的平衡、流量与阻塞结论？关键词：`splitter networks`、`balancing networks`、`counting networks`、`rotor routing load balancing`、`round robin bounded buffer throughput`。
